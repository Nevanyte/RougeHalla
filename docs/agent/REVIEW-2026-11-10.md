## Decisions taken in the rewrite (owner may reverse)

1. **Model mix follows the owner's standing instruction** (Sonnet majority, Haiku simple, Opus for important Rust and
   merges) over the kit's Fable-heavy routing: oracles, `/plan` and high-stakes reviews run on Opus; Fable is the L3
   debugger only. To use Fable for oracles or reviews, change `model:` in `.claude/agents/oracle-author.md` or
   `reviewer-opus.md` and the table in `CLAUDE.md`.
2. **Default implementer is Sonnet**, not Haiku; Haiku keeps mechanical work. The kit's `reviewer` (Opus) became a Sonnet
   `reviewer` plus `reviewer-opus`, which replaces `reviewer-fable`.
3. The coordinator may merge reviewed `LAND` branches into the integration branch (`feature/unity-rework` today, as
   other sessions already do) without asking; promotion to `optimization_port` or `main`, pushes and deploys stay
   owner-gated.
4. A `PreToolUse` hook blocks hand edits to `packages/`, `protocol/fixtures/` and captured `parity/` JSON; the owner can
   lift it for a session with `RH_ALLOW_PROTECTED_EDIT=1`.
