# Holy and Frost passives

## Holy: Demon Blood

Holy gains 0.25 percentage points of lifesteal for every camp clear credited to them. A clear counts when the player damaged that
camp and is still alive and near it when its last mob dies. A normal mob kill by itself never grants this bonus. Training practice
camps count as clears for testing the passive. The bonus grows linearly without the regular card or farm lifesteal ceiling and still
receives lifesteal multipliers; the common total lifesteal cap applies to the other sources, not this earned camp progression.

## Frost: Frozen Justice

Frost gains 15 armor and 15 magic resistance while locked onto a live elite or after a landed hit or block involving a real elite.
The defense lasts for two seconds after the last contact. Normal mobs and training dummies do not trigger it; an elite used in a
training fixture does. The bonus uses the standard defense mirror and its shared bonus-defense ceiling.
