# P-MOBS-1: full regional mob runtime

## ID and outcome
P-MOBS-1. Complete authoritative regional camps in Rust: private roster roll, real non-seat fighters, raid-start spawning, source NPC combat and input-driven AI, territory leash/return, camp-wide aggro, full death/reward/stat effects and 180 s full-roster respawn. Catalog-only, synthetic seated players, neutral mobs without faction signatures, or recorded-but-unused stat stacks do not satisfy this outcome. Deliver four coherent stages below; stage handoffs never close the full packet. Chests/choices are P-CARDS-2; camp-clear heal/wards and elite chest consumers are P-MOBS-2; quests are P-QUEST-1. Keep their producer hooks explicit and pending. Design baseline: optimization_port `2e4aeaec`; verify the actual integration checkpoint before implementation.

## Dependencies
1. Accept P-MOBS-CATALOG-1 as the pure leaf; no runtime acceptance follows from it.
2. Before actor/spawn integration, accept the status seam and coordinator actor/provenance/capacity plan. Existing P-COMBAT-1/2 and P-COMBAT-3 authoritative work are foundations, not proof of generic NPC support. NPC `weaponTimings` and shared `policies.ts` `decide`/`lockSpacing` remain unported at the reviewed baseline and are stage 3 deliverables.
3. Consume existing full `rh-map/2` mapdata/mapgen layers (camps, spots, eliteSpot, territory, chest tile, factions). `crates/rh-sim/src/mapdata/fixtures/seed1.map.json` has 50 camps, 10 elites, 204 spots. These are fixture facts, never custom-map bounds. The old `parity/maps/seed1.map.json` and capture export are `rh-map/1` projections, not evidence that maps need implementation.
4. Full completion requires one authoritative stat modifier producer/readers for actual mob-stack effects and complete faction-on-mob behavior. Pure integrated stat_fold and base-player faction proofs do not supply these. P-CARDS-3 may build on the actor/combat stages before full camp-runtime acceptance; do not create a circular wait for both full packets. Serialize with P-CARDS-1/P-CARDS-3 and faction/status owners; explicitly unresolved producers block full acceptance. Do not duplicate their gameplay or silently substitute base values.

Coordinator (or one explicitly serialized integration owner) retains:

Every serialized stage declares its exact shared-file assignment before implementation. No new dependency is planned. ## Behaviour to preserve
1. **Catalog/roster leaf.** Use accepted 30-type/five-role definitions, lookups/fallbacks and source draw-injected rosters. Roll before joins, excluding training, using actual private four-word sfc32 with 16 warmups, not Mulberry32/public map seed/order_salt. Three independently shuffled rounds, nine draws per represented zone, first-seen zone order, camp order and elite no-draw/wrap rules. Respawn reuses published rosters. Complete source faction scaling must separately include signature params (execute k-squared, Mark shares), role/reach multipliers and region damage trim; today's eight-field faction sheet is insufficient.
2. **True actor/spawn foundation, after accepted status.** Real Mob with shared FighterCore and ordered Store; players enumerate first then mobs in spawn order. Roll at world construction, spawn at existing `raidStarted` hook; preserve type, facing, HP, home and source birth serial. Legacy nextId shares serials among mobs/containers/projectiles; phase is `(world.idCounter * 137) % 500` after allocation. Preserve source identity for tieRank instead of formatting every native mob as a player. Retain legitimate historical actor provenance after reaping so projectiles/statuses/deferred hits can outlive owners. `id < next_id` proves neither actor kind nor legitimate existence. Foundation acceptance may prove spawn/store/restore/observation and submitted movement; combat modes remain explicitly unimplemented until stage 3.
3. **Shared NPC combat and same-input AI.** Reuse existing timings, damage, history, status, targeting, melee/projectiles and faction readers. Port source `decide` and `lockSpacing`, not a new AI approximation. NPC windup is min(0.1, interval) for fists/sword/shield; axe min(0.25, 0.25*interval), bow min(0.6, 0.92*interval), wand min(0.4, 0.8*interval); preserve player profiles. Allied mobs deal zero friendly damage but can physically block/riposte allied melee; projectiles pass allies. Returning mobs remain hittable/targetable, damage adds threat without reaggro and fast healing continues; source Mark group membership separately excludes returning camp members.
   Queue AI after damage for next-tick application. Player shuffled inputs precede mob insertion-order inputs; movement remains players then mobs; projectile sweep precedes projectile birth. That projectile birth phase does not move raidStarted spawning. Preserve exact reap/respawn/think system ordering. Idle/fight/return transitions, hidden/untargetable/stunned/drain behavior, block wakes, lethal wakes and ranked after-pass competing camp wakes follow source. Give up after 20 s since **dealt** damage, no standing player, or leash departure; repath 500 ms with birth phase. Territory leash caps at 24 tiles with circle fallback, axis-margin retreat tests, home arrival 10 units; idle quiet 3 s, idle healing 4 s, return healing 2 s.
4. **Full death/rewards/respawn.** Whole-fight threat credit plus proportional lethal overkill attribution and source tieRank; all victims finish damage before payouts. Capture reward multiplier during damage while median/player feed is unchanged, then preserve feed/catch-up, GV rounding/cap and payout ordering. GV base normal/elite 6/30, shared mob cap 90, feed weight 0.25; source behavior wins. Same-pass downed killers keep GV/feed but receive no stat stack. Qualifying normal kills grant three actual per-type stacks affecting subsequent authoritative combat through the single stat owner, not a parallel or permanently neutral store.
   Any eligible kill can arm an empty hand. Loose weapons are one per cleared camp, chosen by earliest dying mob ID in the pass once final alive count is known; campless mobs can each drop. Settle every queued weapon award for one killer in mob-ID order at that killer's first payout; retain fists/same-held-type, training, departed/resolved/downed-killer rules. Camp states idle/engaged/cleared, partial-clear no respawn, 180000 ms after final death restores the whole published roster with fresh IDs/brains, ordinary corpse reap and teleport reset. Respawn clocks continue through locked gates; ended/training runs do not respawn. Preserve hooks for excluded chest/heal/ward/quest consumers as named pending domains.

## Acceptance checks
1. Each stage has captured unchanged-source inputs/outputs/provenance and an exact case inventory. Canonical Linux x64 Node 22.23.4 capture, closed schema/CLI, reject stale hashes, duplicate/missing/unknown cases and zero-work selection. Never use expected outputs as source inputs. Catalog acceptance is P-MOBS-CATALOG-1, not runtime parity.
2. Foundation: small admitted custom camp map plus existing full seed1 layer, explicitly new runtime fixture; compare roster-before-raid and spawn order/type/facing/HP/home/phase. Genuine non-seat movement through validation/input drain; remove/restore stores; hash every new state leaf and reject forged IDs/kinds, absent provenance and departed-player/mob confusion. Player capacity/admission/results stay independent. Native sfc32 state, draws and continuations match actual source and remain private.
3. Combat: all NPC weapons, both player-to-mob and mob-to-player signatures, camp Mark groups with original-group diminishing pulse shares and player-sized Mark HP pools. Allied shield/block/riposte with zero damage; projectiles pass allies. Return under melee/projectile damage; block/lethal/competing wakes; hidden/stunned/no-standing-target transitions; leash corners, fallback and phased repaths; exact next-tick lag. Base-player singleton signature evidence is insufficient.
4. Rewards/lifecycle: permute victim order; mix empty/armed/downed/resolved/departed killers, partial/full multi-kills, campless deaths, attribution overkill, capped GV and immediate farm-stat effect. Compare ordered hits/deaths/weapon awards, whole-fight credit, feed/catch-up clocks, GV and camp states. Drive at least the 180 s boundary through ordinary 20 Hz steps, with restore immediately before/at it; repeat death/reap/respawn with owner-outliving projectile/status/deferred sources. Training/ended/teleport cases required. No direct clock jump substitutes for ordinary-step evidence.
5. Real mob-enabled headless run/replay and checkpoint continuations; observation leaks for far/occluded mobs and hidden event sources, public targeting from delivered observations, no RNG/state leakage. Run existing content/hash/schedule classification and update only actually ported rows without reordering. Targeted mutations must fail for return immunity, blanket allied collision filtering, phase/tieRank drift, per-kill loose drops and payout multiplier timing.
6. Genuine 8-player/~200-mob workload (not seated scalebench): record hardware/revision/build, comparison to source, mean/p99 and allocations. Historical goal avg <5 ms/p99 <15 ms must be measured, not inferred. Warm ordinary AI/combat remains zero allocation; explicitly report initial-spawn/respawn allocation boundaries and steady capacity. Root runs full integration checks; pending producer or parity gaps prevent full success.
7. Stage handoff states `SUCCESS P-MOBS-1 stage <1-4>: <bounded verified scope>; full runtime pending` or `FAILURE P-MOBS-1 stage <1-4>: <specific blocker>`. Only after all four stages and every full acceptance check: `SUCCESS P-MOBS-1: full regional mob runtime source parity, headless and replay verified`. Otherwise final line is `FAILURE P-MOBS-1: <remaining gate>`. Report excluded consumers without claiming their completion.

## Risk and suggested model tier
High risk overall; serialize four stages with independent review at each shared-state boundary. Sol/medium suits the bounded catalog leaf; actor/provenance and full combat/reward integration warrant Astra/high design/review and appropriately strong implementation. Reuse existing FighterCore/Store/Cx/hooks; no new ECS, cache layer, parallel stat engine or framework.

## Known traps
Source code wins over stale return-immunity, per-kill-drop, 6 s give-up, 12-tile leash and eight-camps-per-zone prose. Full map implementation is already a dependency; old projections must not be repinned under this packet. A role/status leaf is not runtime recipient support. MAX_PLAYERS-sized scratch, signature lists, DoT and headroom assumptions require a deliberate actor audit. IDs must retain birth serial and historical kind/provenance; public ID translation alone cannot preserve phase or tieRank. Private RNG belongs in explicit secret/checkpoint handling, never observations. Events between ticks travel next step. Do not duplicate gameplay to bypass a pending stat/status/faction producer; record the blocking seam and continue the other authorized stage work.
