## 1. ID and outcome
Ground terrain is a `u8` plane (`GameMapData.terrain`, `crates/rh-sim/src/mapdata/model.rs:263`) with codes 0 to 7
(4 ember, 5 bog, 6 ice, 7 light; `crates/rh-sim/src/terrain.rs:56-72`). When this lands two more codes exist: **8 mud**
and **9 water**. Both only slow movement, the same way bog does (`TERRAIN_BOG`, `terrain.rs:60`, read through
`systems/terrain/mod.rs:37-51`), with the same Plague home exemption under `terrain::Mode::Map`. No generator emits
them yet (P-MAPGEN-SIG-PLAGUE-1 does), so every existing map, fixture and parity capture is unchanged.

Proposed numbers (owner signs the DEVIATIONS row): bog stays 0.7; `TERRAIN_WATER` 0.8; `TERRAIN_MUD` 0.9 (movement
multipliers: a 10, 20 and 30 percent slow from the outside of a marsh pool to its middle).

## 7. Behaviour to preserve
- Codes 0-7 behave exactly as today in every `Mode`; parity report all MATCH; no existing fixture or vector changes
  except the additions; a map without codes 8 or 9 encodes to the same bytes as before.
- The home exemption is by faction as for bog: a Plague actor is not slowed by mud or water under `Mode::Map`; under
  `Mode::All` everyone is; under `Off` no one is.

## 8. Acceptance checks
- rh-sim (one loose-proof test per rule): a non-Plague actor walking on mud, water and bog for N ticks covers 0.9, 0.8
  and 0.7 of the open-ground distance (within a tick's rounding); a Plague actor covers the full distance on all three
  under `Mode::Map`; the terrain FX/observation fact a client uses for the HUD chip reports the new kinds.
- Content: the content hash moves (new constants and the surface table loop `terrain.rs:122` covers 0..10); the
  hand-off states the new hash and that `crates/rh-sim/tests/content.rs` passes.
- Wire: golden vectors `map_terrain_mud_water` (accept) and a refusal vector for value 10; Rust, C# and JS decode them.
- Bots: a unit test that the hazard view classifies 8 and 9 as slow ground for non-Plague and as open for Plague.
- `scripts/gate.sh --quick --oracle packet/P-TERRAIN-MARSH-1` passes (the coordinator runs the full gate).

## 11. Known traps
- The Unity ground visual and HUD chip for mud and water are a separate packet (P-UNITY-MARSH-1); until it lands the
  client must not crash on codes 8 and 9 (C# decoder accepts them; unknown ground draws as the zone's floor).
- `Sim::check_layout` forbids `skip_serializing_if` in checkpointed state; the terrain plane's type does not change.
