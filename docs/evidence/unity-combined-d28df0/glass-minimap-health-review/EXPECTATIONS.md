# Offline UI layout capture expectations

Source snapshot: `d28df076bf98d10bfe6cb5fbc88b8d30a3fbe712`. The external `as-tested-source.json` manifest matches the current hashes for `MatchHud`, `MatchHud.Features`, `MythicUi`, `ModernSettingsView`, `TacticalMapController`, `UiLayoutReview`, `ShowcaseSettings`, and `UiInputRoutingEditModeTests`. This note defines the metadata to collect for the coordinator's 12 standard cases (720p/1440p × HUD/planning/map × text scale 1/1.5) and two additional 720p HUD cases at minimap scales .85/1.25. It does not run or add a test.

## Alpha and backdrop reference values

`MythicUi.Backdrop` preserves RGB and clamps alpha to `200/255 = 0.7843137`. This applies to `MythicUi.Panel`, buttons, and the `MatchHud.Panel` helper. For a standard `ShowcasePalette.Panel` fill, expect RGB `(17,24,35)` and alpha `200/255`; for button white backing, expect white RGB and the same alpha. Child TMP text and icons keep their own colors/alpha. A clear panel remains alpha 0: `TacticalMapController` uses this for the minimap shell, and clears the large `MapFrame` image after creating it. Those Images retain `raycastTarget=true` to receive pointer gestures, so transparent does not mean noninteractive. Capture both `color` and `raycastTarget` to avoid confusing a transparent input surface with an opaque panel.

The full-canvas `PlayerHUD` is a `MatchHud.Panel` and its background Image is a 200/255 backdrop. The TacticalMap panel and other active modal panels also use that clamped alpha. Where two such fills overlap, the expected combined coverage is about 95.3% (`1 - (1 - 200/255)^2`), leaving about 4.7% of the scene underlay. Record each panel image's color and active state; do not infer a single-layer underlay from the top panel's alpha alone. The map's `MapFrame` is explicitly cleared, and the map surface draws above it.

The common outline on `MatchHud.Panel` has color `(126,96,51,220)`, distance `(1,-1)`, and `useGraphicAlpha=false`. Button outlines use `ShowcasePalette.GoldDim`, distance `(2,-2)`, `useGraphicAlpha=false`. These outlines may show even when a graphic's fill is translucent, so capture their actual state separately if present.

## HUD frame ornaments

`MatchHud.Panel` adds eight `Image` children named `OrnamentTLH`, `OrnamentTLV`, `OrnamentTRH`, `OrnamentTRV`, `OrnamentBLH`, `OrnamentBLV`, `OrnamentBRH`, and `OrnamentBRV` only on panels named `JoinMenu`, `PlayerHUD`, `Rewards`, `Results`, and `SettingsPanel`. Each ornament expects color `(156,119,62,225)`, `raycastTarget=false`, and anchors at the outer panel corners with length `.026` and thickness `.0025` (fractions of panel width/height). In the offline layouts, inspect active `PlayerHUD` and, in HUD mode when shown, `Rewards`; check all visible ornament rects stay inside the canvas and do not intersect controls or TMP glyph bounds. Their thin strokes scale to only a few pixels at 720p, so verify they remain visible and do not collapse at native resolution.

`ModernSettingsView` uses `MythicUi.Panel` with object name `ModernSettings`, not the `MatchHud.Panel` helper's `SettingsPanel` name. No corner ornaments are created there by these sources. It is hidden in the stated offline fixture; do not count its hidden descendants as visible corner defects or claim that the 12 cases validate the settings screen's ornament treatment.

## Offline review and layout state

`UiLayoutReview` uses an explicit in-process `LayoutTransport`; it labels the scene “STAGED UI REVIEW / NO NETWORK.” Its public `IsReady`, `Hud`, and `SetTextScale` are sufficient to identify the target root and verify scale. For each case record the requested mode/text scale, `Screen.width/height`, `MatchUI` Canvas `pixelRect`, CanvasScaler `scaleFactor`, camera size, screenshot dimensions, and frame/tick advancement. Capture `Graphic.color`, `raycastTarget`, `isActiveAndEnabled`, and `CanvasRenderer.cull`; for TMP capture text, font size/min/max, wrapping, `isTextOverflowing`, overflow index, and rect bounds; for each relevant `Selectable`, record `interactable`, `IsInteractable()`, target graphic, and screen rect. Use `RectTransform.GetWorldCorners` / `RectTransformUtility.WorldToScreenPoint` for pixel bounds; no private reflection is needed.

The HUD builds a full-screen `PlayerHUD` parent and separately hides its VitalsPanel, BuildStrip, and CombatActions when `WorldHudVisible` becomes false. A hidden child can therefore have `activeSelf=false` while its parent remains active. For each layout mode record both `activeSelf` and `activeInHierarchy` for those roots and for `JoinMenu`, `SignInModal`, `ModernSettings`, `ControlsHelp`, `BuildDrawer`, `Results`, `Rewards`, `TacticalMap`, and `Minimap`. Also confirm `Graphic.isActiveAndEnabled=false` under hidden roots and no hidden descendant appears in center-point raycast results. Do not mistake an active translucent `PlayerHUD` parent for an accidentally visible hidden child.

Expected baseline mode behavior is: `TacticalMap.IsOpen` false in HUD mode, true in map and planning modes; `Minimap` visible only when the map is closed and the in-process session is active; `Results`, join/sign-in, help, settings, and build drawer hidden. Rewards may be shown in HUD mode because the fixture publishes one synthetic chest; map/planning suppress world HUD visibility and the reward overlay. Treat these as fixture expectations and record the observed active state rather than changing the offline mode.

## Corner, control, and underlay checks

For each visible button, check the Image/Selectable center is screen-bounded and that the center raycast resolves to that control or its intended child text/icon. Decorative ornament images and text should not be the top raycast target. Transparent interactive map surfaces and viewports may intentionally raycast; check their designated behavior separately. Compare control bounds for paired text-scale captures: text scaling should change TMP sizes/layout only where text autosizing requires it, not shift fixed HUD panel anchors or corner ornament anchors. Record text clipping and line counts rather than assuming a successful draw from `IsReady`.

At 720p HUD minimap scale endpoints, `TacticalMapController` clamps scale to `.85–1.25`, with root anchors `xMin=.985-.28*s`, `xMax=.985`, `yMin=.018`, `yMax=.018+.36*s`. At `.85`, expect anchors `[.747,.018]` to `[.985,.324]`; at `1.25`, `[.635,.018]` to `[.985,.468]`. The HUD moves the reward panel's `yMin` to at least `.018 + .36*s + .015`, giving a nominal normalized vertical gap of `.015` above the minimap. Record both rects in screen pixels, check they do not overlap after canvas scaling, and ensure their child text/buttons remain within their respective rects. At max scale that gap is only about 11 px on a 720-high canvas, so inspect actual text and ornament margins.

Do not force alpha-zero for hidden roots as a substitute for inactive state: `SetActive(false)` and `activeInHierarchy=false` are the expected hidden-underlay behavior. Do not change the backdrop alpha to judge it; report the serialized/runtime Graphic state and the native screenshot together.
