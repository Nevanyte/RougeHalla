## Decision boundary

R-ML-4 and frozen C-OBS require legitimate player-perceivable facts and forbid leaks through hidden updates, IDs, counts, order and RNG. Raw legacy publication can be broader than human presentation and is not automatically a legitimate policy input. Under R-GAME-6 an unresolved intended-behavior change needs a reviewed decision; this packet does not infer a product conflict from an unsupported declared scene or silently correct the diagnostic heuristic.

Root must review the supported source boundary before any transport or native design proceeds. Remaining gates are actual World/Room-consistent NPC lifetime scenes, supported hidden-caster publication histories, complete history/branch/deduplication/expiry equivalence for any proposed rebase, positive lifecycle/immunity prerequisites where required, and producer population/rate/lifetime bounds. No `4096` protocol cap, split budget or proposed 326,110-byte envelope is admitted. This packet stops at the verified source-evidence boundary; the independent protocol 14 integration and browser gates remain separate.
