# Art preparation and runtime coverage

This is an asset/presentation inventory, not a gameplay parity or release-readiness claim. “Prepared” means source bytes, metadata, or Unity assets are present. “Live” means the server-fed arena path currently consumes a fact or catalog entry. A staged preview is explicitly not evidence of live game coverage.

## Specific remaining gaps

- **Mob specificity:** replace or deliberately approve coarse enum-parity goblin/skeleton reuse. The selected family does not supply literal rat/imp sprites, and a compatible free supplement has not been found. Verify role/elite/boss silhouette needs against the canonical [presentation inventory](../unity/PRESENTATION_CONTRACT.md); do not imply every mob has unique art.
- **Hero and faction range:** both live hero atlases already composite the selected six armor/head layers. Five faction insignia/colors share these two body designs. Bespoke silhouettes, runtime equipment customization and additional cosmetic variants remain future art scope.
- **Action/weapon cues:** heroes, goblin and skeleton bind all eight selected actions with source frame-count/duration rates; the knight is stance-only. A stagger currently reuses hit artwork, and unknown or unavailable cues retain conservative fallbacks. Schematic procedural weapon glyphs are a baseline cue, not authored weapon art or proof that each action reads distinctly.
- **Existing authoritative server data:** use current delivered map, actor, camp, prop, collection, event/Fx, arena and result fields where available. The Unity contract already lists exact missing/refused fields and mechanics; key art-facing gaps include richer map/zone/terrain features, complete mob role/elite identity, stable projectile lifecycle, full Wish/cast facts, many signature/status mirrors, quest/trade/loot details, and complete results. Do not invent these from hidden state, local inference, or the staged preview.
- **Live scene dressing and responsiveness:** inspect the final Web build at gameplay scale, including camera framing, overlap/sorting, readability at window sizes and accessible settings. Imported assets and a local 2D scene do not establish final browser acceptance.
- **Performance gate:** browser proof and profiling remain in progress. Current physical display is 1920×1080 at 60 Hz; the requested 2560×1440 / 144 FPS target budget has not been measured or met. Do not report final browser or performance acceptance until the exact final build is tested under that target.
