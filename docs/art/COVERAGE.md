# Art preparation and runtime coverage

Historical matrix: the repair-era rows below retain their original revision scope and contain superseded Wish/barrier availability notes. Use the [current original-game inventory](inventory/README.md) and its [125-entry checklist](inventory/asset-checklist.json) for the updated art audit. Accepted protocol13 all-five-Wish/Trophy browser evidence is separate from asset completeness.

This is an asset/presentation inventory, not a gameplay parity or release-readiness claim. “Prepared” means source bytes, metadata, or Unity assets are present. “Live” means the server-fed arena path currently consumes a fact or catalog entry. A staged preview is explicitly not evidence of live game coverage. Creative reuse of the current art family is permitted; bespoke species, hero or weapon artwork is not a prerequisite. Delivered identities and essential cues still need readable presentation and selected-demo evidence.

The server-fed coverage below describes the served `4f37e9f0` runtime. Later prop/signature and animation-selector source drafts compiled and passed 42 Unity Editor tests, but remain unbuilt and browser-unverified; see [source-cue evidence](evidence/source-cues/summary.json) and [WORK_LOG.md](WORK_LOG.md). These tests do not establish visual readability, performance, or live server-fed coverage.

## Specific remaining gaps

- **Mob specificity:** selected-demo mob/content coverage remains required. Creative reuse of goblin/skeleton art does not require literal rat/imp replacements. Verify delivered role/elite/boss distinctions against the canonical [presentation inventory](../unity/PRESENTATION_CONTRACT.md) and keep unavailable facts separate; do not imply every mob has unique art or infer coverage from camp rows alone.
- **Hero and faction range:** both live hero atlases already composite the selected six armor/head layers. Five faction insignia/colors share these two body designs, but the selected demo still needs all five factions exercised. Non-hue identification is open after rendered review (see [COLOR_REVIEW.md](COLOR_REVIEW.md)). Bespoke silhouettes and runtime customization beyond the selected demo remain future art scope.
- **Normal-map path:** the repair source fixes the showcase configuration gap. The no-override staged-light A/B demonstrates a pixel response, with art-quality acceptance still open; see [NORMAL_MAPS.md](NORMAL_MAPS.md). The combined build passed 19 browser outcomes and three fixed-High GPU diagnostic windows; overall performance acceptance remains open.
- **Action/weapon cues:** heroes, goblin and skeleton bind all eight selected actions with source frame-count/duration rates; the knight is stance-only. A stagger currently reuses hit artwork, and unknown or unavailable cues retain conservative fallbacks. Schematic procedural weapon glyphs are a baseline cue, not authored weapon art or proof that each action reads distinctly.
- **Existing authoritative server data:** use current delivered map, actor, camp, prop, collection, event/Fx, arena and result fields where available. The Unity contract already lists exact missing/refused fields and mechanics; key art-facing gaps include richer map/zone/terrain features, complete mob role/elite identity, stable projectile lifecycle, full Wish/cast facts, many signature/status mirrors, quest/trade/loot details, and complete results. Do not invent these from hidden state, local inference, or the staged preview.
- **Selected-demo content coverage:** the final match and profile reports are partial retained samples. [DEMO_CONTENT.md](DEMO_CONTENT.md) lists their observed lower bounds; missing status, ability, projectile, event or other rows are unknown from these reports and do not prove non-occurrence. Exercise the required five factions and selected-demo content in live server-fed scenes. Runtime customization beyond that selected demo can remain outside this milestone.
- **Live scene dressing and responsiveness:** the repair source at runtime `4f37e9f0` has a 19/19 browser proof and 27 actual Unity EditMode passes; build identity and content limits are in [REPAIR_VERIFICATION.md](REPAIR_VERIFICATION.md). All five factions, selected-demo content, painterly human assets, and non-hue identification remain open coverage items. The repaired static CVD samples were reviewed with limitations; this is not an accessibility pass.
- **Performance gate:** normal/eight-seat/uncapped diagnostic captures and nine profiles belong to the separately preserved older `ebff286b` artifact. The repair Web build is 74,041,096 bytes. Three repair crowded 15-second windows completed within the diagnostic 6.944 ms hold threshold; this is not native displayed 144 Hz acceptance and is not a cost-improvement comparison to the older different-load profiles. Managed allocation and total browser GPU memory remain open. The physical display is 1920×1080 at 60 Hz, so native displayed 144 Hz acceptance remains unverified. See [PERFORMANCE.md](PERFORMANCE.md) and [REPAIR_VERIFICATION.md](REPAIR_VERIFICATION.md).
