## Scope and decoding notes

- The final web run represents one match. Standard, crowded, and uncapped profile reports are separate; do not merge them into a fictional match.
- Camp `mobType` values are direct zero-based IDs. Actor `mobTypeCode` is a one-based wire code; `SnapshotBuffer` subtracts one to form `MobTypeId`. They are separate evidence types. A camp entry is not proof that a mob was rendered.
- Actor/NPC IDs are not treated as mob-kind evidence or persistent identity.
- Five-faction preparation is separate from live proof: the final-match retained facts establish Fire and Holy only.
- Profile rows and match events are partial retained samples. All lists are lower bounds; a missing event, status, effect, ability, or projectile fact means missing evidence, not proof of non-occurrence.
- Code-name and formatter sources are `RoguehallaNet/Net/Views.cs`, `Net/Collections.cs`, `Protocol/State.cs`, `crates/rh-sim/src/potions/mod.rs`, `UnityProject/Assets/Scripts/UI/MatchHud.cs`, and `UI/PotionNoticeFormatter.cs`; their SHA-256 hashes are emitted as `enumSourceSha256` by the script. The four raw evidence report SHA-256 values are emitted under `sourceReports.sha256`.
