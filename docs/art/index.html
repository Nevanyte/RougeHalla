Composition rules for the demo

LayerDirectionUse in the match
GroundCharcoal earth, old stone, narrow warm highlightsKeep the floor darker and lower-contrast than players, mob outlines and pickup indicators.
PlayersDifferent silhouettes, armor layers and faction emblemsUse faction hues as trim and badge colour. Keep names, HP and active casts unmistakable.
Combat effectsFewer, clearer strokes; short ember, frost and violet burstsKeep every effect attached to a server-delivered event or state. Respect reduced-motion and actor visibility rules.
EnvironmentDungeon stone as the first polished stageKeep authoritative footprint edges visible; use purely visual dressing outside the collision truth layer.
UI and audioOriginal Roguehalla typography, panels and score; selected Flare music and effectsMusic, ambience and SFX are a small human-created, free Flare sample with per-file credits and licenses documented separately. UI remains original.
Free-art coverage gap. The selected family supplies rich characters and a dungeon environment, but does not provide a complete custom Roguehalla UI or all five faction-specific hero designs. Bridge those gaps with the existing procedural geometry, original faction marks, palette, lighting and bespoke UI. Review any broader paid art brief only if free-source coverage still proves inadequate; no price or budget is proposed here.

Selected source set: Flare game artwork

Downloaded directly from the official flareteam/flare-game repository at commit af6eee6d339ac98011864bfe89da837fe7769c28. The repository’s official credits state that Flare game art is CC BY-SA 3.0, with later versions permitted, and list specific contributor/source exceptions. The source license, credits and animation/tile metadata are retained under Assets/Art/ThirdParty/Flare. No image was recoloured, cropped or otherwise changed for this board.

Adapted sprites must keep artist/source credit, identify changes, and remain available under the applicable share-alike terms. The share-alike applies to adapted art files; it does not extend to unrelated game code. Check THIRD_PARTY_ASSETS.md before redistributing or changing the art files.

The Unity Asset Store forest sample from the earlier search was omitted from this selected family: its standard Asset Store EULA is distinct from open-source art, and the page does not establish a complete human-authored five-faction set. This board shows the actual imported source family, not a promise that every ready-made image is a final in-game choice.
