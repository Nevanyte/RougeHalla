# R2 map contract, for the quests work (Kimi) and anything else placing things on the map

Source: `claude/r2-zones` (R2 line; verified against packages/shared/src/map.ts, mapgen.ts and docs/R2_GENERATOR_AS_BUILT.md on that branch). The field NAMES are the R0 contract; what changed in R2 is their MEANING and SIZE.

## What changed in meaning
- **Map size:** 96 x 96 tiles (`MAP_TILES_W/H`), `WORLD_W/H` = 3072 u (was 64 x 64 / 2048 u). The coliseum stays FIXED at the centre of the map; read `map.coliseum.centre` / `map.coliseum.rect`, never hard-code coordinates. The footprint, 4 gates (n, e, s, w) and pillar layouts are the same design placed at the new centre.
- **Zones:** the five faction zones are organic, near-equal-area regions (paint-bucket growth), not wedges; there are no ridges or passes between zones and no walls that follow zone borders. `regionGrid` still says which zone a floor tile belongs to (`regionAt(map, x, y)`); `map.regions[]` is still one entry per faction in REGION_FACTIONS order.
- **`camps` (CampDef[])**: 4 camps per zone = 20 per map, in REGION_FACTIONS order then camp index; ids "fire-1".."fire-4". Exactly ONE per zone is the elite camp (`eliteSpot >= 0`, spot 0 = the spot nearest the centre); the others have `eliteSpot` -1. `x, y` is the camp centre tile centre and `chest` is the same tile (the clearing's centre tile; no mob spot uses it). `spots`: 3-5 mob rest positions. `r`: clearing radius in world units. `area`: floor tiles of the camp's TERRITORY (the part of the zone nearer to this camp by path cost: the mob leash area; territories partition each zone). `radius`: farthest territory floor tile from the camp centre in world units (<= 30 tiles); the mob leash is min(radius, 12 tiles) from the home spot. Camp centres are >= 9 tiles apart map-wide. `map.territoryGrid[i]` = 1 + camp index (0 outside any territory). The R0 wedge-adapter values (`area` 0, all-zero `territoryGrid`, `radius` = 9 tiles) no longer apply on generated maps; they still apply to hand-built maps (`mapFromTiles`).
- **`spawnPool`**: replaces the three fixed spawns per region. 24-32 members `{x, y, zone}` across all five zones, all within +/-8% of one walking distance to the coliseum centre; a player is DEALT two of them at planning start (different zones, same tier). `map.spawns` and `regions[].spawns` are VIEWS of the pool. Pool members are screened for sight lines and kept 5 tiles from camp centres: a poor source of 'interesting' places for quest givers.
- **`regions[].pole`** (R0: = anchor): now a floor tile centre outside ruins farthest (5/7 chamfer) from every other zone, the border and the coliseum: the zone's 'heartland', used for its label. `regions[].anchor` is GONE; `regions[].area` is the zone's painted tile count (the five are equal within 8). `regions[].chestSpots` are the zone's weapon-crate spots (3-4 per zone); `map.crateSpots`/`map.groundSpots` still feed weapon loot. `regions[].mobCamps` is a view of `camps`.
- **Gone:** `map.extracts`, `ExtractZoneDef`, `EXTRACT_RADIUS` (no extraction), ridges and passes, the wedge sector code.
- **Ruins:** optional (0-2 per zone; `map.buildings`); there is no 'one indoor chest per region' rule any more.
- **New on GameMap:** `attempt` (the certified attempt: clients and bots must run exactly `generateMap(seed, attempt)`), `terrain` + `props` (zone terrain's hazard layer and cosmetic props; `mapHash` covers terrain but NOT props), and `TILE_LOW` (2) tiles that block movement but not sight (fences, lava) once zone themes are on. Use `blocksTile(map, tx, ty, mask)` / `isSolidTile` / `moveMask` / `sightMask` from shared, not `isWallTile` alone, for anything that asks 'can I stand or see here'.
- **How maps arrive:** the server takes a certified map from a pool (a worker thread) and publishes `RaidState.seed`, `attempt`, `mapHash`; clients build the same map in a Web Worker. Place quest givers deterministically FROM the map (or from `world.rewardRandom` on the server), never inside map generation (the map is public and certified: a quest layer must not change any hashed layer).

## Placing things safely
- Camps and their chests are protected ground (clearing + halo kept off rocks; floor hazards are kept off clearings and chest tiles): put a quest giver on a free floor tile 3+ tiles from a clearing, or at a zone `pole`, or inside a ruin (`map.buildings`).
- Validate any chosen tile with `circleClear(map, p, PLAYER_RADIUS)` on the MOVE mask and check reachability from the coliseum centre with `findPath` (every spot, chest, crate and pool member is reachable on certified maps; an arbitrary tile may not be).
- `coliseum.innerSpawns` and the coliseum footprint are off limits; with the gates locked the coliseum is sealed.
- Load: 20 camps and about 80 mobs per map (was 11 and 39).
