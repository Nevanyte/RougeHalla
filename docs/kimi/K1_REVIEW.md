# Review of K1 (592f2a2) and K3 (4e96e9d)

Verifier's call: **merge after fixes**. All 12 findings hold up, and nothing is a blocker: no crash, no lost data, the gates pass, and honest players are not harmed. But K1 misses four contracts that the plan made conditions of K1 being done and that K2 assumes are in place. First, quests draw from rewardRandom; a private stream was required (#1, #5). Second, hidden givers can be brute-forced from public data in about 1 s (#2, #4), which breaks the hidden-quest premise the K1.4 work was meant to secure. Third, there is no closure at the teleport, at run end, on a camp clear or after completion (#3, #7); a test pins the opposite, and hidden quests reveal behind the locked gates. Fourth, rewards skip the catch-up and reveal ties go by join order (#6, #8): each is a one-line fix plus a test. In K3, the ratio is defined inversely to the plan (#10) and the farm-only baseline dies or stalls in most runs (#11), so none of questBench's quest/farm numbers can be used for K2 item 14 until both are fixed; the harness is report-only, so this does not affect the game. Each fix is local to sim/quests.ts, shared/quests.ts or harness/questBench*.ts.

### #2 [major] (sim) verifier: confirmed / major: Hidden giver positions can be recomputed from public data (map seed plus the five known givers)
- Where: `packages/shared/src/quests.ts`:325
- Failure: A modified client or a bot reads the five known giver positions from state.quests at planning start, runs generateMap(seed) and the exported npcCandidates/spreadSpots, and recovers the positions of all five unknown givers in about one second. The scouting premise (explore to find hidden quests) and the K1.4 requirement that unknown quests stay hidden until revealed are voided for any non-honest client, even though the synced state itself holds nothing about them.
- Evidence: spreadSpots takes ONE secret draw (the first pick) and every later pick is a deterministic farthest-point greedy over public candidates (npcCandidates is a function of the map only). placeQuests calls it at line 445. leakpos.mts brute-forces the first pick, keeps the picks whose set contains the 5 public known positions, and recovers all hidden positions exactly: seeds 1-6, 1 consistent first pick each, 0.8-1.2 s each. - Suggested fix: Make every giver pick depend on the secret stream: for example rejection-sample random candidates with the minimum gap instead of farthest-point greedy, or add secret jitter and choose among the top few per step. Add a test that the known positions do not determine the hidden set (for example two secret streams with equal known positions give different hidden positions).
- Verifier: shared/src/quests.ts:325-346: spreadSpots makes one random draw per gap pass (the first pick). Every later pick is a deterministic max-min greedy over npcCandidates(map), which depends only on the public map. placeQuests (line 445) uses the spots unchanged.
