Three reviewers (correctness, design and balance, integration onto main) and a verifier read your branch. Below: what to keep, every serious finding with the verifier's verdict, and the minor findings. Fix the confirmed ones as part of K1/K2 (see KIMI_WORK_PLAN.md).

## Reviewer: correctness

The quest sim core is well built. It follows the project rules that matter most: rewards are paid in the systems phase after the ledger, all rolls use the private stream, there is a clean off switch, and no wire tables changed. All 16 tests in packages/server/test/quests.test.ts pass, and the worktree is still clean. But it should not merge as written, for four reasons. (1) The five "unknown" quests are not hidden, because everything about them is in the broadcast state. (2) The giver is a full MobState body, so it absorbs arrows and wand balls and steals Sentenced's signature target. (3) Collect tokens have no carry cap, so one player can take every token, and quest GV is uncapped and ungated, against the owner's GV rulings. (4) Nothing consumes the feature on the client or in the bots; neither can pick up a token, and they never see a quest. It also does not compile or merge on current main 8e78689: a read-only merge-tree shows about 8 textual conflict hunks in 6 source files, plus semantic breaks (grantStacks, BuffStat and the stacks fields are gone; buffs now go through grantBuff with mob-type BuffIds). ### Worth keeping
- Reward timing follows the project's tick rules. questSystem is a systems entry (quests.ts:240, :291), so it runs after applyLedger and before settle. Completion needs a player who is `standing` and not down, so a quest can never decide who survives a tick. Progress hooks run from payLater payouts and only bump counters, and all payment happens in the one turn-in place.
- The turn-in race is order independent: one best candidate per quest per tick, picked by tieRank (quests.ts:262-274), and expiry is processed before turn-in in the same tick so a lapse tick cannot be turned in. The single status flip to "done" makes a second finisher in the same tick impossible.
- The integration surface is small and mostly clean. It adds one hook call in payMobKill (mobLife.ts:175, after the spoils and inside payLater), chains playerKilled and dropBody and raidStarted properly, and avoids a loot.ts to quests.ts import cycle by calling `world.quests.onQuestPickup` (loot.ts:93-99 and quests.ts:52-53). The giver is brainless, so mobAI, reapMobs, hurtMob and killMob all skip it and it cannot die (killMob and hurtMob return on no brain; damageMob only handles dummies).
- The off switch and training isolation are a single guard: `install` returns early when `!config.quests || config.training` (quests.ts:278), leaving `world.quests` null and every other site null-checked. Verified by the tests at quests.test.ts:120-127. QUESTS=0 and QUESTS=false parse like MOBS (config.ts:45-47).
- The carrier lifecycle is sound. The parcel drops where the carrier goes down through the dropBody hook, which covers the MIA and leave paths because those go through downPlayer. It reuses loot.ts's born-this-tick guard, so no one can scoop it the tick it appears. Both `s.carrier` and `q.carried` are cleared consistently on pickup, drop, completion and expiry. Quest items never touch the weapon slots.
- Determinism holds: no Math.random or Date.now in shared or server quest code, and all rolls come from the private reward stream. world.random is untouched (verified: identical next draw with quests on and off), and placement is identical on re-run across 60 seeds, with all 10 quests placed and every giver reachable from every spawn.
- It adds no wire changes: no new fx kinds, no new client messages, only state fields, a private toast and a `quest` bucket in GvBreakdown that is plumbed through unbankedGv, resolve and results.ts. Existing-test edits are mechanical `quest: 0` additions.
- The catalog, parity formula and roster invariants (20 defs, at least 2 of each kind per roster, 5 known, per-stat coverage) are data-driven, so adding a quest is adding a def. The runtime tests exercise the real killMob to payLater to mobKilled chain, the real step and systems loop, and the real handlePickup range and line-of-sight rules.

SPECIFIC QUESTIONS
- Travel credited at 0.4 of farm rate: not sound. It is an unmeasured constant. The doc's travel inputs are 3x too high (fetch 100 s against about 30 s measured). Farm yield is not constant: with no respawn it falls to about 0 after minute 2-3 (QUESTS_PLAN), so the parity has to be time-dependent. The formula also credits objSec at the full rate for kill quests, whose kills already pay their own spoils. This double-counts.
- Timers from far and near spawns: absolute from run start (offerQuest, quests.ts:85), identical for all. Completion time from the best, median and worst spawn is about 18/33/44 s for fetch, 24/40/57 for collect and 41/53/65 for cull (R2 port). Every spawn can finish inside half of every window, so the clock never excludes anyone and never binds. The real clock is the first rival. If 8 players all rush one quest, the winner's lead over the runner-up has a median of 1.0-2.2 s, and 63-91% of races are decided within 3 s. That is a real contest point, but only for quests two players choose at the same time.
- First-come standing offers plus known flipping for everyone: simple, with no accept message and no client message. But the scout is unrewarded and unnamed: toast to the scout only, no head start, no announcement. Exploring is a public good, so nobody has a reason to do it. Late discovery on absolute clocks (cull 150 s, collect 180 s) leaves too little time. Near spawns the unknown quests are already known on tick 1.
- Quest GV farmable: yes, with no throwaway callsign needed (see F7).
