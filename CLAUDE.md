# CLAUDE.md — RougeHalla (package name `raid-br`)

Top-down 2.5D roguelike battle arena. Up to 8 players pick a faction, loot, farm mobs and fight. The last one standing wins Glorious Victory (GV, cosmetics-only). Phaser 4 client, Colyseus 0.18 authoritative server, npm workspaces monorepo. Colored shapes only. Design and numbers: `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md`. Running and playing: `README.md`. Faction ids are `fire`, `sentenced`, `plague`, `frost`, `holy` (shown to players as Demon Blood). Weapon ids are `sword`, `bow`, `wand`, `shield`, `axe`. The roguelike overhaul (zones, typed camps, contested chests, skills, relics) is planned in `docs/ROGUELIKE_PLAN.md`; built so far: R0 (contracts), R1/R2 (the zone map generator at 96x96, the map pool and its consumers), R3 (the planning phase and screen) and R4a (the per-mob-type buffs and mono-type camps). Zone terrain (styled interiors), camp rewards (chests, skills, relics), statuses and boons are not built (the rules below say which).

## Architecture rules (do not break)
- **Authoritative server.** Clients send inputs only (`move`, `stop`, `target`, `aim`, `attack`, `release`, `swap`, `pickup`, `plan`: `CLIENT_MESSAGE_TYPES`). The game client sends `target` and no longer sends `attack` or `release`; they are the low-level trigger that mobs and the weapon harness still use (the bots click their foe, like the game client). Never accept positions, damage, or outcomes from a client. Apart from a walk target, the numbers a client supplies are aim angles, a render time `t` (clamped to `MAX_REWIND_MS`, only on `attack`/`release`), a slot, a container id, a fighter id (`target`), an option index 0 or 1 and a ready flag (`plan`) and an order number `seq` (an integer 0..255 on `move`/`stop`; a bad one is dropped and the order still counts). `step.ts` `applyMove` copies a player's `seq` into the synced `PlayerState.moveSeq` on the tick it applies the order: nothing reads it but the client, which learns from it when its order took effect (the prediction's delay, `net/localMotion.ts`).
- **Fixed 20 Hz tick.** Message handlers validate and enqueue; they never mutate game state. `enqueueClientMessage` returns false for a rejected message, and only an accepted one counts as presence (training's idle limit). The sim consumes input queues at the start of each fixed 50 ms step.
- **Sim is pure.** `server/src/sim/*` must not import `@colyseus/core` or touch clients/sockets. It mutates the schema state and a non-synced runtime struct, and returns events. The Room converts events into messages and persistence. Test the sim headless.
- **Tick order is fixed** (`sim/step.ts`; do not reorder): inputs (players in the shuffled `actionOrder`, then mobs) → target locks (`updateTargets`) → weapon state machines → `afterWeapons` → movement → history → projectile sweep (present time, 10 ms substeps) → spawns → melee (rewound) → `beforeDamage` → sudden death's burn (recorded into the ledger) → ledger → systems → settle. That is the run's tick (`active`); the lobby and planning ticks are separate (next rule).
- **Planning phase** (`sim/planning.ts`, `step.ts`). With `SimConfig.planSeconds` above 0 (the live server's `PLAN_SECONDS`) a closing lobby goes `planning` → `active`; unset or 0 keeps today's flow exactly, training forces 0, and a room test that wants that flow sets `planSeconds: 0` and `firstPatienceSeconds: 0` (`loadConfig({})` now plans). The Room locks at planning start (joins are refused from then on), `RaidState.planEndsAt` is set and every player is dealt two spawns from `map.spawnPool`. A planning tick drains every player's inputs in the tick's secret `actionOrder` and applies only `plan` `{ spawn?: 0|1, boon?: 0|1, ready?: boolean }`, an index into the player's OWN two options, never a pool index or a position; everything else is dropped (a `plan` in any other phase too), nobody moves or is hurt, and no mob exists yet. The first to pick a spawn holds it, and within one tick the secret order decides, never the join order; picking the other option frees the first; `ready: true` latches (`PlayerState.planReady`) and freezes the picks. It ends at `planEndsAt` or once every CONNECTED player is ready (a dropped one does not hold it up): `assignSpawns` places everyone (a pick stands; the rest take their first free option, then the other, then the free spawn nearest in walk) and `startRaid` runs as ever. **The first patience timer** is `firstPatienceSeconds` flat (live `FIRST_PATIENCE_SECONDS` 300; unset or 0 = the usual scaling), armed there, after planning; later timers scale. **Deals are private**: drawn only from `world.rewardRandom` (never `world.random` or the public seed), kept in `world.planning`, and sent to a player only as their own `plan` message (`PlanMessage`: `endsAt`, `spawns` as pool indexes, `taken` = held by ANOTHER player's pick, `pick`, `boons` (empty until R7), `boon`) when planning starts, on every change to it and on a reconnect (`RaidRoom.sendPlan` → `planFor`); the synced state holds `planEndsAt` and `planReady` and nothing else (a test compares two differently dealt worlds). The two spawns of a pair lie in one walking tier (within `PLAN_WALK_TOLERANCE` 8% of a shared value, `sameTier`) and in different zones, and no spawn is dealt twice while 2 × players ≤ the pool; when tier pairs run out the deal keeps as many as it can and fills with the closest walks in different zones. A dropped player KEEPS the seat (`allowReconnection`, as in the run, not the lobby: the room is locked, so a blip would cost the whole run) and gets the default spawn without a pick; a leave records nothing and frees what they held.
- **The ledger: one way down, one place to settle.** Every hit (projectile sweep, melee, signature damage) is recorded with `recordHit` (`sim/ledger.ts`) and applied all at once by `applyLedger`. Each hit is multiplied by its attacker's outgoing multiplier (next rule), mitigated per victim, then goes to `applyHits` for a player (`sim/arena.ts`), `hurtMob` for a regional mob or `damageMob` for a dummy. Sudden death's burn is a ledger entry too (`suddenDeath`, recorded after `beforeDamage`: all true damage, from `SUDDEN_DEATH_KILLER`, no `hit` fx, no kill credit of its own), so it lands in the same pass as the tick's hits. Every exit from the fight (death, leave) goes through `downPlayer`. In-tick results come only from `settle()` at the end of the tick, so the shuffled input order never decides a winner. A fighter killed this tick still lands what it started. Simultaneous hits on one victim land together, and the kill goes to the largest rival hit. Simultaneous last deaths go to the higher hp fraction at tick start (an exact tie draws).
- **The outgoing multiplier is fixed at the start of the pass.** `applyLedger` computes every entry's multiplier (`outgoingMult`, `sim/mods.ts`) before any victim takes damage, from the hp as the pass began, so the order victims are processed in never changes it, and applies it to the entry's raw damage before `mitigateShares`. It is 1 + the attacker's damage sum + every `vs` row whose test holds against the victim + every `scale` row's bonus: rows within the damage key ADD (two +5% rows are +10%). It covers every hit the attacker records, signature damage-over-time and the mark included (that is how a damage buff reaches Sentenced, whose damage is nearly all its mark), never the arena's burn; mobs have no rows. `vs` tests `hpAbove`/`hpBelow` read the victim's hp fraction, the others are false until R4b. A `scale` row (Penitent) adds its `per` for every WHOLE `step` of the owner's missing hp (10% steps).
- **Spoils are paid after the pass.** While `applyLedger` runs (`world.combat.collecting`), a kill's GV, bounty (and the buffs it moves), buff grants, drops and a trigger's heal are queued with `payLater`. They run once every victim has taken their damage, from the lobby median feed as it stood before the pass, so a reward never decides who survives (sudden death's burn is in the pass too). A killer who goes down in the pass keeps the kill and its GV but gets no raised bounty and no rampage announcement. Outside the pass (`applyDamage`, tests) `payLater` runs at once. A mob's kill goes to the player who did it the most damage over the whole fight, not whoever lands the last hit. A mob that finishes a player a rival hit within `KILL_CREDIT_SECONDS` credits that rival (kill GV and bounty), as leaving does.
- **`hitLanded` is a hook, not a rule engine** (`WorldHooks`, called in `applyVictim`, `sim/ledger.ts`; `chainHook` adds to it). It fires once per ledger entry that took damage (mitigated amount above 0, so not sudden death's burn and not a victim that nothing got through to; DoT entries count), after the victim's damage and its `hit` fx, still inside the pass (`world.combat.collecting`, so kill spoils are still queued). It gets `(world, entry, applied)`, `applied` being the damage after armor and shield. Unset it costs nothing, and a hook must never change who survives or what a later entry of the same pass sees, or the ledger's order would matter. R4b's on-hit effects hang here: they decide from a snapshot taken at the start of the tick (or defer what they write until the pass ends, as `payLater` does), and what they apply counts from the next tick. Nothing consumes it yet.
- **`hurt` is the hook for a survivor's own hp** (`WorldHooks`, chainable; `applyVictim`, `sim/ledger.ts`). It fires once per victim per pass, after that victim's hits landed together (the arena's burn included) and before their `hit` fx and `hitLanded`, only if they took damage and are STILL standing, with `(world, c, applied)`. A handler decides from that fighter's own state alone and defers what it pays with `payLater`, so it can never change who else survives the pass. Blood Saint's `trigger` row (`on: "hpBelow"`, `sim/factions.ts`) hangs here: a `once` row fires the first time a pass leaves its owner below the threshold, even if it already was when it got the row (`PlayerRuntime.fired`, per run); other rows when a pass takes it across. `heal` with `ignoreLockout` skips the 3 s out-of-combat lockout and nothing else; `barrier` grants through `grantBarrier`; `status`, `cleanse` and `cooldown` actions do nothing yet.
- **Target lock** (`sim/targeting.ts`; `setLock`/`dropLock` in `sim/weapons.ts`; synced as `PlayerState.attackTarget`, "" = none). `target {id}` locks an enemy and the server plays the fight from then on, as mob AI does for a mob: each tick after the inputs, `policies.ts` `decide` with `lockSpacing` (close in to the weapon's `far`, then stand; never back off or strafe) sets the path (re-planned at most every `TARGET_REPATH_MS`, only when the goal moved over 8 u or the path ran out), holds the trigger while the target is in range with a clear line, and aims at its present position and velocity. The client's `aim` is ignored while locked, and `facing` follows the target (the shield's block cone reads it). Accepted only for another combatant that is standing, not evading, hostile (not self, not mob to mob; only players lock), within `TARGET_RANGE` (640 u); anything else is ignored with no change. Line of sight is not needed to lock (a target behind a wall is chased around it), only to attack, but a target out of sight with no path to it is refused (a mob across the locked coliseum gates; the client also offers no mobs while `gatesLocked`). Clicking the fighter already locked changes nothing; a new lock clears the earlier walk. A `target` order costs at most one path search per `TARGET_REPATH_MS` per player (`lockSearchAt`; an order out of sight sooner than that is refused, and its search doubles as the chase's first plan), and the chase's own re-plan time (`lockPlanAt`) survives setting or dropping a lock, so spamming orders (up to `MAX_ACTIONS_PER_TICK` a tick) never buys extra searches. `endRun` drops every lock, so a winner's `attackTarget` is not synced through the linger. Dropped (trigger and path cleared, the fighter stops where it stands) when the target is no longer standing, is evading, is past `TARGET_RANGE`, or has been out of sight for `TARGET_LOS_GRACE_MS` (1.5 s; until then the chase paths around the wall) after having been seen once since the lock (`lockSeen`; a target locked behind a wall is chased until first seen, bounded by the leash), when it is out of sight and the chase finds no path to it, or the player holds no weapon. **Orders replace each other in arrival order within a tick** (`InputQueue.moveBefore`, like `aimBefore`): `move`/`stop`/`attack` drop the lock (an unreachable `move` still does), `target` lets go of the trigger. **A player's own order cancels the attack its lock is winding up** (`cancelLockedWindup`, `sim/weapons.ts`, LoL's move-cancels-windup): a `move`, a `stop` (applied in `step.ts`, an unreachable move included) or an accepted `target` on another fighter, while its `PendingAction.target !== undefined` and it has not landed. No swing, ball or arrow; the cooldown is refunded (`readyAt = startedAt`, it was over when the attack began); the root goes (`cancelAction`); the bow's `drawStartedAt` clears; a `cancel` fx tells every client to drop that fighter's telegraph, and the walk starts in the same tick. Re-clicking the locked fighter (or, once the lock has dropped, the fighter the attack is already aimed at), a refused `target` and a landed attack (the axe's root after its swing stays) cancel nothing, and neither does an `attack` input. `releaseTrigger` (teleport, going down, a dropped client, training revive) drops it too; a swap or a pickup does not. An attack the lock starts records its `target` on the `PendingAction` and is aimed again as it lands (a swing straight at the target's position at its rewind time) or leaves (`leadAim` from the drawn origin at the target's present position and velocity), if the target still stands and is hostile; then it flies straight and can be dodged. An unlocked attack (a mob's, the harness's) keeps the old aims: a swing and a ball go where they were aimed when the attack started, an arrow along the aim held when its draw ends (the bow's aim at release), and `decide` leads the bow for the lags alone, without its windup. A locked attack has no client `t`: `lagMs = TICK_MS`.
- **Windups root.** Every attack roots its fighter from its start until the swing lands or the arrow or ball leaves (`moveMultiplier`: 0 while `action !== null` or inside `rootUntil`, else 1; nothing else slows a walk: buffs and Frost's chill are `world.moveModifiers`). A directional windup (a mob's, the harness's, an `attack` input) is committed: a `move` given during it is kept as the path and walked afterwards, only a stagger (not on the bow) or a swap cancels it. One the target lock started is the player's own click, so the player's next `move`/`stop`/`target` cancels it too (see Target lock): the game client walks away from a windup it no longer wants. The bow is a fixed windup (kind `"draw"`, `landsAt`, `drawStartedAt` synced for the pull), never a partial draw. `weaponTimings`: sword and bash 0.1 s, axe 0.25 s / 25% of the interval and root 0.5 s / 50%, wand `min(WAND_WINDUP 0.4 s, 0.8 × interval)`, bow `min(BOW_WINDUP 0.6 s, 0.92 × interval)`. These are the anti-kiting lever (below, Weapons and factions).
- **Players and mobs fight through `Combatant`** (`sim/combatant.ts`). Weapons, melee, projectiles and the ledger take either; only how they go down differs. A regional mob's faction is its region's scaled by `MOB_K` (elite `ELITE_K`) and paced, read through `factionOf` like a player's, so signatures, armor and timings apply to it unchanged.
- **Mobs are players who send inputs.** `sim/mobAI.ts` is a system (after damage). It pushes `move`/`stop`/`aim`/`attack`/`release` into the mob's own `InputQueue`, and `step.ts` applies them next tick through the same handlers as a player's. Idle mobs send nothing, and mobs never lock a target. A mob walking home (`return`) is evading: `hurtMob` returns 0, `isEvading` keeps it out of hit tests, signature targets and contacts, it starts nothing new (a shot already in the air still lands), and it is whole when it arrives. It gives up when it leaves its leash or 6 s after it last *dealt* damage, so kiting from outside its leash gains nothing. **The leash is the camp's territory** (`leashOk` in shared `mobs.ts`, ROGUELIKE_PLAN D15): a mob of a camp whose territory holds its home spot (`camp.area` > 0) stays on that camp's `territoryGrid` cells and within `min(camp.radius, MOB_LEASH_CAP)` of its home, the cap being 12 tiles (generated camps measure a radius of 10.8 to 30 tiles, so the cap binds on almost all); any other mob (no camp, `area` 0, an all-zero `territoryGrid`, a spot outside the territory: hand-built maps and tests) keeps the `MOB_LEASH_RADIUS` 9-tile circle. A ranged mob backs off only to points whose four ±24 u axis neighbours are inside the leash too. The coliseum teleport puts every mob back on its camp spot at full hp. Mobs never hurt each other (a hit is recorded with scale 0) and their shots fly past each other. A training world never spawns regional mobs.
- **Signatures ride the ledger.** Faction bonus damage (burn, backlash, disease, mark) is recorded in the tick's ledger in the `beforeDamage` phase, so it lands with the tick's hits. Heals go through `heal()` in `sim/factions.ts` (3 s out-of-combat lockout; every lifesteal is exempt, and `ignoreLockout` skips only the lockout: Blood Saint). Three buff effects live in this module, so none of them runs in a world built with `signatures: false`: **lifesteal** works for any faction from `lifesteal` rows, capped at `LS_CAP` 0.25, Holy's own comes on top, the total never passes `LIFESTEAL_MAX` 1 (`lifestealShare`); **`markRate`** makes Sentenced's mark's engaged time grow (1 + rate) times as fast toward the same cap (it does nothing for anyone else); **Blood Saint** is the `hurt` trigger above. `sim/factions.ts` stretches chilled fighters' `readyAt`, bow draw (its start and the moment the arrow leaves, so the cycle stays interval / chill) and press buffer every tick; the other windups are not stretched, so a chilled swing still lands on time. Weapon timing changes must keep the chill working: `npm run harness:factions` checks it against `testing/realtime.py`. A landed stagger (the 0.5 s immunity starts) pauses the staggered fighter's burn, disease and mark for its weapon's windup (`staggerPause`; none for a bow, whose draw a stagger never cancels; the real sim only, `realtime.py` has no staggers).
- **Buffs and the feed** (`shared/src/buffs.ts`, `sim/buffs.ts`, `sim/feed.ts`). Every mob type is also a buff (`BuffId` = `MobTypeId`; `BUFFS` in `MOB_TYPES` order: zone, elite flag, `effects` rows valued at 3 STACKS (D6), `worksWith`/`counters` tags). A mob kill grants stacks of the buff of ITS type: `MOB_BUFF_STACKS` 1.1 × m (`ELITE_BUFF_STACKS` 3.1 × m for an elite), m = the reward curve × the catch-up (`payMobKill` → `grantBuff`, through `payLater`). Stacks live in `PlayerState.buffs` (a float32 map by id; only players hold buffs). At n stacks every number of a buff is scaled by `buffScale(n)` = S(n)/S(3), S(x) = sigmoid(x − 3) − sigmoid(−3): 1 stack 0.16×, 2 0.49×, 3 1×, 4 1.51×, 6 2×, a plateau of about 2.1× (an on-hit effect's `cap` never moves). The bounty (`transferBuffs`, on the `bountyClaimed` hook) moves half of EACH buff id the victim owns. Rows that need statuses (`needs: "status"`: the `onHit` rows of Chain Warden, Bloatfly, Rot Monk and Frost Wisp, Inquisitor's streak and Glacial Warden's slowed `vs` rows) are catalog data: the HUD and tooltips show them, nothing applies them until R4b. The kill combos, kill bursts, kill shield and the old three-stat stacks are gone (the `combo` fx is retired); `barrier` stays for R6's skills. The reward curve and the catch-up read the feed (`kills + 0.25 × mob kills`); the **bounty reads player kills only**. A mob kill's grant no longer draws from `world.random` (the old stat roll did), so loot rolls after a mob kill differ from before. "Shield" in a buff's text is the magic-resistance stat (D7), not the barrier.
- **Stat mods: the mirrors are the one source of truth** (`shared/src/mods.ts`, `effects.ts`, `sim/mods.ts`). `PlayerState.asMult`, `windupMult`, `speedMult` (uint16 THOUSANDTHS, 1000 = ×1) and `armorBonus`, `shieldBonus` (int16 TENTHS of a point) hold the stats that effects change. The quantized value is authoritative (D22): `recomputeMods` writes a mirror only when its quantized value changed (so it patches only then), and the sim reads back the DECODED value (`decodeMult`/`decodeFlat`), never the double it folded, so a client decodes exactly what the sim used. Readers: `attackSpeedMult`/`moveSpeedMult`, `timingsOf` (`windupMult` through `scaleWindup`: the windup, and the axe's root with it), `defenseOf` (the flat bonuses). Max hp, the damage sum, lifesteal and the mark rate are read from the build's sums, not mirrored. A player's build is its buffs' rows (`buffRows`, `needs` rows left out), rebuilt only when its buffs change. The fold: `stat`, `flat` and `while` rows ADD within a key, a multiplier key becomes `1 + sum`, different keys MULTIPLY where the sim combines them (attack speed with damage, `speedMult` with the chill), flat rows add points; `vs`, `scale`, `onHit` and `trigger` rows do not fold; a row whose `requires` ids are not all owned is off. Every `world.modSources` entry is asked too (multipliers multiply in, flats add). Folding is event driven, never every fighter every tick: `grantBuff` (so `transferBuffs`) re-folds at once, so a grant changes the attack and the walk in the same tick; a source whose answer changes must call `recomputeMods`; `updateMods` (the last `world.systems` entry; `gameplay.ts` installs it last) checks only players that own a `while` row and, with sources registered, every fighter's answer. Cinder Hound's `chasingLock` holds while the player has a target lock (on or off, so its mirror patches only when a lock starts or ends) and is re-checked once a tick, so the mirror follows the lock one tick late, by design. A mob has no synced mirror (what a source gives it stays server-side). `sim/mods.ts` is a leaf: it must not import `weapons.ts`, `arena.ts`, `ledger.ts`, `buffs.ts`, a status runtime or anything else that imports it (type-only imports are fine).
- **Clients decode the mirrors, never recompute them.** The client reads `PlayerState.buffs` only to DISPLAY them (the build strip, tooltips, the buff toast). The local prediction (`net/localPrediction.ts`) and `FighterArt` read only the decoded mirrors (`decodeMirrors`/`decodeMult`/`decodeFlat`; `scaleWindup` for the bow's draw and the axe's root, `fullDrawSeconds` takes WIRE values), never stacks or rows, and `sync.test.ts` compares the mirrors bit for bit. A client test fixture spreads `WIRE_IDENTITY_MIRRORS` (1000, 1000, 1000, 0, 0), never 1; to regenerate `SERVER_FULL_DRAW` in `fighterArt.test.ts`, run the server's `timingsOf` on a new `PlayerState` with the mirrors set through `encodeMult`.
- **Camp rosters are mono-type, rolled at world build and published** (`shared/src/rosters.ts`, `sim/camps.ts`; ROGUELIKE_PLAN D5). Before anyone joins, each camp of `map.camps` gets one normal mob type and `RaidState.camps[i]` (`CampState`: `type`, `alive`, and `reward` "" and `state` 0 until R5) holds it at the camp's own index; a training world or one with `mobs: false` publishes none. Every non-elite mob of a camp has the camp's type, and the zone's elite stands at the `eliteSpot` of its elite camp (the rest of that camp is the camp's normal type). Types are drawn per zone without repeats, zones in first-seen order, from `world.rewardRandom` only (never `world.random`, so the loot rolls and every test's scripted sequence are what they were): a generated zone has four camps, so its three normal types each stand in one camp and a fourth camp repeats one (counts 1, 1, 2; the first three camps in camp order are always distinct); a hand-built map's zone with fewer camps lacks a type. Mobs still appear at `raidStarted`: `spawnRegionalMobs` walks `map.camps` and sets each mob's `MobState.type`, `.camp` (index + 1) and name from the roster and `MOB_TYPES` (a mob placed with no type falls back through `mobTypeOf`), `killMob` counts `alive` down, and a world assembled without `gameplay.ts` gets its roster from `ensureRosters` when mobs spawn. The roster is public from the lobby on, by design (the planning screen shows each camp's type and buff); only what is rolled after it stays private (the `rewardRandom` rule).
- **Combat netcode.** Projectiles are not in the synced state: they are plain objects in `world.projectiles`. A projectile is announced once, in the `fxs` batch of the step that loosed it, and never updated. Clients fly it from `(x, y, vx, vy, t)`, and a `projEnd` fx says where and why it stopped. A client that joins or reconnects mid-flight is sent the projectiles then flying as a batch of its own (`FxChannel.inFlight`, queued behind its full state by core). Melee samples the attacker and every target at the attacker's render time (`rewindT`: the client's `t` on a low-level `attack`; a locked attack, which is all the game client makes, has none and rewinds one tick, and the server aims it from the present: the target lock). A projectile leaves from the shooter's rewound (drawn) position, and the `aim` angles that low-level attacks and the shield use are measured from where the client draws its own player: since the local fighter is predicted, that is the PREDICTED position, which is where the server will have it when the aim lands (closer to the server's present than the old delayed one). Locked attacks stay server-aimed from the present; `attack`/`release` users (the harness, mobs) keep the rewound origin and `t`. Keep those three consistent.
- **Wire format.** All fx of a step go out as ONE `fxs` message (`shared/src/fxCodec.ts`, `rooms/FxChannel.ts`): integer tuples, fighters named by index into the append-only `RaidState.roster`, projectile launches in the same batch (`FxBatch.p`). Players still in the lobby are not rostered (no fx names one, and an entry is for good). A mob is rostered when it is placed. `unpackFx(batch, room.state.roster, onDrop?)` needs the client's copy of the roster: without it every id-bearing fx is dropped, and `onDrop` is how a skipped tuple is noticed (the client warns once in dev builds; the e2e run asserts nothing was dropped). The client holds `fxs` batches from the join until the scene starts (`net/fxInbox.ts`). The code tables are wire format: grow them at the end, never reorder (the newest fx kind is `cancel`, 13: `[13, id]`, a player's own order cancelled its lock's attack). `buff` (10) is `[10, id, B, stacks × 1000, total × 1000]`, B being the buff's code 0..19 in `MOB_TYPES` order (the BUFF table: append a type at its end); kind 11, `combo`, is retired with the kill combos: the encoder never emits it, its code is never reused, and the decoder drops it through `onDrop`. Adding a field to an `FxEvent` means adding it to its tuple and to `packages/shared/test/fxCodec.test.ts`. Synced numbers are narrow on the wire (float32 positions and hp, uint32 timers); the server keeps doubles.
- **The map is never synced.** State carries only how to build it: `seed`, `attempt` (the certified generator attempt) and `mapHash` (uint32 of `mapHashU32(map)`: the 32-bit FNV-1a of every gameplay layer: seed, attempt, size, `tiles`, `terrain`, `regionGrid`, `territoryGrid`, camps, spawn pool, regions, ruins, crate and ground spots; NOT the cosmetic `props`), set once in `createWorld`. The server, the bots and every client run exactly `generateMap(seed, attempt)`: that one attempt, never a loop (it throws when the attempt does not certify). The server and the bots build with `decor: false` (no `props`, drawn from a stream of their own so no other layer moves); the client builds with decor, and a test guards that both hash the same. A client whose map hashes to anything else shows "This raid's map did not match your client. Please reload." and leaves with a consented leave (`net/roomMap.ts`, `main.ts`: the profile is freed at once); a bot throws (`roomMap`). `generateMap(seed)` without an attempt loops: for tests, benches and tools, never for a map a peer must match. Loot contents are rolled server-side with a non-public RNG. While `state.gatesLocked`, movement, line of fire and loot line of sight use `collisionMap(world)` (`withGates(world.map)`), never `world.map`. `coliseumSpots` hands out the coliseum's inner spawns. `RaidScene`, `MapView`, `PlanningView` and the minimap take the map as a parameter and never generate it.
- **Generated maps are certified, with no relaxed attempt** (`mapgen.ts`, `zones.ts`, `cover.ts`, `coliseum.ts`; `test/mapCertify.ts` re-checks every guarantee below from the `GameMap` alone). `generateMap(seed, attempt?, { styleZones?, decor? })`: with an attempt, exactly that one (throws when it fails); without, attempts 0, 1, ... until one certifies (a guard throws after 64). `tryAttempt(seed, attempt)` is the map or null; `certifiedMap(seed)` the first of `MAX_ATTEMPTS` 16 that certifies, or null (the pool discards that seed). Any failed check fails the attempt; nothing is ever relaxed. **Determinism:** one `mulberry32` stream per attempt (`seed` for attempt 0, else `seed + attempt * 0x9e3779b1`), drawn in a fixed order (changing the order moves every map: re-pin on purpose with `scripts/pinMaps.ts`); only `+ - * /`, `sqrt`, `floor`, `ceil`, `round`, `abs`, `min`, `max` (`detSin`/`detCos` in `cover.ts` are built from them), never `Math.pow`, `exp`, `log`, `atan2`, `hypot`, `sin`, `cos` or `random` (a test spies on 23 Math functions through a generation, lane tables included); integer value noise, integer 5/7 chamfer distance transforms and heap keys; total comparators (value, then tile index). Hot imports are copied to module constants in `zones.ts`, `cover.ts` and `mapgen.ts`: under vitest each use of an import is a property read (30% slower).
- **What an attempt certifies** (96x96; the numbers are in `zones.ts`, `mapgen.ts`, `coliseum.ts`).
  - *Connectivity and gates:* all walkable floor is one 4-connected piece with the gates open; with `withGates` closed the arena is sealed, in one piece, and the rest is one piece. Four straight 4-wide gates (n, e, s, w) are floor in `map.tiles` and the only links between the arena and the rest; a clear lane lies in front of each. The coliseum is the same fixed layout on every map (one of 8 pillar variants per seed).
  - *The 480 u sight cap:* with the gates open, no 32 u-wide strip of sight-open ground is longer than `MAX_SIGHT_CORRIDOR` (also inside the coliseum with the gates closed). It is certified on the fine lattice (48 directions, lines a quarter tile apart, every lane at most 14.98 tiles) **anchored at `LATTICE_ORIGIN`**, the coliseum centre less (32, 32): the pillar layouts, shifted by `CX - 32`, were certified where they sat at 64x64, and from a corner anchor some leave a 15.5-tile lane inside the footprint that no rock may cut. `longestLane` and the tests use the same anchor. The independent certifier also measures the exact strip at off-lattice angles (longest 464 u) and bounds 16 u discs at 576 u (every 10th seed); narrower gaps stay unguaranteed (Known TODOs).
  - *Zones:* five, each one 4-connected painted piece, painted areas within 8 tiles of each other (`AREA_TOLERANCE`; equal within 1 tile before the territory repair), walkable floor share max/min <= 1.15, at least 24 ring-road tiles each (`RING_CONTACT_MIN`: every zone touches the coliseum), at most 100 sliver tiles (`SLIVER_MAX`). They are grown "paint bucket" style from a ring-road arc joined to an outer seed by its spine, so there are no ridges or passes: zones meet directly, and the order of the five around the ring is shuffled by the seed. Every tile off the border wall is a zone or the coliseum in `regionGrid`.
  - *Camps and territories:* four camps per zone (`CAMPS_PER_ZONE`), one elite camp per zone (its camp farthest from the coliseum, the elite at spot 0), centres at least 9 tiles apart map-wide (`CAMP_GAP`: a camp's halo, its 2.5-tile clearing plus one tile, is protected ground, so two closer would form a lane no rock could cut). A **territory** is the part of its zone nearer to a camp than to the zone's other camps by path cost; the territories of a zone partition all its painted tiles (`territoryGrid` = 1 + camp index), and each one's floor is one 4-connected piece holding its clearing, spots and chest tile (the cover's territory guard treats a rock that would split one as a last resort; the repair after the cover joins a cut-off piece to the territory it touches, or moves it to another zone when it is at most `STRAY_MAX` 40 tiles, and fails the attempt otherwise). The camp stands at its territory's **pole** (the deepest cell by 5/7 chamfer distance whose halo fits) and no territory floor tile is more than `TERRITORY_RADIUS_MAX` 30 tiles from it. That bound is a seating rule, not a filter (as a filter it would reject about 65% of layouts): the pole is searched only among cells within 30 tiles of every tile of the territory, the camp seeds are placed for coverage, and a zone set whose camps cannot be seated is redrawn inside the attempt.
  - *Spawn pool:* 24 to 32 members tagged by zone, in at least 4 zones, all within 8% of one BFS 5/7 walking distance to the coliseum centre (picked within 4.5%, checked again after the cover), at least 5 tiles apart and from every camp centre (picked 7 away, so the cover can cut ground between a spawn and a halo), each with a clear 3x3 outside ruins, and screened so that no two members within 480 u can shoot each other (every aim ray within `RANGED_HIT_REACH` of the other's centre meets a wall first). The `findPath` walk ratio max/min over the pool is 1.17 on average and 1.198 at worst (seeds 0-1999; the owner's target is 1.2).
- **The zone style hook** (`styler.ts`; the zone-terrain contract). `ZONE_STYLE_HOOK` is null: no hook ships, so generated maps have no LOW tiles, terrain or props yet, and zone terrain fills the hook. It runs once per attempt with the whole zone grid (`StyleState`), after the zones, camps, territories and ruins and BEFORE the rock chunks, the spawn pool, the crates and the cover, so the walking contour, the spawn and crate picks and the cover certification all see the styled ground and its walls count as cover. It may only turn painted FLOOR outside `protect` into WALL or LOW, write `terrain` kinds and append `props`, and draws from `styleRng(seed, attempt, zone, stream)` (a placeholder for zone terrain's `hashSeed`: swapping it changes every styled map), never the generator's stream. The generator throws on a breach (a styler bug, not a failed attempt), drops props whose tile changed class, and `validateTerrain(map, { styled: true })` gates a styled attempt; spawn-pool members (5x5), crates (3x3) and ground spots only take tiles with no terrain.
- **Every map query names its mask** (`map.ts`). `TILE_LOW` (lava, fence, crevasse) blocks movement but not sight. Sight (lanes, shots, line of sight, the ranged reach of signatures) reads `MASK_SIGHT` = `TILE_WALL`; bodies (A*, discs and sweeps, placement, pickup reach) read `MASK_MOVE` (everything but floor, so a future class blocks walkers by default); a swing reads `MASK_MELEE`. `cover.ts`, the territory repair and the certifier read `MASK_SIGHT` for lanes, cones and the spawn screen and `MASK_MOVE` for connectivity, sealing, the ring road, the territory guard and walking distances; probes restore the byte they saved, so a probed LOW tile never comes back as floor.
- **Map contract** (`map.ts`, ROGUELIKE_PLAN 3.2; fully real since R2). `GameMap` carries `attempt`, `camps: CampDef[]` (`id` "fire-1", `zone`, centre, clearing radius `r`, 3-5 `spots`, `eliteSpot`, the reward `chest` tile (the clearing centre, which no spot takes), `area` (floor tiles of its territory), `radius` (its farthest floor tile)), `territoryGrid` (0 = none, else 1 + the camp's index in `camps`), `spawnPool`, `terrain`, `props`, and `regions[]` with `pole` (the zone's label point: a floor tile outside ruins) and `area` (its painted tiles). `camps` runs in `REGION_FACTIONS` order, then camp index; `regions[].mobCamps`, `regions[].spawns` and `map.spawns` (the pool interleaved by zone) are VIEWS of `camps` and `spawnPool`; `crateSpots` (3-4 weapon crates per zone) and `groundSpots` (20-30) feed `loot.ts` as before. Extraction (`extracts`, `ExtractZoneDef`, `EXTRACT_RADIUS`), the ridges and passes between zones, `regions[].anchor` and the wedge generator are gone; a hand-built map (`mapFromTiles`) has no regions or camps and all-zero grids. Read today: `camps` (the mob spawn, rosters, planning screen, bots), `spawnPool` (the deal), `pole` (labels), `territoryGrid`, `area` and `radius` (the leash), everything (`mapHash`); nothing reads `chest` until R5. A map is read-only once built: the caches are per object (`withGates`, the masks, `moveComponents`; `forgetMapCaches` for a test that edits tiles).
- **Rooms take their map from the pool** (`server/src/mapPool.ts`, ROGUELIKE_PLAN D20). `RaidRoom.buildWorld` (a room's first join, a training room's too) calls `maps.take()` synchronously and hands the map to `createGameWorld({ seed: map.seed, map })`, so `onAuth` and `onJoin` stay synchronous and a room nobody joins takes none; `createWorld` without a `map` calls `generateMap(state.seed)` in process (tests, benches, the harness). One `worker_thread` per process (`mapWorker.ts`, started through `mapWorkerBoot.mjs`, with its own 44 MB of lane tables) certifies crypto-random seeds with `certifiedMap(seed, { decor: false })`, only while the queue is short (`MAP_POOL_TARGET` 8, refilled at `MAP_POOL_REFILL_AT` 6: an idle server costs nothing), and posts each whole map by structured clone (about 52 KB, 0.2 ms) with its hash; the main thread checks seed, attempt and `mapHashU32` again and drops a mismatch, so the pool serves only a certified (seed, attempt) a client can rebuild. An empty queue (a burst, a worker that is down or warming) falls back to ONE synchronous `certifiedMap` on the main thread, logged (about 35 ms, 75-120 at p99; a discarded seed moves on to the next, up to `MAP_FALLBACK_SEEDS` 8). A worker that crashes or errors restarts with a backoff of 1 s doubling to 60 s and never takes a room down. `warmMapgen()` runs at boot on both threads and shutdown terminates the worker; `MAP_POOL=0` (tests: `MapPool({ target: 0 })`) runs no worker.
- **The client builds the map off the page's thread** (`net/mapWorker.ts`, `mapWorkerEntry.ts`, `roomMap.ts`). A module Web Worker builds the lane tables (44 MB) and runs one throwaway attempt once the hub is drawn and the page idle (`warmWhenIdle`), so the first real map meets a warm JIT. `enterRoom` then shows "Building the map…", awaits `generateMap(seed, attempt)` from the worker (tile layers transferred, cached by (seed, attempt), one map object per room), checks the hash and only then registers its handlers and starts the scene; a room that closes meanwhile is noticed there. Without a worker (tests, a failed spawn) the same call builds on the page thread.
- **`world.rewardRandom` is a private stream** for reward and roster rolls (camp rosters and plan deals draw from it today; chest options and "?" kinds will). `createWorld` seeds it (sfc32, a 128-bit state) from `rewardSalt`, four words `RaidRoom` takes from `crypto.randomBytes` (never the public seed, which every client holds; a 32-bit state could be brute-forced once rolls are revealed). A number is also accepted and expanded, for tests. It is separate from `world.random` so that drawing rewards never shifts the loot rolls or the sequence tests script (`createWorld` takes exactly one draw from `random`, for `orderSalt`, and none for this), and separate from `orderSalt` because that salt also keys `tieRank`, whose outcomes a client can stage and read (Known TODOs): a stream derived from it could be brute-forced. Without a `rewardSalt` (tests, the harness) it is derived from `orderSalt` by a tagged hash, so it matches neither `actionOrder` nor `tieRank`: fine for a test world, never for a live room. Anything hidden ("?" flags, chest rolls, plan deals) comes from here and stays server-side or in private messages; a roster is rolled from here too but is published in `state.camps`.
- **Clients render in the past, except the local fighter.** Remote entities render at `serverNow − INTERP_DELAY_MS` via `SnapshotBuffer` (`shared/interp.ts`). The LOCAL fighter is predicted (`net/localMotion.ts`, presentation only: nothing it computes is ever sent, the server stays authoritative): an order walks a model at once along the same shared A* path, `drawn = sim + visual`, and each patch pulls the model back in line (`S(T)` against the model `delay` earlier, `delay` measured from `moveSeq`; small errors go into a decaying `visual` offset, never faster than the `CORRECT_SPEED_*` caps). It cannot drift or hang: an order the server never applies (lost while reconnecting) is forgotten after `ORDER_EXPIRE_MS` and does not hold the comparison off; samples off by more than `DIVERGE_ERR` for `DIVERGE_PATCHES` in a row with nothing in flight and the server not heading where the model walks (a revive, a halt, a walk it did not order) drop the model's walk; an error past `SNAP_DISTANCE` (even with an order pending, beyond what that order's walk explains) or an offset past `VISUAL_SNAP_DISTANCE` snaps to the server and plans the destination again from there; the teleport and the local death fx `cut()` it (the next sample snaps, however short the jump); the scene resets it while `room.reconnection.isReconnecting`; a click is clamped to the world as `parsePoint` clamps it. Dead, in the lobby, after the run ends, and until a teleport is drawn it is drawn from the snapshots, except that while its death or the teleport's cut is still ahead of what is drawn it stays where it was last drawn (`localDrawSource`). After a `target` it follows the server's chase (the newest sample carried one tick by its velocity): the lock is the server's. The model enters follow when a patch shows the lock (`PlayerState.attackTarget`; not while a move or stop of ours is still in flight, which would end it) or at once on a click it expects to be accepted (`expectLock`, for the responsiveness), and leaves when the lock empties; a lock that never shows within `ORDER_EXPIRE_MS` was refused, and the walk it interrupted is planned again. The scene feeds it the speed mirror and the chill (a Frost mob chills like a Frost player), and the axe's root (`net/localPrediction.ts`); anything unmodelled is the server's correction. Remote fighters and fx stay in render time, so what is drawn at the local fighter (hits on it, its telegraph) plays at its predicted position: accepted.
- **Rendering is separate from logic.** Phaser scenes only read interpolated state and send inputs. Projection lives in `client/src/render/projection.ts` (screenY = worldY · Y_SCALE, height drawn upward, depth = worldY). `MapView` draws the 96x96 map as Graphics chunks (floor 24 x 8 tiles, walls one row by 24), hidden each `PRE_RENDER` when the camera does not show them (`mapCull.ts`; a Graphics replays its whole buffer whether or not it is seen): any new static map layer goes inside a chunk. Zone borders come from `zoneEdgeStrips(regionGrid, walls)` (`zoneEdges.ts`): per side of a border, in that zone's colour and dash (`ZONE_DASH`, one rhythm per zone: the cue that is not hue), on see-through tiles only, built once per map. Client tests derive map spans from `WORLD_W`/`WORLD_H` or `mapSpan()`, never a literal size, and painter goldens use hand-built maps.
- **The map screens read and send `plan` only** (`ui/planning.ts`, `mapPainter.ts`, `route.ts`, `mapHold.ts`). One `PlanningView` in four modes, chosen each frame by `planningModeFor(phase, { training, held })`: `preview` (lobby), `planning`, `map` (a run, while Tab is held) and `hidden` (everything else; hidden takes no pointer and no key). It lives in `uiRoot` under the chrome, is not an Esc panel, is laid out in CSS (so it survives a resize or fullscreen) and its markers are real buttons placed by percent over the painted canvas. The deal reaches it through `PlanInbox`, registered in `enterRoom` before the first state (the newest message wins, so a deal that beats the scene is kept). **The route is client-only**: waypoints in `sessionStorage` under `raidbr.route.v1:<roomId>` (a reload finds them), planned with the shared `findPath`, never sent, and restarted from the player's real position on the first active frame (the client cannot know which spawn the server chose on a timeout). Planning freezes the client's world: `moveTo`, `sendTarget`, `handleKey` and `streamAim` return early. Hold Tab (`MapHold`) cancels the browser's Tab only during a run, lets go on blur or a hidden page and defers to text fields and the open settings or help card. A zone is never told by hue alone (`ZONE_SHAPE`, `ZONE_PATTERN`; a matchup chip carries a sign as well as a tone), and the minimap's output is pinned by a golden-hash test. Planning keys: 1 and 2 pick the spawns, arrows rove the markers, Enter on a camp toggles a route stop, Delete removes one; Ready is its own button. Settings `showRoute` and `textScale` (these screens' text only).
- **The build strip is display only** (`ui/buffsHud.ts`, `buffTip.ts`, `buffTheme.ts`). One chip per catalog buff, built once in catalog order and repainted only when a displayed value changes. A chip's identity is its zone colour, the zone's marker shape and, for an elite's buff, a star; chips take the pointer for their tooltips, so a right-click on one does not walk. A row the server does not apply yet is marked "(coming)". There are no kill bursts or combo UI (`ToastTone` is buff, error or ok).
- **The picture is a viewport, not the window.** `view/viewport.ts` fixes the world height every player sees (`REF_VIEW_H` projected px; wider windows show more width, capped at 21:9 with bars; below 4:3 the width stays at the 4:3 minimum), the canvas is rendered at the device density (capped at 2x and a 4K pixel budget, `settings.hiDpi` off = 1x) and the camera zoom is canvas px per projected world px. Never assume camera zoom 1 or 1 canvas px = 1 CSS px: read a pointer's world point through the camera (`screenToWorld`, `pointer.updateWorldPoint`), set a scroll with `scrollForTopLeft` or `centerOn`, and scale a Phaser camera shake by `1 / zoom²` (`ScreenJuice`). The DOM overlay `#ui` is laid out at `--ui-w` x `--ui-h` and CSS-scaled, so overlay code uses those variables and `data-ui-short`/`data-ui-narrow` instead of `vw`/`vh`/size media queries, and `getBoundingClientRect()` for anything that meets the pointer.
- **The hub is client-only.** `HubScene` has no room and no server. Its layout and walking rules are pure (`hub/hubLayout.ts`), and a station only calls back into the menu (`onStation`). What it shows comes from the REST profile.
- **Training is a mode, not a fork.** `client.create(TRAINING_ROOM_NAME)` makes a private one-seat room of the same `RaidRoom` class. The server registers two handlers (`defineRoom(RaidRoom, { mode: "arena" })` under `ROOM_NAME`, `{ mode: "training" }` under `TRAINING_ROOM_NAME`). Colyseus merges handler options over the client's, so a client cannot choose the mode. A training world has `SimConfig.training` + `dummies`: a plain dummy, a shield dummy and a rack of every weapon next to the spawn, started the moment its player joins. It has no planning phase (the Room forces `planSeconds` 0), regional mobs, camp rosters, patience timer, teleport, sudden death, result, GV or stats. Three locks keep it from recording anything: `trainingSettle` replaces `suddenDeath`/`settle` (anyone down is revived), `RaidRoom.settle` and `bankVoided` return early in training (a down *between* ticks still emits `resolved`, which the Room ignores), and leaving is `abandonPlayer`, never MIA. A dropped or leaving client frees the profile at once (`onDrop` holds no seat in training). A player with no accepted input for `trainingIdleSeconds` (300) is let go: only an input `enqueueClientMessage` accepts refreshes `lastInputAt`. Every training branch sits behind `world.config.training` / `RaidRoom.training`; an arena world never reaches it.
- **GV banks at resolve.** The sim only accumulates run GV and reports it in `resolved` results. The Room banks it (`recordRun`, or `bankGv` for a run voided by shutdown). Run gear never persists.
- **Persistence is `ProfileStore`.** Mutations are synchronous in memory (nothing awaits between a check and its change), writes are coalesced and atomic (temp file, fsync, rename, directory fsync) with retry and backoff, and `flush()` runs on shutdown. A v1 file migrates on load. A file that cannot be parsed stops the server and is moved aside, never overwritten.
- **The shop is server-only.** Purchases and equips are `POST /api/profile/:name/purchase|equip` with the profile token. The client sends only a cosmetic id (and a slot). `ProfileStore.purchase/equip` check the token, the catalog price, ownership and the balance in one synchronous step, so a double spend is impossible. Spending lowers `gv` only, never `gvLifetime` or `gvByMonth` (the leaderboard ranks GV earned). Achievements are evaluated inside `recordRun`. An id lands in `achievements` once, and its reward cosmetics are granted in the same step. Win achievements and the per-faction/weapon counters count only `winQualified` wins (the win-bonus gate: `!winBonusWithheld`), so a walkover against a throwaway callsign earns nothing. Kill and bounty achievements are not gated (their rewards are unpriced; an owner decision). The leaderboard ranking is cached per month (dropped when GV is credited in it), and `you` on `GET /api/leaderboard` is sent only with that profile's own token. Equipped cosmetics reach other players as one synced `PlayerState.look` word, fixed at join.

## Conventions
- Colyseus 0.18 APIs (not 0.15/0.16 tutorials): `defineServer/defineRoom` from `@colyseus/core`, `Room<{ state: X }>`, `this.onMessage` (or `messages = { ... }`), `setTimestep` (`setSimulationInterval` is its deprecated name), `client.sessionId` (no `client.id`), a static `onAuth` for matchmake-time checks, client `@colyseus/sdk`. `RaidRoom` sets `patchRate = 0` after `setTimestep` and sends each patch itself after the step. The scene reads `room.state` every frame; there are no per-field callbacks.
- Schema uses the decorator-free `schema({ ... }, "Name")` + `t.*` builders. Give primitives `.default(...)`. No decorators anywhere.
- Only the server imports `@raid/shared/schema` at runtime; the client uses `import type` from it (it decodes by reflection).
- Tunables live in `shared/src/constants.ts`, the catalogs (`weapons.ts`, `factions.ts`, the buff rows in `buffs.ts`, `cosmetics.ts`) and the signature numbers in `signatures.ts`. Mob scale, leash, the buff S-curve and grants, the lifesteal cap, the reward curve and the planning numbers are in `constants.ts`. Loot weights are in `server/src/sim/lootTables.ts`. Units: world units (1 tile = `TILE` = 32), seconds for durations unless a name ends in `Ms`, server time in ms.
- Weapon numbers live in `shared/src/weapons.ts`: the v3 faction × weapon formula (a weapon is a % boost on the faction's rate; `attackInterval`, `damagePerHit`, `backlashPerSwing`). It must keep matching `testing/realtime.py`: `python3 testing/weapon_reference.py` prints the reference tables, pasted into `shared/test/weapons.test.ts` and `server/src/harness/parity.ts` when the design numbers change. `DUEL_PACE` (2) multiplies every faction damage number (not hp; Sentenced's mark ramp by k²) through `pacedFaction`. Sim combat reads the paced sheet through `factionOf` (`sim/combatant.ts`), never the raw one.
- Design rules the sheet left open are recorded in `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` 9.4. Change them there and in the tests together.
- No `Math.random` in shared code. No enums or decorators (keeps tsx/Vite/TS7 happy).
- Keep comments sparse; explain *why*, not *what*.

Identity and persistence
- No rate limits beyond two narrow ones. Matchmaking verifies the profile token before creating a room or reserving a seat (the static `RaidRoom.onAuth`), and refuses a profile that is already active or opened a still-unjoined room in the last 5 s. A target order out of sight costs at most one path search per `TARGET_REPATH_MS`. In-match orders are otherwise unlimited (up to `MAX_ACTIONS_PER_TICK`): a scripted client sending `stop` then `target` every tick starts and cancels a windup each tick, which broadcasts about 40 small fx a second (one windup and one `cancel`) to the room. It's bounded and cheaper than move spam (an A* search per tick), but both want a per-player order budget. Profile creation is free: any client can `GET /api/profile/<new name>` as often as it likes, `/api` answers every origin (`Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *`), and each claim adds a profile and a file rewrite. The shop and equip endpoints have no limit either. Training rooms have only the one-active-room guard per profile, and a running room broadcasts a patch at 20 Hz until its player leaves or idles out, so many throwaway profiles can hold many rooms.
- Profiles are weakly authenticated: a random token in `localStorage`, stored in plain text in the profile file, no passwords, recovery or expiry, and no TLS. Clearing site data forfeits the profile.
- GV banks when a player's run resolves. A graceful shutdown (SIGTERM/SIGINT, uncaught exception) pays the GV earned so far (`bankVoided`). A hard kill (SIGKILL, power loss, OOM) mid-run loses it. - `tieRank` (`sim/world.ts`, the target choice between equal hits) is keyed on the same secret `orderSalt` as `actionOrder`. Mobs
- Strength and spacing were measured with one scripted player per duel (`bench/mobDuel.ts`) and, since R4a, per camp (`bench/campDuel.ts`), never with humans or other players. Expect playtests to move `MOB_K` (0.45), `ELITE_K` (0.65) and `MOB_LEASH_CAP` (12 tiles; the 9-tile `MOB_LEASH_RADIUS` is only the fallback). `mobDuel` fights on a hand-built floor, so its mob keeps that 9-tile circle (the nearest what-if for a generated camp is `--leash 336`, the cap `--leash 384`); `campDuel` gives its camp a disc territory (about the median one, 330 tiles). A player who stands and fights (`--style stand`) finishes a normal mob in 24 of 25 starter-against-region pairings and an elite in 23 of 25; the default `policy` style, where ranged players back away, loses 5 of 25 normal pairings.
- Known holes: a Frost shield starter cannot finish a Frost shield mob (both block), so the second weapon is the answer (owner decision pending). A chasing mob's swing hits every standing player in its arc, so a player can drag an elite onto someone who never touched it (the leash limits it). A wand mob cannot hit a player standing on top of it (a ball that starts inside a body only hits if it heads into it). A retreating shooter drags a melee mob to its leash, where it evades and heals, so a ranged starter must burst a melee mob down inside one round.

Roguelike overhaul (docs/ROGUELIKE_PLAN.md; R0, R1/R2, R3 and R4a are built; zone terrain, R4b and R5 on are not)
- The status runtime (timed statuses, silence, cleanse, invulnerability, displacement, areas) and the ability framework belong to the Wishes branch (`claude/wishes-349ec2`; owner ruling, plan D21 reversed). `StatusKind` and `TriggerAction` in `shared/effects.ts` are placeholders that mirror its unions: when it merges, replace them with imports (`index.ts` re-exports both modules, so two declarations would collide), register its attack slow as a `ModSource` and have its applied/removed hook call `recomputeMods`. Until then the four `onHit` rows (Chain Warden, Bloatfly, Rot Monk, Frost Wisp) and the two `vs` tests (Inquisitor's streak, Glacial Warden's slowed) are catalog data with `needs: "status"`: shown, never applied (R4b). `hitLanded` has no consumer, and a trigger's `status`, `cleanse` and `cooldown` actions do nothing.
- Mobs have no synced mirrors (`MobState` has no `asMult`, `windupMult` or `speedMult`): what a mod source gives a mob stays server-side, so once mobs can be slowed (R4b) a client would draw a slowed mob's bow pull at the unslowed pace. R4b adds them.
- Zone terrain is not built: `ZONE_STYLE_HOOK` ships null, so the five zones differ by tint only (no LOW tiles, terrain or props), and zone terrain's session fills the hook. `styleRng` is a placeholder for its `hashSeed(seed, attempt, zoneIndex, stream)` (shared/rng.ts): the swap is one line and changes every styled map (re-pin the maps then).
- Camps are half-built: `CampState.reward` and `.state` stay "" and 0 until R5, so a cleared camp gives only its mobs' drops, and nothing reads `CampDef.chest` yet.
- Camp-mates still wake one at a time (`mobAI.aggro`), so a mono-type camp can be pulled one mob at a time; the plan's D24 (hurting one wakes the camp) is the owner's call for R5, and `bench/campDuel.ts` says it matters (the R4a run, 3 seeds, a starter on its signature weapon against 3 normals on open floor): pulled singly, 18 of 25 faction × zone cells are cleared in every seed; with the whole camp awake, 9. The elite camp (the elite and 2 normals): 16 and 7. Some pairings fail even singly (Fire's axe against the Frost and Holy zones, Sentenced's bow against Fire, Frost's shield and Holy's wand against Sentenced and Frost). Generated camps hold 3 to 5 mobs, about 4 (R2 re-run, 75 runs, report only): a starter clears an all-awake camp of 4 in 8 of 75 runs (24 of 75 at 3 mobs), so camp-wide aggro would make group pulls very hard until the mobs are weaker; the owner wants weaker mobs (`MOB_K`) and that tuning is pending on the R5 branch.
- The deal on zone maps (a pool of 24 to 32, 200 maps): for 2 to 8 players every dealt spawn lies in one walking tier on 100% of maps, with no same-zone pair and no spawn dealt twice; a deal takes under 0.9 ms and `spawnWalks` 3.5 ms per map. (The wedge maps' pool of 15 gave 95.8% at 8 players and one shared spawn on every map.)
- A deal reaches a client only as `plan` messages, resent only when its content changes or on a reconnect, and nothing acknowledges one: a message lost before the client's handler existed (the last joiner's deal goes out one tick after it joins) would stay lost. The client registers the handler in `enterRoom` right after the join and holds the newest message, and a player with no deal still presses Ready and is given a spawn, so it has not shown up; a bot presses ready after `PLAN_WAIT_MS` (1 s) without one.
- One buff is weaker than one stat it replaces: mostly +4% to +30% at the quoted value (3 stacks, after the owner's "double them" ruling), plus +20 armor or +16 and +20 magic resist, and about 2.1 times that at the plateau, against +67% max hp, +48% attack speed and +29% move speed (a build stacks several buffs). `ELITE_K`, the feed curve, the catch-up and the bounty's half-the-stacks were tuned to the old magnitudes, and the owner retunes. The plan's second R4a report gate, "power at minute N, old against new", was produced as a separate read-only report and is not in the repo.
- Training has no build console: nothing can grant a buff there (`grantBuff` is what the plan's console would call).
