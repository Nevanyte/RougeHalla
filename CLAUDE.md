# CLAUDE.md — RougeHalla (package name `raid-br`)

Top-down 2.5D roguelike battle arena. Up to 8 players pick a faction, loot, farm mobs and fight. The last one standing wins Glorious Victory (GV, cosmetics-only). Phaser 4 client, Colyseus 0.18 authoritative server, npm workspaces monorepo. Colored shapes only. Design and numbers: `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md`. Running and playing: `README.md`. Faction ids are `fire`, `sentenced`, `plague`, `frost`, `holy` (shown to players as Demon Blood). Weapon ids are `sword`, `bow`, `wand`, `shield`, `axe` (the five loop weapons, `WEAPON_IDS`: the only weapons that are items) and `fists` (what an empty hand fights with: every run starts on it; `ALL_WEAPON_IDS` has all six). The roguelike overhaul (zones, typed camps, contested chests, cards, relics) is planned in `docs/ROGUELIKE_PLAN.md`; built so far: R0 (contracts), R1/R2 (the zone map generator at 96x96, the map pool and its consumers), R3 (the planning phase and screen), R4a's mono-type camps and R5 (the camp runtime: camp-wide aggro, a contested shared chest per cleared camp, the training room's practice console). Zone terrain (collision classes, hazards, the five zone stylers, the zone art) is wired into the generator's hook (ZT-6): every map the game builds is themed (`MAP_STYLERS` on). **The balance pass** (branch `claude/balance-pass`, work in progress; "Status after the balance pass" in the plan) replaced R4a's per-mob-type buffs and R5's 21 passives: a mob kill gives GV, feed and the mob's weapon and never a stat, and all stat power is one of 75 cards from camp chests and quests (`shared/src/cards.ts`), built on AD/AP stats and weapon scaling, with the PvP factor `pvpFactor(P)` scaling player-to-player damage by card power and the guards G1-G13 bounding what a stack can do. It also made fists the start weapon, compressed the faction sheet (typed signatures), added 30 mob types with roles, bag-based rosters, the camp-clear heal and the weapon specials. Active skills (Q/E), relics, curses, boons and "?" camps are not built (the rules below say which). K1 quests are built: ten standing offers, five known in planning and five privately revealed, each paying a card and GV from a capped quest-GV pot (`docs/quests.md`). Each faction's Wish (`D`: `freeze`, `swarm`, `trial`, `blaze`, `lastStand`; GAME_DESIGN 8.8) and the status runtime and ability framework under it (8.9) are built.

Server env overrides (see `packages/server/src/config.ts`): `RAID_PORT`, `STASH_FILE` (the profile file; the name predates profiles v2), `MIN_PLAYERS`, `LOBBY_SECONDS`, `FILL_SECONDS`, `PATIENCE_SECONDS` (at 8 alive), `PATIENCE_MIN_SECONDS` (at ≤3 alive; default 40% of `PATIENCE_SECONDS`), `SUDDEN_DEATH_SECONDS`, `PLAN_SECONDS` (the planning phase after the lobby, 12; 0 skips it), `FIRST_PATIENCE_SECONDS` (the first patience timer of a run, flat, 300, or `PATIENCE_SECONDS` when only that one is set; 0 = it scales with the players like the later ones), `TRAINING_IDLE_SECONDS`, `WISH_LOCK_SECONDS` (default 45: how long every Wish stays locked after the run starts; the e2e uses 5), `COLYSEUS_LATENCY` (simulated minimum RTT in ms), `NET_JITTER_MS`, `NET_LOSS_PCT`, `NET_SEED` (the TCP-faithful model of `server/src/net/netSim.ts`: per connection and direction a FIFO, right-skewed jitter, a lost message stalls everything behind it for a retransmission timeout; core's own `COLYSEUS_LATENCY` handling is switched off because it would stack), `DUMMIES=1` (training dummies and a weapon rack next to every spawn in arena runs too), `MOBS=0` (no regional mobs: they are on by default, one at every camp spot), `QUESTS=0` (no quest offers; on by default in arena runs, never in training), `MAP_POOL` (certified maps the pool keeps ready, 8; 0 = no worker thread, every room certifies its map on the main thread), `BOTS_FILL_TO` (6; 0 turns the fill bots off), `BOTS_ARRIVAL_SECONDS`, `BOTS_TIER_MIX`, `BOTS_PERSONA_MIX`, `BOTS_LATENCY_MS`, `BOTS_JITTER_MS`, `BOT_STATS_FILE` (`off` disables learning) and `BOTS_SEED` (the fill bots, `docs/BOTS.md` 7.3). Hosting (a public link; `CLIENT_DIR` or `NODE_ENV=production` switches `sameOriginOnly` on): `CLIENT_DIR`, `PLAYTEST_PASSCODE`, `RAID_EXIT_ON_STDIN_END` (set by `scripts/playtest.mjs`: the entry block shuts the server down gracefully when its stdin pipe ends, i.e. when the launcher died), `CORS_ORIGINS`, `MAX_ARENA_ROOMS`/`MAX_TRAINING_ROOMS`, `MAX_NEW_PROFILES_PER_HOUR`/`MAX_NEW_PROFILES_PER_IP_PER_HOUR`, `SHOP_BURST`/`SHOP_PER_SECOND` (these caps default on only when hosting, 0 = no limit), `ORDER_BURST`/`ORDER_PER_SECOND` (on everywhere). Client: `VITE_SERVER_URL` (unset, a production build talks to its own origin, a dev build to `hostname:2567`). A dev build of the client exposes `window.__raid = { scene, room }`.

## Layout
- `packages/shared` — TS source consumed directly (no build). Contracts and pure logic used by both sides. `index.ts` re-exports everything except the schema.
  - Contracts: `constants.ts` (the tunables), `schema.ts` (Colyseus state: players (`build` holds their cards, id → count; `adBonus`/`apBonus` mirror their AD and AP), mobs, camps (`CampState.type` and `.elite`), containers, the areas in `fields`, the roster; no projectiles; import via `@raid/shared/schema`), `messages.ts` (client inputs, `FxEvent`, the private `PlanMessage`, results, profile/shop/leaderboard DTOs), `fxCodec.ts` (the compact `fxs` wire form).
  - Statuses and abilities (Wishes): `status.ts` (`StatusKind`/`STATUS_KINDS` in wire order, `shred` the 14th, `STATUS_RULES`: the only table of caps, max times, stacking and cleanse classes, and the `EFFECT_*` bits of `PlayerState.effects`), `abilities.ts` (`AbilityDef`/`AbilityRank`/`AbilityEffect`, `ABILITIES`, `WISHES`, the Wish numbers, `abilityInSlot`, `slotReadyAt`/`setSlotReadyAt`, `abilityCooldownMs` the one cooldown funnel, `hasteMultiplier`, `FIELD_KINDS`), `blink.ts` (`blinkLanding`: the pure landing rule that the server's cast and the client's prediction both run).
  - Combat rules: `factions.ts` (the faction sheet (compressed by the balance pass), damage law, `BEATS`; `bonusParts` splits a signature into its typed parts), `weapons.ts` (the catalog with fists, `WEAPON_IDS` (the loop) and `ALL_WEAPON_IDS`/`FISTS`, `WeaponSpec.scaling` (what AD, AP, bonus max hp and bonus defense add to a hit), `onHitPower` and `special` (the axe's shred, the wand's splash), the faction × weapon formula, `scalingDamage`, timings, `WEAPON_BEATS`), `interactions.ts` (shield, stagger and ball-vs-arrow rules), `signatures.ts` (real-time signature numbers, chill, matchup score, damage flash) with `signatureShares.ts` (`SIGNATURE_SHARES`: the damage type of each signature, a leaf that `signatures.ts` re-exports), `policies.ts` (how to play each weapon, fists included: bots, harness, mobs, and `lockSpacing` for the target lock).
  - Arena rules: `rules.ts` (feed, bounty, patience and sudden-death formulas, the mob reward curve and catch-up, `lifestealShare`, the camp-clear heal `campHealAmount`, the PvP factor `pvpFactor` and a Race to 0's `hpRaceMult`), `cards.ts` (the 75-card catalog: 24 base stat cards (eight stats × three tiers, which stack), 11 conditionals (tier 2, each `requires` a base stat) and 40 titled augments; `CardDef`, `MechanicTag`, `cardPower`, `ownedCards`, `whyOffered`, `cardBlock`), `glossary.ts` (the wording of the tags and stats: tooltips, chips and the help overlay's Mechanics page), `effectText.ts` (an effect row in words), `hpView.ts` (the HUD's hp and barrier maths), `mobs.ts` (the regional mob catalog: `mobFaction` = region faction scaled by `MOB_K`/`ELITE_K`, its execute scaled by k once more (k²); `mobTypeFaction`/`mobTypeWeapon` put a type's role and weapon on top (`MOB_ROLE_DEFS`: grunt, swarm, brute, skirmisher, caster); the territory leash: `territoryAt`, `territoryLeash`, `leashOk`; `MOB_TYPES`, the 30 mob types, six per zone (four normal types and two elites, each with a role and a weapon), with `mobTypesOf`/`normalTypesOf`/`elitesOf`/`mobTypeById`/`mobTypeIn`; `mobName` is only the name of a mob placed with no type), `rosters.ts` (camp rosters, pure: `rollZoneTypes`/`rollCampTypes` shuffle each zone's bag from the stream the caller hands in, `rollCampElites` and `campMobs` say what stands at each spot), `planning.ts` (the planning maths the server's deal, the bots and the client share: `spawnWalks`, `sameTier`, `nearestCamps`, `matchupShare`/`campMatchup`, `walkSeconds`).
  - Quests (K1): `quests.ts` (the 20-def catalog, each def paying a base card (`reward.card`: `questTier`, `questRewardCard`, `questRewardText`) and GV, roster, map-contract placement (`placeQuests`: two passes, known offers then hidden ones; `questHomeComponent`, one cached `moveComponents` flood per map, is the reachability rule) and time-parity maths), with `QuestState` and the visible quest map in `schema.ts` and the private reveal message in `messages.ts`. Unknown offers are server runtime, never `known: false` state. `docs/quests.md` is the rule and balance reference; `docs/quests-bot-hook.md` is the interface-only bot contract.
  - Roguelike spine (R0, wired by R4a, grown by the balance pass): `effects.ts` (the closed `Effect` union that cards, relics and curses are rows of: `stat`, `flat` (armor, shield, `ad`, `ap`), `vs`, `scale`, `while`, `onHit`, `trigger`, `convert`, `mult`, `flag`; the 16 `EFFECT_STATS`; an exhaustive `visitEffect`; `needs: "status"` is still in the type and no card carries it; and `TriggerAction`, whose `status` and `cleanse` verbs are `AbilityEffect`'s (D28) and whose `StatusKind` comes from `status.ts`), `mods.ts` (the stat mirrors' quantized wire form and decoders, `scaleWindup`/`mirrorTimings`, `foldRows`: the whole fold the sim and the tests share, and the guards' maths).
  - Camps, chests and cards (R5, cards by the balance pass): `rewards.ts` (`rollChestOptions`: three cards by `CHEST_TIER_ODDS`, `cardOptionView` for the panel, the skip's numbers, `CHEST_REFUSAL`), `training.ts` (the practice console's commands and caps, `TRAIN_*`). The card catalog itself is `cards.ts` (Arena rules above; `skills.ts`, the 21 passives, is gone).

## Architecture rules (do not break)
- **Authoritative server.** Clients send inputs only (`move`, `stop`, `target`, `aim`, `attack`, `release`, `swap`, `pickup`, `plan`, `cast`, `choose` (a pick at a camp's shared chest), and `train` (the practice console, accepted in a training room only): `CLIENT_MESSAGE_TYPES`). The game client sends `target` and `cast` and no longer sends `attack` or `release`; they are the low-level trigger that mobs and the weapon harness still use (the bots click their foe, like the game client). Never accept positions, damage, or outcomes from a client. Apart from a walk target, the numbers a client supplies are aim angles, a render time `t` (clamped to `MAX_REWIND_MS`, only on `attack`/`release` and `cast`; a Wish never rewinds, so a cast's `t` only sets `CastRuntime.lagMs`, which nothing reads yet), a slot (a weapon slot, or an ability slot `wish`/`q`/`e` on `cast`), a container id, a fighter id (`target`, and a `cast`'s unit target `id`), the cursor's ground point `x, y` on a `cast` (always sent, clamped like a `move`'s point: a point or direction for the ability, ignored by a self cast), an option index 0 or 1 and a ready flag (`plan`), a pick index (`choose`: -1 skips; `train` adds catalog ids and a copy count, in training rooms only) and an order number `seq` (an integer 0..255 on `move`/`stop`/`cast`; a bad one is dropped and the order still counts). `step.ts` `applyMove` copies a player's `seq` into the synced `PlayerState.moveSeq` on the tick it applies the order, and `handleCast` does the same for a `cast` that carries one, accepted or refused (a refused blink is then un-predicted at its ack): nothing reads it but the client, which learns from it when its order took effect (the prediction's delay, `net/localMotion.ts`).
- **Fixed 20 Hz tick.** Message handlers validate and enqueue; they never mutate game state. `enqueueClientMessage` returns false for a rejected message, and only an accepted one counts as presence (training's idle limit). The sim consumes input queues at the start of each fixed 50 ms step.
- **Sim is pure.** `server/src/sim/*` must not import `@colyseus/core` or touch clients/sockets. It mutates the schema state and a non-synced runtime struct, and returns events. The Room converts events into messages and persistence. Test the sim headless.
- **Tick order is fixed** (`sim/step.ts`; do not reorder): inputs (players in the shuffled `actionOrder`, then mobs) → abilities (`updateAbilities`: statuses and areas expire, every cast due lands, a Trial zone ends when its subject leaves it) → target locks (`updateTargets`) → weapon state machines → `afterWeapons` → movement → history → projectile sweep (present time, 10 ms substeps) → spawns → melee (rewound) → `beforeDamage` (signature damage, then the terrain's ember burn, then status damage: trail contacts and `dot` ticks) → sudden death's burn (recorded into the ledger) → ledger (status changes made inside it wait for its end; it opens with the entries cards queued last tick, `World.combat.deferred`) → systems → settle. The abilities phase was ADDED by the Wishes; nothing was reordered. It runs before the locks so a fighter that just became untargetable loses its locks in the same tick. Within `systems`, the quest system runs after mob AI and before the final `updateMods`, so reveal, expiry and turn-in happen after the ledger and its deferred payouts without reordering combat. That is the run's tick (`active`); the lobby and planning ticks are separate (next rule).
- **Planning phase** (`sim/planning.ts`, `step.ts`). With `SimConfig.planSeconds` above 0 (the live server's `PLAN_SECONDS`) a closing lobby goes `planning` → `active`; unset or 0 keeps today's flow exactly, training forces 0, and a room test that wants that flow sets `planSeconds: 0` and `firstPatienceSeconds: 0` (`loadConfig({})` now plans). The Room locks at planning start (joins are refused from then on), `RaidState.planEndsAt` is set and every player is dealt two spawns from `map.spawnPool`. A planning tick drains every player's inputs in the tick's secret `actionOrder` and applies only `plan` `{ spawn?: 0|1, boon?: 0|1, ready?: boolean }`, an index into the player's OWN two options, never a pool index or a position; everything else is dropped (a `plan` in any other phase too), nobody moves or is hurt, and no mob exists yet. The five known quest offers have `QuestState` rows during planning, but their giver mobs and item containers are created only at `raidStarted`. The first to pick a spawn holds it, and within one tick the secret order decides, never the join order; picking the other option frees the first; `ready: true` latches (`PlayerState.planReady`) and freezes the picks. It ends at `planEndsAt` or once every CONNECTED player is ready (a dropped one does not hold it up): `assignSpawns` places everyone (a pick stands; the rest take their first free option, then the other, then the free spawn nearest in walk) and `startRaid` runs as ever. **The first patience timer** is `firstPatienceSeconds` flat (live `FIRST_PATIENCE_SECONDS` 300; unset or 0 = the usual scaling), armed there, after planning; later timers scale. **Deals are private**: drawn only from `world.rewardRandom` (never `world.random` or the public seed), kept in `world.planning`, and sent to a player only as their own `plan` message (`PlanMessage`: `endsAt`, `spawns` as pool indexes, `taken` = held by ANOTHER player's pick, `pick`, `boons` (empty until R7), `boon`) when planning starts, on every change to it and on a reconnect (`RaidRoom.sendPlan` → `planFor`); the synced state holds `planEndsAt` and `planReady` and nothing else (a test compares two differently dealt worlds). The two spawns of a pair lie in one walking tier (within `PLAN_WALK_TOLERANCE` 8% of a shared value, `sameTier`) and in different zones, and no spawn is dealt twice while 2 × players ≤ the pool; when tier pairs run out the deal keeps as many as it can and fills with the closest walks in different zones. A dropped player KEEPS the seat (`allowReconnection`, as in the run, not the lobby: the room is locked, so a blip would cost the whole run) and gets the default spawn without a pick. **Leaving during planning is MIA** (owner ruling: planning is part of the run, so nobody can see the deal and requeue for free; only the lobby is free to leave): a consented leave, or a drop whose reconnect grace ran out, goes through `removePlayer` → `missInPlanning` (`sim/arena.ts`): a `resolved` result with outcome `mia`, `place` = the players still in the room, 0 GV, which `recordRun` counts as a run and a death (`runs + 1`, `deaths + 1`); no body, no kill credit, no settle. `leavePlanning` then frees what they held. The leaver stays a STARTER (`PlanningRuntime.starters`, set at planning start, feeds `startedWith` in `beginRun`), so the win-bonus gate counts them; with one player left in a room that started planning with two or more (`lastOneStanding`) the run starts at once and the next tick's settle makes that player the winner with the bonus withheld (nobody was damaged). A shutdown during planning voids the run instead (`RaidRoom.onLeave` while `shuttingDown` → `abandonPlayer` + `bankVoided`: no result, nothing recorded). The void is sticky: `abandonPlayer` sets `world.arena.voided` in planning as in the run, and `updatePlanning` returns at once while it is set, so a tick between two abandons can never start the run (`lastOneStanding`) and hand the last player a win; only `beginRun` clears the flag, and a voided room never reaches it. `fighterOut "removed"` fires for every planning leave, and the Wishes' cleanup tolerates a fighter with no statuses, cast or `world.abilities`.
- **Quests are standing offers with a private scouting boundary** (`shared/quests.ts`, `sim/quests.ts`; `docs/quests.md`). The roster is ten defs (five known) rolled when planning starts from `world.questRandom`, a separate private stream: `createWorld` seeds it from its own `questSalt` option, four more crypto-random words that `RaidRoom` takes from `crypto.randomBytes`, independent of `rewardSalt` (the mixed derivation `questSaltFrom` in `sim/world.ts` is only the fallback for a world built without one: tests, the harness, questBench). Quest code never draws from `world.rewardRandom`, `world.random` or the public map seed. Quest setup draws nothing when quests are off, in training, or on a map with no camps, so those paths leave both existing streams untouched. Placement (`placeQuests`) is two passes: the known offers first, then the hidden ones in their own random pass (`PlaceOptions.hiddenRandom` is a test option) with a gap to the known givers and a clearance of `QUEST_REVEAL_RADIUS` + 2 tiles from every spawn-pool start (the reveal is a plain distance test, so a hidden giver nearer a start would reveal itself to whoever spawns there on the first tick; `QUEST_HIDDEN_START_CLEARANCES` steps down 14, 12, 6, 0 tiles only when fewer than two spots per hidden offer remain, which no generated map of seeds 1..150 needed; the known givers keep only the 8-tile start clearance), and the returned offers come out in a private shuffle that gives the quest ids, so known positions and public ids do not determine the hidden set. Known offers are `QuestState` rows on the planning screen; no giver or item exists until `raidStarted`, no quest system ticks during planning, and active deadlines are anchored there. Unknown offers, their givers and their items remain ONLY in server runtime until publication; synced state carries only a numeric rumour count (`unknownQuests`). The standing player in `QUEST_REVEAL_RADIUS` with the lowest `tieRank` is the finder, never the first in join order and never a fill bot. Only that client gets the private preview, then the offer becomes public four seconds later; this reserves no progress. No reveal starts once `gatesLocked` is set.
- Every offer is global and per-player progress is public; the first ready player within turn-in range and line of sight closes it, with same-tick ties decided by `tieRank`. Givers are neutral kind `quest` mobs guarded out of target lock, mob AI, melee, projectiles, Wish targeting/effects, camp waking, damage and statuses. Tokens and parcels use ordinary `pickup`; there is no accept/complete quest input. `pickup { containerId }` acts on exactly the id sent (the server never chooses between containers: `sim/loot.ts` `handlePickup`, 56 u of the server position with a move-mask line, not stunned or casting) and refuses a quest item, leaving it in place, when its quest is not open and published, when the player already carries any parcel, when the parcel is carried by someone else, or (tokens) when the player's progress is at the count; the client's F pick (`render/interact.ts`) mirrors those cases and never offers an item the server would refuse. `mobKilled` supplies `{ type, zone, camp, elite }` (`camp` is the index in `map.camps`, -1 for a camp-less mob; a console practice mob never reaches it: practice kills pay nothing) to chained objective hooks, from `payMobKill` after the kill's spoils; a killer who is not standing then gets no progress. Region culls count any mob of their zone, an elite included. Headhunter counts a victim outcome of `died`, never `mia`, and neither its killer nor its victim may be a fill bot. Fill bots take no part in quests (docs/BOTS.md: a bot's death pays no GV, bots touch no profile or GV, and a bot winning would take the offer from a human): they get no kill, token or parcel progress, cannot pick a quest item up, turn in or win, are never a hidden giver's finder and do not count as someone who could still finish a kill offer. A player carries one parcel; death drops it, Swarm and Blaze Step keep it, and Swarm never hides the parcel or its exact carrier marker. Completion pays the GV first (`awardQuestGv`) and then the card: `grantCard` of `questRewardCard(def, feed)`, the def's `reward.card`, a base card whose tier is the quest's time in camps (`questTier`: `(objSec + TRAVEL_RATE_SHARE × travelSec) / CAMP_SECONDS × CARD_TIER_RATE × STACK_PREMIUM`, rounded into 1-3; the player-kill quest is capped at tier I), one tier lower (never under I) for a player whose mob reward curve is under `QUEST_FEED_BRAKE` (the catch-up does not count), with the toast "Quest complete: <Card title>". Quest GV uses its own per-run pot capped at `QUEST_GV_CAP = 24`, separate from `GV_MOB_CAP`, and has its own results row; an exhausted pot still pays the card.
- **Quest lifecycle.** `retire` is the one closing step for completion and every expiry: it clears the carrier link, the ground items and the giver and clamps `expiresAt` to the close time. A published row then stays in synced state for 20 s as `done` or `expired` (a row is `open`, `done` or `expired`) and `questSystem` deletes it, but only while the run is `active`; an offer that never went public is deleted at once. `campCleared` does NOT close anything itself: it sets `QuestsRuntime.recheck` (it runs before the clearing kill's own `mobKilled`), and `questSystem` evaluates the flag after the ledger pass (`closeImpossible`/`killSupplyLeft`): an open kill offer closes only when no STANDING player either has the whole objective already or has enough live mobs of its zone (any elite, or unvisited zones for the Grand Tour) left to finish it; hidden ones are skipped (deleting one at a camp clear would drop the public `unknownQuests` count and show that a hidden offer of that zone existed) and checked once, for that offer only, when they go public, where they close like any public offer. `teleported` and `runEnded` close every open offer without pay: published rows become `expired`, unpublished offers are deleted, and all givers, tokens, ground or carried parcels and carrier links are removed. Thus a parcel never survives the coliseum teleport. Announcements: expiries of one tick are batched into one line ("N quests expired"), the teleport announces once, the run's end is silent. A hidden offer closes unseen only at its own deadline, the teleport or the run's end, and that drops the public `unknownQuests` count (publication lowers it too; a camp clear never does). A deadline that only one def has (165, 220 or 280 s) names that def; no drop says where an offer stood. That is the same leak class as a timed expiry, known and accepted. K1 keeps fixed counts and expiries, no per-player completion cap or carrier farm lockout, and exact carrier minimap positions. Training never installs quests; J still opens there to explain that practice has no quests.
- **Quest placement and UI on R2.** Placement reads only the map contract (`camps`, `spawnPool`, `regions[].pole`, the coliseum, terrain and reachability) and assumes no zone shape, camp count or map size; it never changes map generation. Reachability is the coliseum centre's movement component (`questHomeComponent`/`moveComponents`, one cached flood per map; it falls back to the spawn pool's most-voted component on a hand-built map whose centre is a wall, then to no restriction). Givers use clear plain floor in it outside the coliseum, three tiles beyond camp clearings and from every camp chest and mob rest spot, off `TILE_LOW` and hazard terrain; tokens and parcels reject hazard floor, a one-tile halo round chests and a two-tile halo round rest spots, stay in the giver's component, and never share a tile with each other or a giver. Hidden givers also stay `QUEST_REVEAL_RADIUS` + 2 tiles from every spawn-pool start (0 of 750 on seeds 1..150). Cost: `placeQuests` 0.8 ms median and 7.4 ms max over seeds 1..30 (`spawnQuests` end to end 0.9 and 3.9 ms; the first version's path search per candidate was 48.5 and 76 ms); an audit of 1500 offers on seeds 1..150 dropped none and found no unreachable, hazard, `TILE_LOW`, clearing, chest-or-spot or shared-tile case. J opens in planning and in the active phase (training included), is placed live below the measured HUD plate (`ui/questLogLayout.ts`) and ignores J while typing or in a `[role=dialog]`. Log rows group by status (open, done, expired) and stop timers when closed; closed offers have no live giver drawing. The server sends one public completion line and batches same-tick expiries, and the client folds them again (`ui/questToasts.ts`). Previews are dropped at the teleport and the run's end too, and the rumour count excludes a revealed preview. F never offers a quest item the server would refuse. Numbers for the current build are in `docs/quests.md`.
- **The ledger: one way down, one place to settle.** Every hit (projectile sweep, melee, signature damage, a card's damage) is recorded with `recordHit` (`sim/ledger.ts`) and applied all at once by `applyLedger`. Each hit is multiplied by its attacker's outgoing multiplier (next rule), mitigated per victim, capped (below), then goes to `applyHits` for a player (`sim/arena.ts`), `hurtMob` for a regional mob or `damageMob` for a dummy. Sudden death's burn is a ledger entry too (`suddenDeath`, recorded after `beforeDamage`: all true damage, from `SUDDEN_DEATH_KILLER`, no `hit` fx, no kill credit of its own), so it lands in the same pass as the tick's hits. Every exit from the fight (death, leave) goes through `downPlayer`. In-tick results come only from `settle()` at the end of the tick, so the shuffled input order never decides a winner. A fighter killed this tick still lands what it started. Simultaneous hits on one victim land together, and the kill goes to the largest rival hit. Simultaneous last deaths go to the higher hp fraction at tick start (an exact tie draws). **Entries from the cards.** Damage that answers a pass (Spiked Shell's returned damage, Overkill's splash) cannot join the pass it answers, whose victims and multipliers were fixed when it began: `deferHit` queues it in `World.combat.deferred` and the NEXT tick's ledger starts with it. An entry can carry `final` (its packet already holds every multiplier, the PvP factor included: the ledger only mitigates it, and applies the Race to 0 its victim holds), `derived` (a follow-on of another hit, a wand ball's splash or Overkill's: it sets off no on-hit effect, streak, Wishful Thinking or trigger, so a card's damage never answers itself) and `passive` (damage returned to an attacker). A packet carries `crit` (a critical strike: `raw` already holds `CRIT_MULT`, and the `hit` fx says so) and `mult` (the multipliers baked into `raw` beyond the fighter's own `hit` mult, which G1's cap counts). **The PvP caps** (`capPvp`, after mitigation, between two players only): no hit that is not damage over time takes more than `PVP_HIT_CAP_SHARE` of the victim's max hp (G2), and one source's damage over time on one victim no more than `PVP_DOT_CAP_SHARE` of it per second (G3, a budget per tick); the world's damage (the arena, mobs) is never capped. `SimConfig.pvpRules: false` switches the factor and the caps off for a test or a bench that checks the damage law's own arithmetic (a run never does).
- **The outgoing multiplier is fixed at the start of the pass.** `applyLedger` computes every entry's multiplier (`outgoingMults`) before any victim takes damage, from the hp as the pass began, so the order victims are processed in never changes it, and applies it to the entry's raw damage before `mitigateShares`, together with the status runtime's `damageFactor` (Trial's zone; the status table is frozen during the pass, so it too reads the same for every entry). The card part (`outgoingMult`, `sim/mods.ts`) is 1 + the attacker's damage sum + every `vs` row whose test holds against the victim + every `scale` row's bonus: rows within the damage key ADD (two +5% rows are +10%). G1 caps it: that row multiplier times the entry's own `hit`, `dot` or `returned` mult (`outgoingKindOf`) and the multiplier already baked into the packet (a crit, Plant Your Feet) never passes `OUTGOING_ROW_CAP`, so a stack of damage rows gives way to Here Comes the Boom's ×2.5 instead of passing it. Per entry, on top of that: **the PvP factor** (`pvpScale`: `pvpFactor(cardPower(attacker's build))` for an entry between two players of the run, never a mob, a dummy, a Wish or the arena, times the harness's `duelScale`; the PvP rule below); **a Race to 0** (G4: ×1 / hp fraction as the pass began, up to the card's ceiling, on the damage its holder deals AND on the damage it takes, so a `final` entry still takes it; the extra it adds feeds no lifesteal, whoever dealt the hit: a landed hit carries the dealer's and the victim's Race multipliers together as `race`, and lifesteal reads `amount / race`); and **Gambler's roll** (a hash, outside the cap). It covers the attacker's weapon hits and its signature damage, damage-over-time and the mark included (that is how a damage row reaches Sentenced, whose damage is nearly all its mark), and NEVER an ability entry (`e.ability` set: a Wish's damage and its `dot` chunks keep a multiplier of 1; flat power, see Statuses, abilities and areas; owner ruling "Keep Wishes flat") or the arena's burn; mobs have no rows. `vs` tests `hpAbove`/`hpBelow`/`playerAbove` read the victim's hp fraction, `higherFeed` and `thirdParty` the ledger's `vsProbe`, `slowed`, `elite`, `higherPower`, `player`, `rooted` and `streak` `sim/vsProbe.ts`, all from the state as the pass began. A `scale` row adds its `per` for every WHOLE `step` of its source (`ownMissingHp` in 10% steps, the camps the owner helped clear, the hits in a row on the target), up to its `cap`.
- **Spoils are paid after the pass.** While `applyLedger` runs (`world.combat.collecting`), a kill's GV, bounty, the weapon a mob drops or arms, a trigger's heal and a camp's clear (its fx, its chest and its heal) are queued with `payLater`. They run once every victim has taken their damage, from the lobby median feed as it stood before the pass, so a reward never decides who survives (sudden death's burn is in the pass too). A killer who goes down in the pass keeps the kill and its GV but gets no raised bounty and no rampage announcement. Outside the pass (`applyDamage`, tests) `payLater` runs at once. A mob's kill goes to the player who did it the most damage over the whole fight, not whoever lands the last hit. A mob that finishes a player a rival hit within `KILL_CREDIT_SECONDS` credits that rival (kill GV and bounty), as leaving does.
- **`hitLanded` is a hook, not a rule engine** (`WorldHooks`, called in `applyVictim`, `sim/ledger.ts`; `chainHook` adds to it). It fires once per ledger entry that took damage (mitigated amount above 0, so not sudden death's burn and not a victim that nothing got through to; DoT entries count), after the victim's damage and its `hit` fx, still inside the pass (`world.combat.collecting`, so kill spoils are still queued). It gets `(world, entry, applied)`, `applied` being the damage after armor and shield. Unset it costs nothing, and a hook must never change who survives or what a later entry of the same pass sees, or the ledger's order would matter. Its consumer is `sim/cardHooks.ts`: the on-hit pipeline (`sim/onHit.ts`: the axe's shred, Hemorrhage's bleed, the sword's and fists' on-hit power, Fists of Fury, Third Time's the Charm, Eye for an Eye) and the triggers on a hit TAKEN (Adrenaline, Spiked Shell). It decides from the entry and the memory as the pass began and writes only statuses (`addStatus`, `removeStatuses`, `cleanse` and `displace` defer themselves during the pass, see Statuses, abilities and areas, so what it applies counts from the next tick), `payLater` closures and entries for the next tick's ledger (`deferHit`); the streak and Wishful Thinking read the tick's hits in a system AFTER the pass. Two more chainable hooks serve the cards. **`blocked(world, holder, attacker, prevented, melee)`** (`sim/melee.ts` for a blocked sword or bash, `sim/projectiles.ts` for an arrow the shield stopped or reflected) fires OUTSIDE the pass (the melee and sweep phases), so what it applies counts at once: Tank Engine's tally, Pocket Sand's slow, Best Offense. **`overkilled(world, victim, hits, overkill, total)`** (`applyVictim`, once per killed victim, inside the pass) hears that a kill had damage to spare (what a barrier absorbed is not overkill): Overkill's splash is found after the pass (`payLater`) and queued for the next tick.
- **`hurt` is the hook for a survivor's own hp** (`WorldHooks`, chainable; `applyVictim`, `sim/ledger.ts`). It fires once per victim per pass, after that victim's hits landed together (the arena's burn included) and before their `hit` fx and `hitLanded`, only if they took damage and are STILL standing, with `(world, c, applied, hpBefore)` (`applied` is what reached the pool, the whole hit under a Last Stand floor, so a "crossed the threshold" test reads `hpBefore`, the hp as the pass began, never hp + applied). A handler decides from that fighter's own state alone and defers what it pays with `payLater`, so it can never change who else survives the pass. Blood Saint's and Second Wind's `trigger` rows (`on: "hpBelow"`, `sim/factions.ts`) hang here: a `once` row fires the first time a pass leaves its owner below the threshold, even if it already was when it got the row (`PlayerRuntime.fired`, per run); other rows when a pass takes it across. `heal` with `ignoreLockout` skips the 3 s out-of-combat lockout and nothing else, and with `ms` it is spread over time (`startHealOverTime`); `barrier` grants through `grantBarrier`; under an `hpBelow` row the `status`, `cleanse` and `cooldown` actions do nothing (a `status` runs under `hitTaken` and `block`, a `cooldown` under `hit`: `sim/cardHooks.ts`).
- **Statuses, abilities and areas** (`shared/src/status.ts`, `abilities.ts`, `blink.ts`; `sim/status.ts`, `fields.ts`, `displace.ts`, `abilities.ts`; GAME_DESIGN 8.8 and 8.9).
  - **One status runtime, one table.** `sim/status.ts` is a leaf: it imports only shared, `buffs`, `mods` (a leaf), `combatant` and `world`, and what a status DOES to a cast, a windup or an area is `sim/abilities.ts`, called through the hooks below. `STATUS_RULES` (`shared/status.ts`) is the ONLY place for a kind's max time, cap, stacking (`any`/`sum`/`max`/`product`), cleanse class (`soft`/`hard`/`never`), effect bit and `playersOnly`: never hard-code one elsewhere. An instance is (kind, source id, ability ?? tag): re-applying extends `until` (at most now + `maxMs`) and keeps the larger `value`, or accumulates with `add: {per, cap}`; different instances combine through `statusValue`. Readers ignore an instance past its `until`, `updateAbilities` removes it, and a stale one counts as a fresh application. `addStatus` returns null for a `playersOnly` kind on a mob (`invulnerable`, `hidden`, `deathFloor`) and for a fighter that is not standing. `invulnerable` raises `immuneUntil` (never removed early). The bits live in the synced `effects` (`refreshEffects`, written only on a change; `EFFECT_HIDDEN` only outside the reveal window, `EFFECT_CASTING` while a cast with `castMs > 0` is pending); the statuses themselves, casts and reveal times are server-only. `STATUS_KINDS` (14 kinds: the balance pass appended `shred`, the axe's armor shred, which sums up to `AXE_SHRED_CAP` and has no effect bit), `ABILITY_IDS` and `FIELD_KINDS` are wire orders: append only.
  - **The status table is frozen during the ledger pass** (`world.combat.collecting`). `addStatus`, `removeStatuses`, `cleanse` and `displace` called then queue themselves with `payLater` (they return null or empty) and run once the pass is over, still in the tick and before `systems`, so `damageFactor`, `floorHp` and immunity read one consistent table and the shuffled order decides nothing. `fighterOut` clears wait the same way. A status applied by a hit therefore counts from the next tick's damage, and lifesteal (a system) already sees it.
  - **Hooks** (`WorldHooks`, chainable; `sim/abilities.ts` installs them): `statusApplied(world, c, st, fresh)` (after the bits: a stun cancels the weapon windup, the bow's draw included, its cooldown spent, and a stun, silence or root interrupts a cast whose def lists it; applying a `slowAttack` calls `recomputeMods`, and `removeStatuses` (so a cleanse, an expiry, the teleport's clear) calls it when one is removed, so no weapon reads a slow that is gone), `attackStarted` (`startAttack`: a hidden attacker is revealed), `staggered` (when a stagger takes: interrupts a cast that lists `stagger`; no Wish does), `fighterOut(id, "down" | "removed")` (`downPlayer`, `killMob`, `removePlayer`, `abandonPlayer`) and `runEnded` (`endRun`). The sim reads statuses through guards: `cannotMove` (stunned, rooted or casting a cast with a cast time; `statusMoveMultiplier` is a `world.moveModifiers` entry that returns 0 for it, else `(1 - slowMove) x hasteMultiplier`), `actionBlocked` (stunned or casting: no attack start, swap or pickup; a cast with no cast time lands in its own tick, never counts as casting, and so blocks nothing queued after it), `isStunned` (facing frozen: `applyAim`, `handlePress`, `handleRelease`; `follow` does not chase, aim or fire; a stunned mob's inputs are drained and lost and its AI does not think), `isUntargetable` (see Swarm below). A stunned or rooted player's `move`/`stop`/`target` still apply and are walked or fought afterwards; a shield still blocks while stunned. Attack slow is a `world.modSources` source (`attackSlowSource`: an `asMult` of `1 - slow`), so the stat mirrors stay the one source of truth for attack speed.
  - **The cast pipeline.** A `cast` input becomes an `ActionCommand`; `handleCast` (players only) validates in this order and a refusal changes nothing (no cooldown, no fx) except that a `seq` is echoed in `moveSeq`: the slot holds an ability (`abilityInSlot`: `wish` is the faction's Wish, `q`/`e` are empty until skills), the run is active, the caster stands, the coliseum teleport is not within `CAST_TELEPORT_GRACE_MS` (300 ms: a cast that was aimed at the world before the teleport is refused, and a refusal is free; `world.abilities.teleportedAt`), `castBlocked` (the def's `blockedBy`: Wishes list stun and silence, a root does not stop one), no `rt.cast` already, `now >= slotReadyAt`, then the target (`unit`: `validUnitTarget` within `range + CAST_RANGE_SLACK`, standing, hostile, not evading, not untargetable, not teleport-immune, in line of sight on `collisionMap`; `point` with a blink: `blinkLanding` is not null). Accepting spends the cooldown at once (`abilityCooldownMs`), stores a `CastRuntime` in `rt.cast` (OUTSIDE `PendingAction`: it roots through `castMs` but only its def's `cancelledBy` ends it; Wishes list `stun` and `silence`, so a sword stagger never wastes one), faces the target or point, and, for `castMs > 0` or an `ownOrder`, cancels the caster's weapon windup (`cancelLockedWindup`, else `cancelAction` plus a `cancel` fx); an `ownOrder` (the blink) also drops the lock and the path, and a `dropsLock` (Swarm, a best guess: a locked auto-attack would reveal it at once) drops the lock only (`dropLock` also takes a locked caster's chase with it; a walk the caster ordered itself survives the cast root and goes on after it, so an escape does not stop you). The lock survives any other cast. `updateAbilities` (the phase) expires statuses and areas, then lands every cast due in TWO passes: pass 1 re-validates all of them against the state as it stood when the phase began (a failure is `cancelCast("fizzle")`), the survivors stop casting, then pass 2 applies all their SELF effects and then all their TARGET effects, each in the tick's secret rank, through an exhaustive switch over `AbilityEffect` (a new verb is a compile error until it is handled), so no processing order decides an outcome; then zones, then the bits. **Fizzle costs:** refused at validation is free; accepted and then failing at landing (`castEnd` `fizzle`) or interrupted (`interrupted`) keeps the cooldown spent; the coliseum teleport cancels a cast and REFUNDS it (`prevReadyAt`, `castEnd` `cancelled`); a death, a leave and the run's end cancel without a refund.
  - **Displacement and areas.** `displace(world, c, to)` is the one forced move: it sets the position, resets the rewind history to `to` (as the teleport does), re-plans a walk in hand from there (dropped if unreachable), bumps `PlayerState`/`MobState.displaceSeq` (uint8, wrapping), and restarts the lock of every player who has locked the fighter ("not seen yet", re-plan at once), so a blink through a wall is chased to, not dropped by the line-of-sight grace (the `TARGET_RANGE` leash still bounds it). Areas are `FieldRuntime`s in `world.abilities.fields` (null until first use: every entry point tolerates that), mirrored ONCE into the synced `state.fields` (`FieldState`) and deleted when they end; the server never updates one (a cloud is drawn at its owner's drawn position, `vx`/`vy` are for a later moving area). Zones and clouds end with their owner, a trail outlives it (as a projectile does), and the teleport and the run's end remove all.
  - **Ability damage is named.** It goes through `recordHit` under the caster (`by`) with `weapon: null`, `ability` and, for damage over time, `dot: true` (`LedgerEntry`, `LandedHit`), so kill credit, the bounty, `lastHit`, mob threat and the mob kill credit work unchanged. `contactsOf` skips it (it never starts or keeps a signature engagement); it feeds lifesteal and the heal lockout's combat clock like signature damage. A `dot` entry makes no `hit` fx of its own: `abilityDotFx` (a system) reports one per victim and source every `DOT_FX_MS`, with the ability code as the fifth tuple element, and the client draws no damage flash for `isAbilityDot`. `statusDamage` (a `beforeDamage` entry) ignites a trail's contacts (anyone standing on it but its owner, not evading and not teleport-immune, gets a `dot` status from the owner) and then records every active `dot` instance as true damage: the generic ticker, so an on-hit burn needs no code of its own. Trial lives in ONE place, `applyVictim`: it multiplies each non-arena entry's raw by `damageFactor(attacker, victim)` before mitigation (a zoned subject's outgoing damage by `dealtMult`; damage to it from the zone's caster by `takenFromSourceMult`; several zones multiply; the arena's burn is exempt; the `zoned` status carries `dealtMult` as its `value`, so `statusValue(zoned)`, a product kind, is what the subject deals now and 1 when it is not zoned). Last Stand lives in `applyHits`: `floorHp` holds hp at `min(floorHp, before)` and adds the whole `total` (after mitigation and the kill barrier, including the overkill it prevented) to the status's `taken`, and `applyHits` then returns that `total` (what reached the pool) instead of the hp removed, so a hit on a fighter already at the floor still staggers it, shows its number, lands in `world.combat.landed` and reaches `hitLanded`; its heal is `healShare` of `taken`, paid at expiry or by the teleport through `heal(..., { ignoreLockout: true })` (an anti-heal still cuts it).
  - **Flat power, one funnel.** No ability number reads cards, AD or AP (the ledger skips ability entries in `outgoingMults`, so a caster's damage, `vs` and `scale` rows never touch a Wish's damage or its DoT ticks; Trial's `damageFactor` still applies): `haste` ADDS `value x PLAYER_SPEED` (`hasteMultiplier(base, bonus) = 1 + bonus / base`), trail damage is true, and neither chill nor attack speed stretches a cast or a cooldown. `abilityCooldownMs` is the only place a cooldown is computed (a `flat` def ignores reduction; a training world gives a Wish `TRAINING_WISH_COOLDOWN_SECONDS`). Trial's ±20% is the one relative number (owner rule), and the cards have two sanctioned exceptions (G13): Arcane Overload's `wishCooldown` mult goes through `abilityCooldownMs`, and Wishful Thinking trims the cooldown (`sim/cardHooks.ts`: at most `WISHFUL_BUDGET_MS` in any `WISHFUL_WINDOW_MS`, never under `WISHFUL_FLOOR_SHARE` of the cooldown cast; `handleCast` records the cast in `wishCast`).
  - **Lifecycle.** Death, leaving and `abandonPlayer` (`fighterOut`, deferred in a pass): `castEnd` `cancelled` with the cooldown spent, every status removed with no heal, the zones and clouds the fighter owns and the zones it is the subject of removed (no stun), its trail stays. The coliseum teleport (`teleported` hook): every cast refunded, every status and area ended, a Last Stand still pays. The run's end (`runEnded`): everything cleared, nothing paid. Expiry: a `hidden` ends its cloud, a `zoned` its zone, a `deathFloor` pays. A dropped connection leaves casts and statuses alone (everything drawable is synced, so a reconnect is right at once). `raidStarted` (`armWishes`) sets `wishReadyAt = raidStartedAt + wishLockSeconds x 1000` (`SimConfig.wishLockSeconds`, env `WISH_LOCK_SECONDS`, default 45; ready at once in training) and clears statuses, casts and areas.
  - **Swarm (the `hidden` status).** It hides on the CLIENT (`PlayerView` draws no remote hidden fighter, the local one at 0.45 alpha; `targetable(effects)` keeps it out of the click pick) and the SERVER makes it untargetable: `lockable` refuses it, locks on it drop in the same tick (`updateAbilities` runs before `updateTargets`), `lockedFoe` never re-aims at it, `validUnitTarget` refuses it, and a mob's `pickTarget` skips it (the threat is kept; with no one else the mob gives up and walks home). Casting it drops the caster's own lock (`dropsLock`, a best guess) but not its walk: its locked auto-attack would otherwise reveal it at once, and a Plague that wants to ambush clicks its target again. Its own attack or cast reveals it from the NEXT tick (`reveal` sets `revealFrom = now + TICK_MS`, so a reveal applied mid-phase by `attackStarted` or `handleCast` never changes what another fighter reads in that tick, whichever joined first) until `landsAt + HIDDEN_REVEAL_MS` (500 ms); being hit does not. A second Swarm while the first is up (training's 5 s cooldown) replaces the first cloud and extends the one status. Projectiles in flight, swings along their start aim, areas and signatures still reach it, and its position stays in the synced state (see the Known TODO on cheating).
- **Target lock** (`sim/targeting.ts`; `setLock`/`dropLock` in `sim/weapons.ts`; synced as `PlayerState.attackTarget`, "" = none). `target {id}` locks an enemy and the server plays the fight from then on, as mob AI does for a mob: each tick after the inputs, `policies.ts` `decide` with `lockSpacing` (close in to the weapon's `far`, then stand; never back off or strafe) sets the path (re-planned at most every `TARGET_REPATH_MS`, only when the goal moved over 8 u or the path ran out), holds the trigger while the target is in range with a clear line, and aims at its present position and velocity. The client's `aim` is ignored while locked, and `facing` follows the target (the shield's block cone reads it). Accepted only for another combatant that is standing, not evading, hostile (not self, not mob to mob; only players lock), within `TARGET_RANGE` (640 u); anything else is ignored with no change. Line of sight is not needed to lock (a target behind a wall is chased around it), only to attack, but a target out of sight with no path to it is refused (a mob across the locked coliseum gates; the client also offers no mobs while `gatesLocked`). Clicking the fighter already locked changes nothing; a new lock clears the earlier walk. A `target` order costs at most one path search per `TARGET_REPATH_MS` per player (`lockSearchAt`; an order out of sight sooner than that is refused, and its search doubles as the chase's first plan), and the chase's own re-plan time (`lockPlanAt`) survives setting or dropping a lock, so spamming orders (up to `MAX_ACTIONS_PER_TICK` a tick) never buys extra searches. `endRun` drops every lock, so a winner's `attackTarget` is not synced through the linger. Dropped (trigger and path cleared, the fighter stops where it stands) when the target is no longer standing, is evading, is past `TARGET_RANGE`, or has been out of sight for `TARGET_LOS_GRACE_MS` (1.5 s; until then the chase paths around the wall) after having been seen once since the lock (`lockSeen`; a target locked behind a wall is chased until first seen, bounded by the leash), when it is out of sight and the chase finds no path to it, or the player holds no weapon. **Orders replace each other in arrival order within a tick** (`InputQueue.moveBefore`, like `aimBefore`): `move`/`stop`/`attack` drop the lock (an unreachable `move` still does), `target` lets go of the trigger. **A player's own order cancels the attack its lock is winding up** (`cancelLockedWindup`, `sim/weapons.ts`, LoL's move-cancels-windup): a `move`, a `stop` (applied in `step.ts`, an unreachable move included) or an accepted `target` on another fighter, while its `PendingAction.target !== undefined` and it has not landed. No swing, ball or arrow; the cooldown is refunded (`readyAt = startedAt`, it was over when the attack began); the root goes (`cancelAction`); the bow's `drawStartedAt` clears; a `cancel` fx tells every client to drop that fighter's telegraph, and the walk starts in the same tick. Re-clicking the locked fighter (or, once the lock has dropped, the fighter the attack is already aimed at), a refused `target` and a landed attack (the axe's root after its swing stays) cancel nothing, and neither does an `attack` input. `releaseTrigger` (teleport, going down, a dropped client, training revive) drops it too; a swap or a pickup does not. An attack the lock starts records its `target` on the `PendingAction` and is aimed again as it lands (a swing straight at the target's position at its rewind time) or leaves (`leadAim` from the drawn origin at the target's present position and velocity), if the target still stands and is hostile; then it flies straight and can be dodged. An unlocked attack (a mob's, the harness's) keeps the old aims: a swing and a ball go where they were aimed when the attack started, an arrow along the aim held when its draw ends (the bow's aim at release), and `decide` leads the bow for the lags alone, without its windup. A locked attack has no client `t`: `lagMs = TICK_MS`.
- **Windups root.** Every attack roots its fighter from its start until the swing lands or the arrow or ball leaves (`moveMultiplier`: 0 while `action !== null` or inside `rootUntil`, else 1; nothing else slows a walk: cards and Frost's chill are `world.moveModifiers`). A directional windup (a mob's, the harness's, an `attack` input) is committed: a `move` given during it is kept as the path and walked afterwards, only a stagger (not on the bow) or a swap cancels it. One the target lock started is the player's own click, so the player's next `move`/`stop`/`target` cancels it too (see Target lock): the game client walks away from a windup it no longer wants. The bow is a fixed windup (kind `"draw"`, `landsAt`, `drawStartedAt` synced for the pull), never a partial draw. `weaponTimings`: sword, bash and fists 0.1 s, axe 0.25 s / 25% of the interval and root 0.5 s / 50%, wand `min(WAND_WINDUP 0.4 s, 0.8 × interval)`, bow `min(BOW_WINDUP 0.6 s, 0.92 × interval)`. These are the anti-kiting lever (below, Weapons and factions); a windup mod (the Quickdraw card) shortens melee windups only, and attack speed never cuts a ranged windup below its base-speed value (G9, `mirrorTimings`), so no card shortens the lever (the Stat mods rule).
- **Players and mobs fight through `Combatant`** (`sim/combatant.ts`). Weapons, melee, projectiles and the ledger take either; only how they go down differs. A regional mob's faction is its region's scaled by `MOB_K` (elite `ELITE_K`), then by its TYPE's role (hp and damage: `mobTypeFaction`), and paced, read through `factionOf` (by `MobState.type`) like a player's, so signatures, armor and timings apply to it unchanged; a mob holds no cards, so it has no outgoing rows and no PvP factor.
- **A run starts on fists** (`shared/weapons.ts`, `sim/combatant.ts`, `sim/loot.ts`, `sim/mobLife.ts`, `sim/weapons.ts`; balance contract 2). `WeaponId` has six members: `WEAPON_IDS` is the five loop weapons (the only items: chests, the ground, a mob's hand, the rack, `WEAPON_BEATS`) and `ALL_WEAPON_IDS` adds `fists` (`FISTS`, `isLoopWeapon`). `addPlayer` leaves both slots EMPTY (`""`, training too) and an empty slot fights with fists: `slotWeapon` returns `FISTS` for `""`, so `activeWeapon` is never null for a player and the lock, the attack and the signatures work unarmed; `slotItem` and `handIsEmpty` say what is physically carried, and fists never travel as an item (`setSlot` stores `""`, `dropBody` and the rack skip them, a pickup of `"fists"` is refused). Fists' spec is a design rule: reach 40, boost 1.5, `dpsMult` 0.60, windup 0.1 s (`FISTS_WINDUP`), scaling AD 0.6 + AP 0.6, on-hit power 1.2; they pierce a shield, never stagger and meet no ball. A pickup goes into the HAND's slot when it is empty (the hand arms at once: `weaponChanged`, `SWAP_SECONDS`), else into the other slot when that is free (the hand stays), else it replaces the hand's weapon, which drops; a swap to an empty slot is allowed (it is fists) and a swap between two empty slots does nothing; Committed keeps the second slot unused (nothing is picked up into it unless it is the hand). **A mob that dies to a player whose hand is empty arms the killer** (`awardMobWeapon`): the weapon goes straight into the hand, with the toast "Armed: <Weapon>" and nothing on the ground; an armed killer, one who went down in the same pass, or no killer leaves today's drop (a loose weapon at the mob's feet, unless the killer already holds that type); a mob that carries fists drops nothing. Auto-arming is a best guess (contract Q4). `POLICIES.fists`, the harness, the benches and the Python model treat fists as a weapon of their own (`WEAPONS["fists"]` in `realtime.py`, parity rows), and the benches' starters are fists unless told otherwise; `SIGNATURE_WEAPONS` stays the faction's REFERENCE weapon (the harness, the benches, the Python oracle and the fixtures fight with it) and is no longer what a run starts with.
- **Mobs are players who send inputs.** `sim/mobAI.ts` is a system (after damage). It pushes `move`/`stop`/`aim`/`attack`/`release` into the mob's own `InputQueue`, and `step.ts` applies them next tick through the same handlers as a player's. Idle mobs send nothing, and mobs never lock a target. A mob walking home (`return`) is evading: `hurtMob` returns 0, `isEvading` keeps it out of hit tests, signature targets and contacts, it starts nothing new (a shot already in the air still lands), and it is whole when it arrives. It gives up when it leaves its leash or 6 s after it last *dealt* damage, so kiting from outside its leash gains nothing. **The leash is the camp's territory** (`leashOk` in shared `mobs.ts`, ROGUELIKE_PLAN D15): a mob of a camp whose territory holds its home spot (`camp.area` > 0) stays on that camp's `territoryGrid` cells and within `min(camp.radius, MOB_LEASH_CAP)` of its home, the cap being 12 tiles (generated camps measure a radius of 10.8 to 30 tiles, so the cap binds on almost all); any other mob (no camp, `area` 0, an all-zero `territoryGrid`, a spot outside the territory: hand-built maps and tests) keeps the `MOB_LEASH_RADIUS` 9-tile circle. A ranged mob backs off only to points whose four ±24 u axis neighbours are inside the leash too. The coliseum teleport puts every mob back on its camp spot at full hp. Mobs never hurt each other (a hit is recorded with scale 0) and their shots fly past each other. A mob fights with its TYPE's weapon (`MobState.weapon0`: a caster shoots a wand or a bow through the weapon's policy), which is also what its kill drops or arms, and its role's speed is a `world.moveModifiers` entry (`mobRoleMove`). A training world places no mobs of its own (the console's practice camps aside).
- **Signatures ride the ledger.** Faction bonus damage (burn, backlash, disease, mark) is recorded in the tick's ledger in the `beforeDamage` phase, so it lands with the tick's hits, and each signature deals its own damage type whatever the faction's weapon hits are (`SIGNATURE_SHARES`: burn and disease magic, backlash physical, the mark true; a mob's signatures are typed the same way). Heals go through `heal()` in `sim/factions.ts` (3 s out-of-combat lockout; every lifesteal is exempt, and `ignoreLockout` skips only the lockout: Blood Saint, Second Wind, the camp-clear heal; the `healTaken` rows, Wellspring and Vampiric Pact, scale every heal but lifesteal). Cards act in this module too, so none of what follows runs in a world built with `signatures: false` (`createGameWorld` skips the whole module): **AP on damage over time** (every running burn, disease or mark adds `AP_DOT_RATIO` × the owner's AP as magic damage per second, a second entry under the same `sig`, so the DoT cap, the fx chunks and lifesteal see one damage); the `dot` and `returned` products (Boom, Slow Burn) on each tick and on Fire's backlash; **lifesteal** from `lifesteal` rows (`lifestealTotal`: the rows capped at `LS_CAP`, times Bloodthirst's `lifestealMult`, plus Siphon's share of damage over time and Holy's own, the total never past `LIFESTEAL_MAX`, G5; the extra a Race to 0 adds feeds none); **`markRate`** (Gathering Storm: Sentenced's mark's engaged time grows (1 + rate) times as fast toward the same cap; it does nothing for anyone else), and against a player the same clock also runs at its owner's PvP factor; **`linger`** (Slow Burn: a damage-over-time signature stays engaged longer after its target leaves reach); **Festering Arcana** (when a damage-over-time signature stops, a magic `dot` status keeps ticking `FESTERING_TICKS` more times); **`slowResist`** (Iron Will: the chill's cuts, every slow status and the bog are weaker, `resistSlow`); **Blood Saint** is the `hurt` trigger above; **Second Wind** is the same trigger, and its heal over time runs in the module's `healOverTime` system; **Pestilence for a Plague fighter** is the execute band in the signature (`executeBandOf`; its `vs` row for the other factions is the ledger's and does not need the module). A held Second Wind or Pestilence-for-Plague does nothing in such a world. `sim/factions.ts` stretches chilled fighters' `readyAt`, bow draw (its start and the moment the arrow leaves, so the cycle stays interval / chill) and press buffer every tick; the other windups are not stretched, so a chilled swing still lands on time. Weapon timing changes must keep the chill working: `npm run harness:factions` checks it against `testing/realtime.py`. A landed stagger (the 0.5 s immunity starts) pauses the staggered fighter's burn, disease and mark for its weapon's windup (`staggerPause`; none for a bow, whose draw a stagger never cancels; the real sim only, `realtime.py` has no staggers).
- **The PvP factor and the guards** (`shared/rules.ts`, `shared/constants.ts`, `sim/ledger.ts`, `sim/mods.ts`; balance contract 3.4 and 4.6). Power decides fights through one curve: every player-to-player ledger entry (a weapon hit, a signature's damage; not a Wish, not the arena, never a mob or a dummy) is multiplied by `pvpFactor(P)` = `PVP_F0` + (`PVP_FMAX` − `PVP_F0`) × (1 − e^(−P / `PVP_P0`)), P the attacker's card power (`cardPower`: the sum of tier × count). A fresh fighter (P 0) deals less than the plain sheet and a fed one many times what a fresh one does: early fights are slow and farming wins fights. The curve is fit to the TTK targets (a median even duel of about 20 s at minute 0, about 5 s at minute 5 of the typical kit: `bench/ttkCurve.ts`), and the mark's clock against a player runs at the same factor, so the factor is a PvP-only `DUEL_PACE`: equal-power fights keep their outcome and only their pace changes. Guards G1-G13 bound what a stack of cards can do; each is a constant in `constants.ts` and a case in `server/test/guards.test.ts`: G1 the row product (`OUTGOING_ROW_CAP`), G2 the per-hit PvP cap (`PVP_HIT_CAP_SHARE` of the victim's max hp), G3 the PvP damage-over-time cap (`PVP_DOT_CAP_SHARE` a second), G4 A Race to 0 (`HP_RACE_CAP`; the extra damage it adds feeds no lifesteal), G5 lifesteal (`LS_CAP` on the rows before Bloodthirst's doubling, `LIFESTEAL_MAX` in all), G6 the barrier (`BARRIER_MAX_SHARE`, decaying: the maths is in `shared/mods.ts`, but nothing in the sim calls it yet, see the Known TODOs), G7 bonus armor and magic resist (`BONUS_DEFENSE_CAP`), G8 max hp (`MAX_HP_FLOOR_SHARE`, Tank Engine's `TANK_ENGINE_CAP`), G9 attack speed (`AS_MULT_CAP` on the rows, the `asPost` products after it; a ranged windup never under its base-speed value), G10 move speed (`MOVE_BONUS_CAP`; Fleet is off while shooting), G11 conversions (one pass, `CONVERT_AD_AP_CAP`), G12 crit (`CRIT_CHANCE_MAX`, `CRIT_MULT`, a deterministic accumulator), G13 Wishes (flat except Arcane Overload's cooldown ×2 and Wishful Thinking's trims, never under `WISH_COOLDOWN_MULT_MIN` / `WISHFUL_FLOOR_SHARE`). The harness seam: `DuelSpec.damageScale` fills `World.combat.duelScale` (a fighter's PvP outgoing-damage multiplier: the noise model and the flip bisection of the benches) and `DuelSpec.hpRace` fills `World.combat.hpRace`.
- **Weapon specials and the weapon layer** (`WeaponSpec.special`/`scaling`/`onHitPower` in `shared/weapons.ts`, `shared/interactions.ts`, `sim/onHit.ts`, `sim/projectiles.ts`, `sim/melee.ts`; balance contract 3.3 and 5.5). A hit's packet is the faction's own hit plus what the weapon scales the fighter's stats into (`packetFor` → `scalingDamage`: AD, bonus max hp and bonus armor and magic resist physical, AP magic, each × `asMult` × the interval; blended into one packet so the ledger is unchanged), all × the fighter's `hit` mult. The axe SHREDS: each hit takes `AXE_SHRED_ARMOR` off its target for `AXE_SHRED_MS`, summing up to `AXE_SHRED_CAP` (the `shred` status, a negative armor bonus through the card mod source; `defenseOf` stops total armor at 0, and a weapon never scales with a debuff). The wand SPLASHES: a ball's impact also hits every other enemy within `WAND_SPLASH_RADIUS` for `WAND_SPLASH_SHARE` of the ball (`splashFrom`: `derived` entries; a wall stops it, the primary is never hit twice, a mob's ball never splashes a mob). `SimConfig.weaponSpecials: false` (the faction-cycle parity harness) leaves every weapon with its damage alone, because `testing/realtime.py` models neither. The loop's other levers are constants too: a raised shield stops only `SHIELD_BLOCK_SHARE` of a frontal sword or bash hit (the rest lands and never staggers; the riposte is `RIPOSTE_SHARE` of the BLOCKED part) and reflects an arrow that carries `ARROW_REFLECT_SHARE` of its damage (the holder takes the rest as a hit); `STAGGER_IMMUNITY_SECONDS`; a wand ball that deletes an arrow keeps `BALL_AFTER_ARROW` of its damage. All provisional (Known TODOs).
- **No per-kill power: the feed and what a kill pays** (`shared/src/rules.ts`, `sim/feed.ts`, `sim/mobLife.ts`, `sim/arena.ts`). The per-kill buffs are gone (`shared/buffs.ts` and `buffView.ts`, `PlayerState.buffs`, `grantBuff`, the bounty's stack transfer, `MOB_BUFF_STACKS`, the `buff` fx of code 10: balance contract 3.6): a kill never gives a stat. A mob kill pays GV (`creditMobKill`: `GV_MOB`, an elite `GV_ELITE`, × m, within `GV_MOB_CAP` a run), +`FEED_MOB_KILL_WEIGHT` of a player kill's feed and the mob's weapon (the fists rule); a player kill pays the kill GV, the victim's bounty GV (never a stat) and +1 feed, and leaves the victim's body. m = the reward curve (`rewardCurve`: near 1 while hungry, falling around a feed of `REWARD_MID`, never under `REWARD_FLOOR`) × the catch-up (`CATCHUP_MULT` for a feed more than `CATCHUP_GAP` under the lobby median); the same curve is the feed brake on a quest's card tier. The feed is `kills + 0.25 × mob kills`; the **bounty reads player kills only**. All stat power is a card (the cards rule), from a camp's chest or a quest, so the pace of power is the pace of cleared camps. `barrier` stays for R6's skills and for Second Skin (whose barrier is not wired: Known TODOs). "Shield" in a card's text is the magic-resistance stat (D7), not the barrier.
- **Stat mods: the mirrors are the one source of truth** (`shared/src/mods.ts`, `effects.ts`, `sim/mods.ts`). `PlayerState.asMult`, `windupMult`, `speedMult` (uint16 THOUSANDTHS, 1000 = ×1) and `armorBonus`, `shieldBonus`, `adBonus`, `apBonus` (int16 TENTHS of a point) hold the stats that effects change; the identity is 1000, 1000, 1000, 0, 0, 0, 0 (`WIRE_IDENTITY_MIRRORS`). The quantized value is authoritative (D22): `recomputeMods` writes a mirror only when its quantized value changed (so it patches only then), and the sim reads back the DECODED value (`decodeMult`/`decodeFlat`), never the double it folded, so a client decodes exactly what the sim used. All seven have a reader: `attackSpeedMult`/`moveSpeedMult`, `timingsOf` (`mirrorTimings`: `windupMult` through `scaleWindup` for the windup and the axe's root; a mult below 1 shortens MELEE windups only, a ranged attack's timings come back untouched, and attack speed never cuts a ranged windup below its base-speed value, G9), `defenseOf` (the flat armor and magic resist bonuses; total armor stops at 0, so the axe's negative bonus never makes anyone take more than the bare hit) and `packetFor` (`adOf`/`apOf` through the weapon's `scaling`). Max hp, the damage sum, lifesteal, the mark rate, the crit chance and the `mult` products are read from the build's folded sums, not mirrored. A player's build is its cards' rows: each live card's rows once per COPY held (a base card stacks), with its faction's own rows where the card has some (`cardRows`), rebuilt only when `PlayerState.build` changes. The fold is shared `foldRows` (pure; `shared/test/mods.test.ts` pins it): 1. `stat`, `flat` and held `while` rows ADD within a key (the 16 `EFFECT_STATS`: max hp, attack speed, move speed, damage, windup, lifesteal, mark rate, heal taken, damage taken, cooldown, execute band, slow resist, lifesteal mult (a product), lifesteal on damage over time, crit chance, heal barrier; and the flat `armor`, `shield`, `ad`, `ap`); a multiplier key becomes `1 + sum`, different keys MULTIPLY where the sim combines them (attack speed with damage, `speedMult` with the chill); 2. max hp from the plain sums (never under `MAX_HP_FLOOR_SHARE` of the faction's, G8); 3. the `convert` rows (Juggernaut, Spellsword, Big Brain, Arcane Tempo, Momentum, Stone Skin) in ONE pass from the plain sums (a conversion never feeds another; AD and AP from conversions are held to `CONVERT_AD_AP_CAP`, G11); 4. the caps (bonus armor and magic resist `BONUS_DEFENSE_CAP` G7, move speed `MOVE_BONUS_CAP` G10), then AP times its `ap` mult; 5. the `mult` rows, one product per key, clamped (`hit` to `OUTGOING_ROW_CAP`, `asPost` to `AS_POST_MIN`..`AS_POST_MAX`, `wishCooldown` above `WISH_COOLDOWN_MULT_MIN`, `hpRace` to `HP_RACE_CAP`); `flag` rows set rules. `vs`, `scale`, `onHit` and `trigger` rows do not fold (the ledger, `sim/onHit.ts` and the hooks read them); a row whose `requires` ids are not all owned is off (a base id counts as owned at any tier). Every `world.modSources` entry is asked too (multipliers multiply in, flats add): the Wishes' `slowAttack`, the axe's shred (a negative armor bonus) and Adrenaline's boost. **`AS_MULT_CAP` (1.5, owner ruling) caps attack speed from the rows and the sources together**: `mirrorsFromFold` multiplies the rows' fold and every source's `asMult` (slows included), applies `capAsMult`, THEN the `asPost` product (Here Comes the Boom ×0.5, Machine Gun ×2: G9), then the result is encoded, so the final value, between 0.5 and 3.0, is the one on the wire and the one the sim, the local prediction and the bow art decode; a slow multiplies the uncapped product, so it only shows once the product is under the cap. The damage rows are capped only by G1; the kiting numbers behind the attack-speed cap are in `testing/TUNING_ROGUE.md` (the old sheet). Folding is event driven, never every fighter every tick: `grantCard` re-folds at once, so a card changes the attack and the walk in the same tick; a source whose answer changes must call `recomputeMods`; `updateMods` (the last `world.systems` entry; `gameplay.ts` installs it last) checks only players that own a `while` row and, with sources registered, every fighter's answer. A `while` mirror follows its condition one tick late, by design: `chasingLock` (on or off with the target lock), `notShooting` (the Fleet cards: it holds except while a bow or wand attack is in progress (`weapon.action`) and for `SWIFT_BOOTS_PAUSE_MS` after the shot leaves (`WeaponRuntime.lastShotAt`, set in `land`; a windup that is cancelled leaves no pause), so its `speedMult` mirror patches when the first attack of a bout of shooting starts and when the pause after the last shot ends, not per shot), `hpBelow` (Last Rites), `unarmed` (Fists of Fury: the hand's slot is empty) and `fightTime` (Slow Starter: a fight is a hit landed or taken within `FIGHT_GAP_SECONDS`). A mob has no synced mirror (what a source gives it stays server-side). `sim/mods.ts` is a leaf: it must not import `weapons.ts`, `arena.ts`, `ledger.ts`, `buffs.ts`, a status runtime or anything else that imports it (type-only imports are fine).
- **Clients decode the mirrors, never recompute them.** The client reads `PlayerState.build` only to DISPLAY it (the cards row, the build strip's power and PvP factor, tooltips, the chest panel's cards). The local prediction (`net/localPrediction.ts`) and `FighterArt` read only the decoded mirrors (`decodeMirrors`/`decodeMult`/`decodeFlat`; `scaleWindup` for the bow's draw and the axe's root, `fullDrawSeconds` takes WIRE values), never cards or rows, and `sync.test.ts` compares the mirrors bit for bit. - **Generated maps are certified, with no relaxed attempt** (`mapgen.ts`, `zones.ts`, `cover.ts`, `coliseum.ts`; `test/mapCertify.ts` re-checks every guarantee below from the `GameMap` alone). `generateMap(seed, attempt?, { styleZones?, decor? })` (`styleZones: null` builds the plain map with no stylers, the `styler=none` of the benches): with an attempt, exactly that one (throws when it fails); without, attempts 0, 1, ... until one certifies (a guard throws after 64). `tryAttempt(seed, attempt)` is the map or null; `certifiedMap(seed)` the first of `MAX_ATTEMPTS` 16 that certifies, or null (the pool discards that seed). Any failed check fails the attempt; nothing is ever relaxed. **Determinism:** one `mulberry32` stream per attempt (`seed` for attempt 0, else `seed + attempt * 0x9e3779b1`), drawn in a fixed order (changing the order moves every map: re-pin on purpose with `scripts/pinMaps.ts`); only `+ - * /`, `sqrt`, `floor`, `ceil`, `round`, `abs`, `min`, `max` (`detSin`/`detCos` in `cover.ts` are built from them), never `Math.pow`, `exp`, `log`, `atan2`, `hypot`, `sin`, `cos` or `random` (a test spies on 23 Math functions through a generation, lane tables included); integer value noise, integer 5/7 chamfer distance transforms and heap keys; total comparators (value, then tile index). Hot imports are copied to module constants in `zones.ts`, `cover.ts` and `mapgen.ts`: under vitest each use of an import is a property read (30% slower).
- **What an attempt certifies** (96x96; the numbers are in `zones.ts`, `mapgen.ts`, `coliseum.ts`).
  - *Connectivity and gates:* all walkable floor is one 4-connected piece with the gates open; with `withGates` closed the arena is sealed, in one piece, and the rest is one piece. Four straight 4-wide gates (n, e, s, w) are floor in `map.tiles` and the only links between the arena and the rest; a clear lane lies in front of each. The coliseum is the same fixed layout on every map (one of 8 pillar variants per seed).
  - *The 480 u sight cap:* with the gates open, no 32 u-wide strip of sight-open ground is longer than `MAX_SIGHT_CORRIDOR` (also inside the coliseum with the gates closed). It is certified on the fine lattice (48 directions, lines a quarter tile apart, every lane at most 14.98 tiles) **anchored at `LATTICE_ORIGIN`**, the coliseum centre less (32, 32): the pillar layouts, shifted by `CX - 32`, were certified where they sat at 64x64, and from a corner anchor some leave a 15.5-tile lane inside the footprint that no rock may cut. `longestLane` and the tests use the same anchor. The independent certifier also measures the exact strip at off-lattice angles (longest 464 u) and bounds 16 u discs at 576 u (every 10th seed); narrower gaps stay unguaranteed (Known TODOs).
  - *Zones:* five, each one 4-connected painted piece and each one's walkable floor one 4-connected piece too (the cover treats a rock that would split its zone's floor locally as a last resort, as bad as rocking the ground around a spawn, and the generator checks the result), painted areas within 8 tiles of each other (`AREA_TOLERANCE`; equal within 1 tile before the territory repair), walkable floor share max/min <= 1.15, at least 24 ring-road tiles each (`RING_CONTACT_MIN`: every zone touches the coliseum), at most 100 sliver tiles (`SLIVER_MAX`). They are grown "paint bucket" style from a ring-road arc joined to an outer seed by its spine, so there are no ridges or passes: zones meet directly, and the order of the five around the ring is shuffled by the seed. Every tile off the border wall is a zone or the coliseum in `regionGrid`.
  - *Camps and territories:* four camps per zone (`CAMPS_PER_ZONE`), one elite camp per zone (its camp farthest from the coliseum, the elite at spot 0), centres at least 9 tiles apart map-wide (`CAMP_GAP`: a camp's halo, its 2.5-tile clearing plus one tile, is protected ground, so two closer would form a lane no rock could cut). A **territory** is the part of its zone nearer to a camp than to the zone's other camps by path cost; the territories of a zone partition all its painted tiles (`territoryGrid` = 1 + camp index), and each one's floor is one 4-connected piece holding its clearing, spots and chest tile (the cover's territory guard treats a rock that would split one as a last resort; the repair after the cover joins a cut-off piece to the territory it touches (the one whose camp keeps the piece within 30 tiles when there is a choice), walls up a piece of at most `WALL_UP_MAX` 120 tiles that touches only other zones' floor when it holds no crate, pool member, camp ground or ruin and the rest of the floor stays one piece (the zone keeps its painted area), moves a piece of at most `STRAY_MAX` 40 tiles to the zone it touches otherwise (hazards of the old zone's kind on it are cleared; low ground on it fails the attempt), and fails the attempt for any other piece). The camp stands at its territory's **pole** (the deepest cell by 5/7 chamfer distance whose halo fits) and no territory floor tile is more than `TERRITORY_RADIUS_MAX` 30 tiles from it. That bound is a seating rule, not a filter (as a filter it would reject about 65% of layouts): the pole is searched only among cells within 30 tiles of every tile of the territory, the camp seeds are placed for coverage, and a zone set whose camps cannot be seated is redrawn inside the attempt.
  - *Spawn pool:* 24 to 32 members tagged by zone, in at least 4 zones, all within 8% of one BFS 5/7 walking distance to the coliseum centre, at least 5 tiles apart and from every camp centre (picked 7 away, so the cover can cut ground between a spawn and a halo), each with a plain 3x3 of floor outside ruins and no floor hazard in its 5x5, and screened so that no two members within 480 u can shoot each other (every aim ray within `RANGED_HIT_REACH` of the other's centre meets a wall first). **The pool is picked before the stylers**, on the open ground: up to 40 members within 4.5% of one contour value, their 3x3s protected from the stylers and hazards cleared from their 5x5s afterwards. Picked after the stylers, their walls and hazards left half the candidates and the pool did not survive the cover's shifts in walking distance (the cover moves a spawn's walk by 3% to 100%). After the cover and the territory repair the longest run of the pool within 8% is kept and trimmed to 32 from its extremes. The `findPath` walk ratio max/min over the pool is 1.17 on average and 1.201 at worst on the themed sweep of seeds 0-999 (1.202 on seed 5920 in the plain generator, one seed in a few thousand over the owner's report-only target of 1.2).
  - *Terrain (the zone stylers' layer, `validateTerrain(map, { styled: true })` T1-T11 gating every attempt):* every kind lies in its home zone; no floor hazard (ember, bog, ice, light) on a spawn's 5x5, the 3x3 of a crate, camp chest or ground-loot spot, or the camp clearing + 1; ICE has plain floor on all eight sides (T11). The cover does not avoid ice (keeping rocks off it, even as a last resort, split the Rime's territories and made one attempt in three fail): a rock clears the ICE around it, and the Rime then freezes over every clear tile it has left (`freezeClearGround`, about 40 tiles a map, 3% of its floor: the 480 u cap puts a rock in nearly every 3x3). A styler breach, a failed `validateTerrain` and a rezoned piece holding low ground fail the attempt (`A:styleBreach`, `A:terrain`, `A:rezoneLow`); `A:styleBreach` never happens on the 200 swept seeds (a test asserts it), the other two are rare normal retries.
- **The zone style hook** (`styler.ts`, `zoneStyle/hook.ts`; the zone-terrain contract). `ZONE_STYLE_HOOK` is `zoneStylerHook` while `MAP_STYLERS` (constants.ts, a compile-time switch: server, bots and clients must be built alike; `mapHash` is what tells them apart) is on, which it is since ZT-6, and null when it is off (the plain generator, for tests and what-if runs: `generateMap(seed, attempt, { styleZones: null })`). The hook is a plain adapter from the generator's `StyleState` onto `styleZones(ZoneStyleState)`: `territoryGrid` is passed as `territory`, `decor` becomes the option, `camps[].r` is in TILES (`CAMP_R`) on both sides, the generator's `protect` is the stylers' `hard`, and the `StyleResult[]` telemetry lands in `STYLER_STATS` (nothing in a map depends on it). - **Collision classes and masks.** `tiles` is the collision layer: `TILE_FLOOR` 0, `TILE_WALL` 1 (stops everything), `TILE_LOW` 2 (fence, lava, crevasse: stops bodies and melee swings, not sight or shots). Every query names its mask (`map.ts`): `MASK_SIGHT` (= wall; eyes, projectiles, target acceptance, `lockSeen`, abilities, ranged signature reach), `MASK_MOVE` (0xff; bodies, A*, placement, pickup reach) and `MASK_MELEE` (wall | low; swings), the last through `reachMask(weapon)` (`interactions.ts`: an arc weapon gets the melee mask, a projectile weapon the sight mask), so the lock's `clear` (the `los` `decide` gets), mob `los`, the arc-weapon signature reach and the bots' brain (`bots/brain/tactics/fight.ts` `inReach`: whether a melee bot counts as attacking) all read it, and flipping the melee mask is one constant. `raycast`/`hasLineOfSight` default to sight; `circleClear`/`segmentClear`/`findPath` default to move. `isWallTile` means OPAQUE, never use it for movement (`server/test/architecture.test.ts` scans for it). The masks are derived views (`sightMask`, `moveMask`, `moveComponents`, cached per map object, the gated copy has its own), never `GameMap` fields; maps are read-only, a test that turns wall into floor after a query calls `forgetMapCaches`. `moveComponents` makes an unreachable goal O(1) and the lock drops a target that is provably unreachable; string pulling looks ahead at most `PULL_WINDOW` candidates. On the generator's side `cover.ts`, the territory repair and the certifier read `MASK_SIGHT` for lanes, cones and the spawn screen and `MASK_MOVE` for connectivity, sealing, the ring road, the territory guard and walking distances; probes restore the byte they saved, so a probed LOW tile never comes back as floor.
- **Terrain hazards** (`terrain.ts`, `sim/terrainEffects.ts`, `shared/walk.ts`; GAME_DESIGN 5.2). `GameMap.terrain` holds a kind per tile (none, lava, fence, crevasse on LOW; ember, bog, ice, light on floor; a WALL tile may keep a LOW kind as a pillar; append-only ids), `props` is cosmetic and never read by the sim. A fighter is judged by the tile under its centre, the home faction of the zone is exempt (Fire/ember, Plague/bog, Frost/ice) or boosted (Holy/light x2), regional mobs are of their region's faction. Ember is a ledger entry like sudden death's burn (`arena: true`, true damage, `by` "terrain:ember", no fx, not combat, credit by `recentRival`); bog is a `world.moveModifiers` entry (the product of all modifiers is clamped to `MOVE_MULT_FLOOR` 0.35 when above 0); light is `heal(..., {quiet: true})` so the 3 s lockout applies; ice replaces `advanceAlongPath` with `walkFighter` only while a fighter stands on slipping ground or still carries slide state (otherwise today's code, bit for bit), and any root plants a sliding fighter. All of it returns on its first line when the map has no such terrain or `SimConfig.hazards` is false (`SimConfig.hazards` is server-only and not synced: keep it out of live room configs or the prediction is wrong for good). `MoveModifier` takes an optional `dtMs`; burn skips a locked arena (sudden death owns that pass; the coliseum has no terrain); `TerrainRuntime.combatAt` is light's own combat clock so the 3 s lockout holds without the faction layer; `WorldHooks.arenaHurt` reports the hp an `arena` entry really removed (`HazardStats.damage`). A path-search budget note: the slide adds its own stuck re-plans (at most 4 a second per stuck fighter, none on a valid map) and a `regrip` search on leaving ice, on top of the lock's one search per `TARGET_REPATH_MS`.

- **Rooms take their map from the pool** (`server/src/mapPool.ts`, ROGUELIKE_PLAN D20). `RaidRoom.buildWorld` (a room's first join) calls `maps.take()` synchronously (a training room calls `takeForTraining()`: a `structuredClone` of one of the last 4 maps handed out, or `take()` when none has been yet, so a burst of practice rooms never drains the pool) and hands the map to `createGameWorld({ seed: map.seed, map })`, so `onAuth` and `onJoin` stay synchronous and a room nobody joins takes none; `createWorld` without a `map` calls `generateMap(state.seed)` in process (tests, benches, the harness). One `worker_thread` per process (`mapWorker.ts`, started through `mapWorkerBoot.mjs`, with its own 44 MB of lane tables) certifies crypto-random seeds with `certifiedMap(seed, { decor: false })`, only while the queue is short (`MAP_POOL_TARGET` 8, refilled at `MAP_POOL_REFILL_AT` 6: an idle server costs nothing), and posts each whole map by structured clone (about 52 KB, 0.2 ms) with its hash; the main thread checks seed, attempt and `mapHashU32` again and drops a mismatch, so the pool serves only a certified (seed, attempt) a client can rebuild. An empty queue (a burst, a worker that is down or warming) falls back to ONE synchronous `certifiedMap` on the main thread, logged (about 35 ms, 75-120 at p99; a discarded seed moves on to the next, up to `MAP_FALLBACK_SEEDS` 8). A worker that crashes or errors restarts with a backoff of 1 s doubling to 60 s and never takes a room down. So does one that HANGS (stuck in a loop, never replies, never exits): a watchdog (`MAP_WORKER_HANG_MS` 5 s, option `hangMs`; a map takes about 35 ms and a cold worker's first about 250 ms) terminates a worker that sends no message for that long while requests are in flight, without waiting for it (`discard`), and takes the crash path (the same backoff exponent, counted in `stats.crashes` and `stats.hangs`, logged as `[maps] map worker hung: no map in 5000 ms with N on the way; restarting in ...`) while the fallback covers the gap. It is one unreffed timer per worker that exists only while requests are in flight, restarted by every message the worker sends (so the clock runs from the oldest unanswered request or the last reply, whichever is later, and a slow worker that keeps answering never trips it; posting more requests does not move it), and cleared when the worker dies, is replaced or the pool stops; an idle pool or `MAP_POOL=0` holds no timer, and the terminated worker's late messages are ignored (`onReply` checks it is still the current worker). `warmMapgen()` runs at boot on both threads and shutdown terminates the worker (waiting at most `stopTimeoutMs` 2 s; the profile store is closed first); `MAP_REJECT_LIMIT` (3) consecutive hash rejections count as a crash (the backoff); `MAP_POOL=0` (tests: `MapPool({ target: 0 })`) runs no worker.

- **`world.rewardRandom` is a private stream** for reward and roster rolls (camp rosters, plan deals and chest options draw from it today; "?" kinds will). `createWorld` seeds it (sfc32, a 128-bit state) from `rewardSalt`, four words `RaidRoom` takes from `crypto.randomBytes` (never the public seed, which every client holds; a 32-bit state could be brute-forced once rolls are revealed). A number is also accepted and expanded, for tests. It is separate from `world.random` so that drawing rewards never shifts the loot rolls or the sequence tests script (`createWorld` takes exactly one draw from `random`, for `orderSalt`, and none for this), and separate from `orderSalt` because that salt also keys `tieRank`, whose outcomes a client can stage and read (Known TODOs): a stream derived from it could be brute-forced. Without a `rewardSalt` (tests, the harness) it is derived from `orderSalt` by a tagged hash, so it matches neither `actionOrder` nor `tieRank`: fine for a test world, never for a live room. Anything hidden ("?" flags, chest rolls, plan deals) comes from here and stays server-side or in private messages; a roster is rolled from here too but is published in `state.camps`. Quests are the exception: their roster, ids and placement use `world.questRandom`, which `createWorld` seeds from its own `questSalt` (four more `crypto.randomBytes` words from `RaidRoom`, independent of `rewardSalt`; only a world built without one, in tests, the harness and questBench, derives it from the reward salt through the mixing `questSaltFrom`), so enabling or disabling quests never advances `world.rewardRandom` or `world.random`.
- **Clients render in the past, except the local fighter.** Remote entities render in the past via `SnapshotBuffer` (`shared/interp.ts`) at a time `RenderClock` gives (`EntityInterpolator.renderTime`; `new EntityInterpolator({ adaptive: false })` is the old fixed `INTERP_DELAY_MS` 100 behind a `ServerClock` that snaps back on a late patch, kept for A/B and as a fallback). `RenderClock` judges every patch by `r = (server time of the previous patch) − (its arrival)`, the most render time may be ahead of local time for the segment to be complete, and holds render time at the 98th percentile of the last 4 s (the second lowest `r` of about 80 patches) less 10 ms: the delay (`PATCH_MS` + how far behind the median `r`) follows the network between `MIN_INTERP_DELAY_MS` 70 and `MAX_INTERP_DELAY_MS` 150, grows at up to 20% of real time (render slows to 80%) and falls at 1% (up to 5% when far above what is asked). **Render time never runs backwards**: it goes down against the local clock only by slewing at no less than 80% speed, or by standing still (0%) while it recovers from a stall or a step, and it jumps only FORWARD (when the wanted value is `snapMs` 250 ms or more ahead); `renderTime` also returns the larger of its value and the last one, so a rounding error cannot show. Two things are not jitter and are told from it, because the 4 s window would take half of itself to turn over: a *stall* (an arrival gap of `stallMs` 1 s or more: the link froze, a reconnect, a tab that slept) empties the window and leaves the burst that ends it out (its patches are each judged against one far older; 2.2 s of it used to fill half the window and snap render time back by up to 2.6 s), and the delay asked before stands until `rewarmMin` 5 new patches show the median; a *step* (the newest `stepPatches` 3 patches, at distinct arrivals over at least `stepSpanMs` 80, all more than `stepMs` 100 from the median on one side: the server dropped sim time after a hitch, because `RaidRoom.tick` runs at most `MAX_CATCHUP_STEPS` 3 steps and drops the rest so its clock trails ours for good, or the link's latency changed for good) empties the window, seeds it with those patches, resyncs the `ServerClock` (`resync`) and, for a step down, lets render time stand still until it is where the stream is. Render time is then ahead of the newest data (the picture draws the newest patch as it comes, at 20 Hz) for about the dropped time plus 50-100 ms (a hitch of 450 ms drops 300: 350 ms measured) and smooth again after; that is the least a clock that cannot go back can do, where the legacy clock stepped back once by the dropped time. The `ServerClock` under it (`now()`, the order stamps, the HUD's deadlines) has no late snap there (`lateSnapMs = Infinity`): lateness is the delay's job, and the clock can wobble without moving the picture. Everything drawn at render time (fx, deaths, hit-stop juice) follows the delay; `INTERP_DELAY_MS` is the delay a client starts with and what the harness and the bots assume a player sees. In a dev build `window.__raid.interp` gives `{ delay, target }`. Measured with `bench/netProbe.ts`/`netTraceEval.ts` (see Known TODOs, Netcode). The LOCAL fighter is predicted (`net/localMotion.ts`, presentation only: nothing it computes is ever sent, the server stays authoritative): an order walks a model at once along the same shared A* path, `drawn = sim + visual`, and each patch pulls the model back in line (`S(T)` against the model `delay` earlier, `delay` measured from `moveSeq`; small errors go into a decaying `visual` offset, never faster than the `CORRECT_SPEED_*` caps). It cannot drift or hang: an order the server never applies (lost while reconnecting) is forgotten after `ORDER_EXPIRE_MS` and does not hold the comparison off; samples off by more than `DIVERGE_ERR` for `DIVERGE_PATCHES` in a row with nothing in flight and the server not heading where the model walks (a revive, a halt, a walk it did not order) drop the model's walk; an error past `SNAP_DISTANCE` (even with an order pending, beyond what that order's walk explains) or an offset past `VISUAL_SNAP_DISTANCE` snaps to the server and plans the destination again from there; the teleport and the local death fx `cut()` it (the next sample snaps, however short the jump); the scene resets it while `room.reconnection.isReconnecting`; a click is clamped to the world as `parsePoint` clamps it. Dead, in the lobby, after the run ends, and until a teleport is drawn it is drawn from the snapshots, except that while its death or the teleport's cut is still ahead of what is drawn it stays where it was last drawn (`localDrawSource`). After a `target` it follows the server's chase (the newest sample carried one tick by its velocity): the lock is the server's. The model enters follow when a patch shows the lock (`PlayerState.attackTarget`; not while a move or stop of ours is still in flight, which would end it) or at once on a click it expects to be accepted (`expectLock`, for the responsiveness), and leaves when the lock empties; a lock that never shows within `ORDER_EXPIRE_MS` was refused, and the walk it interrupted is planned again. The scene feeds it the speed mirror and the chill (a Frost mob chills like a Frost player), and the axe's root (`net/localPrediction.ts`), and what the abilities do to it: a stun or root and a cast's own root from the `status`/`cast`/`castEnd` fx (the synced `effects` bits stand in only when no fx told of one, as after a join), Last Stand's haste added on top as the server adds it, and a blink. A Blaze Step is predicted: `castSlot` runs the shared `blinkLanding` on the predicted position, tells the prediction (`noteCast`, which calls `LocalMotion.blink`: the model and the drawn fighter jump at once, the walk and lock in hand stop being predicted, the order carries its jump so the comparison does not read the lead as a divergence) and sends the `cast` with a `seq`; the patch that acks it also shows a changed `displaceSeq`, which `LocalMotion` reads as its OWN blink (the error to the server's landing is absorbed like any other, or snaps past `SNAP_DISTANCE`: a refused blink is echoed in `moveSeq` with no `displaceSeq` change, so the whole jump is the error and the prediction is pulled back to the server), while a changed `displaceSeq` with no blink of ours behind it is a forced displacement (the model, its history and the drawn fighter all shift by the jump, with no snap test, and a walk in hand is planned again from there). `EntityInterpolator` treats a changed `displaceSeq` as a cut for that fighter, so a remote blink is not slid into. Slow magnitudes are not predicted (only a bit is synced); anything unmodelled is the server's correction. Remote fighters and fx stay in render time, so what is drawn at the local fighter (hits on it, its telegraph) plays at its predicted position: accepted.
- **Rendering is separate from logic.** Phaser scenes only read interpolated state and send inputs. Projection lives in `client/src/render/projection.ts` (screenY = worldY · Y_SCALE, height drawn upward, depth = worldY). `MapView` draws the 96x96 map as Graphics chunks (floor 24 x 8 tiles, walls one row by 24), hidden each `PRE_RENDER` when the camera does not show them (`mapCull.ts`; a Graphics replays its whole buffer whether or not it is seen): any new static map layer goes inside a chunk. Zone borders come from `zoneEdgeStrips(regionGrid, walls)` (`zoneEdges.ts`): per side of a border, in that zone's colour and dash (`ZONE_DASH`, one rhythm per zone: the cue that is not hue), on see-through tiles only, built once per map. Client tests derive map spans from `WORLD_W`/`WORLD_H` or `mapSpan()`, never a literal size, and painter goldens use hand-built maps.
- **Whatever keeps its shape is a baked texture, so the y-sorted world stays in one draw batch** (`render/bake.ts`, `phaserFixes.ts`). Phaser draws Graphics and Shapes (Arc, Ellipse, Rectangle) through its flat-triangle batch and Images and Text through its textured-quad batch, and flushes (a draw call, a vertex upload, a shader switch) every time the draw order moves from one to the other. A scene of fighters made of Graphics with a Text name each flushed about twice per fighter: 142 draws a frame in an 8-player arena with 36 mobs, the GPU process at 99% of a core and 30-57 fps on a 144 Hz display; the same arena now draws about 35 and holds 144 (count `drawElements` on `window.__raid.scene.game.renderer.gl` per frame in a dev build). So a fighter's shadow, body disc, weapon, block arc, hp bar, nose, elite marks, a loot container, the target and loot rings, and the Wish icon are Images of textures made once per look with `ensureBake`/`bakedImage`/`swapBake` (`bakeScale()` texels per world pixel: `BAKE_SCALE` 2, sharp up to a camera zoom of 2, or 2.5 (at most `MAX_BAKE_SCALE` 3) on a screen that zooms past 2, a 16-inch MacBook Pro fullscreen or a 4K display (zoom 2.4-2.48); it is picked once from the window and its screen's fullscreen size and fixed for the page session, because the views size their Images from it and a texture keeps the density it was baked at (a window dragged to a denser screen later keeps it); on a 4K display the fighter, ring and chest textures go from 1.3 to 2.1 MB for the same looks; every object that looks the same shares one texture; `weaponTexture`, `BodyDisc`, `BodyFlash`, `HpBar` are the pattern, `scaledPart` lets `FigureJuice` pose a baked part, `pixelImage` is a 1x1 white texture for flat rectangles). A look that is chosen from a few steps (the bow's pull in 16, the wand's charge in 8, the block arc's facing in 96, the barrier in 8) is baked per step, never per frame. The map is `MapView`'s own (R2): a styled map's ground and walls are baked by `zoneArt/bake.ts` and its chunks culled to the camera, so the playtest branch's separate map bake (`mapBake.ts`, which baked the legacy MapView's wall rows into atlas pages) was dropped when R2 landed; a new static map layer goes into `MapView`'s chunks or its zone-art bake. What animates every frame (signature and status layers, fields, fx, projectiles, trails) stays a Graphics, and each live one costs about two flushes, so do not add a per-fighter Graphics that redraws every frame for something static. `phaserFixes.ts` coerces the `lighting` flag the Shape objects pass as `undefined` and Graphics as `false` (the flat-triangle handler flushed on every change of it). A Phaser upgrade is re-checked by the draw count.
- **The map screens read and send `plan` only** (`ui/planning.ts`, `mapPainter.ts`, `route.ts`, `mapHold.ts`). One `PlanningView` in four modes, chosen each frame by `planningModeFor(phase, { training, held })`: `preview` (lobby), `planning`, `map` (a run, while Tab is held) and `hidden` (everything else; hidden takes no pointer and no key). It lives in `uiRoot` under the chrome, is not an Esc panel, is laid out in CSS (so it survives a resize or fullscreen) and its markers are real buttons placed by percent over the painted canvas. The deal reaches it through `PlanInbox`, registered in `enterRoom` before the first state (the newest message wins, so a deal that beats the scene is kept). **The route is client-only**: waypoints in `sessionStorage` under `raidbr.route.v1:<roomId>` (a reload finds them), planned with the shared `findPath`, never sent, and restarted from the player's real position on the first active frame (the client cannot know which spawn the server chose on a timeout). Planning freezes the client's world: `moveTo`, `sendTarget`, `handleKey`, `streamAim` and `castSlot` return early. Hold Tab (`MapHold`) cancels the browser's Tab only during a run (`mapHoldAllowed`: not in practice, and not while the results panel is up, which a death shows while the run goes on, so the keyboard can reach "Back to hub"), lets go on blur or a hidden page and defers to text fields and the open settings or help card. A zone is never told by hue alone (`ZONE_SHAPE`, `ZONE_PATTERN`; a matchup chip carries a sign as well as a tone), and the minimap's output is pinned by a golden-hash test. Planning keys: 1 and 2 pick the spawns, arrows rove the markers, Enter on a camp toggles a route stop, Delete removes one; Ready is its own button. Settings `showRoute` and `textScale` (these screens' text only).
- **The cards row and the build strip are display only** (`ui/cardsRow.ts`, `ui/buildHud.ts`, `ui/buildModel.ts`, `ui/rectTips.ts`). The cards row is one chip per held card in catalog order (its title, and "x2" for a base card held twice), repainted only when the build changes; its tooltip is the card's text, the sum over a base card's tiers once two or more are held, and the glossary line of each tag. The build strip is nine cells: AD, AP, armor, magic resist, attack speed, move speed, leech, card power P and the PvP factor it earns ("PvP x1.39"), from the decoded mirrors and the synced build through the shared helpers the sim uses (`decodeMirrors`, `cardPower`, `pvpFactor`, `lifestealTotal`); a stat nothing adds to is drawn dim, not hidden. Both are click-through (`pointer-events: none`, pinned by tests): a right-click move or a left-click lock under them reaches the canvas and the aim keeps streaming, so a tooltip is found by hit-testing a passive window `pointermove` against the elements' rectangles (`RectTips`, which the weapon bar uses for its slots too), never by `:hover`; do not give a chip or a cell pointer events. There are no kill bursts, combo or buff UI (`ToastTone` is buff (the local player's own gains, amber: the name predates the cards), error or ok).
- **The picture is a viewport, not the window.** `view/viewport.ts` fixes the world height every player sees (`REF_VIEW_H` projected px; wider windows show more width, capped at 21:9 with bars; below 4:3 the width stays at the 4:3 minimum), the canvas is rendered at the device density (capped at 2x and a 4K pixel budget, `settings.hiDpi` off = 1x) and the camera zoom is canvas px per projected world px. Never assume camera zoom 1 or 1 canvas px = 1 CSS px: read a pointer's world point through the camera (`screenToWorld`, `pointer.updateWorldPoint`), set a scroll with `scrollForTopLeft` or `centerOn`, and scale a Phaser camera shake by `1 / zoom²` (`ScreenJuice`). The DOM overlay `#ui` is laid out at `--ui-w` x `--ui-h` and CSS-scaled, so overlay code uses those variables and `data-ui-short`/`data-ui-narrow` instead of `vw`/`vh`/size media queries, and `getBoundingClientRect()` for anything that meets the pointer.
- **The hub is client-only.** `HubScene` has no room and no server. Its layout and walking rules are pure (`hub/hubLayout.ts`), and a station only calls back into the menu (`onStation`). What it shows comes from the REST profile.
- **Training is a mode, not a fork.** `client.create(TRAINING_ROOM_NAME)` makes a private one-seat room of the same `RaidRoom` class. The server registers two handlers (`defineRoom(RaidRoom, { mode: "arena" })` under `ROOM_NAME`, `{ mode: "training" }` under `TRAINING_ROOM_NAME`). Colyseus merges handler options over the client's, so a client cannot choose the mode. A training world has `SimConfig.training` + `dummies`: a plain dummy, a shield dummy and a rack of every weapon next to the spawn, started the moment its player joins. It has no planning phase (the Room forces `planSeconds` 0), map mobs, camp rosters, patience timer, teleport, sudden death, result, GV or stats. Three locks keep it from recording anything: `trainingSettle` replaces `suddenDeath`/`settle` (anyone down is revived), `RaidRoom.settle` and `bankVoided` return early in training (a down *between* ticks still emits `resolved`, which the Room ignores), and leaving is `abandonPlayer`, never MIA. A dropped or leaving client frees the profile at once (`onDrop` holds no seat in training). A player with no accepted input for `trainingIdleSeconds` (300) is let go: only an input `enqueueClientMessage` accepts refreshes `lastInputAt`. Every training branch sits behind `world.config.training` / `RaidRoom.training`; an arena world never reaches it.
- **GV banks at resolve.** The sim only accumulates run GV and reports it in `resolved` results. The Room banks it (`recordRun`, or `bankGv` for a run voided by shutdown). Run gear never persists.
- **Bots in the sim** (server-filled players, `docs/BOTS.md`). A bot is a normal `PlayerState` + `PlayerRuntime` added with `addPlayer(..., { bot: true })`: `PlayerState.bot` (synced, fixed at join) and `PlayerRuntime.bot`. It has no client, profile or seat, its `profile` is only a display name that may equal a real callsign, and it plays everything else like a human. Six rules, the first four owner defaults (BOTS.md "Owner questions"): (a) killing one pays no kill GV and no bounty GV, and does not climb the killer's bounty ladder (`updateBounty` reads `kills - botKills`, so a bounty cannot be built on bots and cashed by an alt), but the kill still counts in the run (`PlayerState.kills` and the HUD; `creditKill`, tallied in `botKills`); (b) a `RunResult.kills` leaves bot kills out, while `PlayerState.kills` and the HUD count them; (c) the win bonus reads human starters (`arena.startedHumans`) and human opponents (`damagedHumans`), `contested` and `RunResult.players` still count bots, and `RunResult.winBonusWhy` (`"starters"`: fewer than `WIN_BONUS_MIN_PLAYERS` human starters, checked first and also given to a run that was not contested; `"opponents"`: damage on fewer than `WIN_BONUS_MIN_OPPONENTS` other humans; present only with `winBonusWithheld`, and an older server's payload has none) tells the results screen which rule held the bonus back, because a player cannot see from the state which fighters count for it (`ui/results.ts`); (d) a bot's `resolved` event carries `bot: true` and `RaidRoom.settle` drops it (`// bots never touch persistence`): no `recordRun`, `bankGv`, registry or `pendingResults`, whatever its name, even if it was removed before the event is handled. `RunResult.killedByBot` lets the results screen tag a bot killer; the client draws every bot name through `render/displayName.ts` (`[BOT] name`), the chest panel's "Taken by" included (`playerLabel`). (e) None of the hosting guards sees a bot: the room caps count rooms and `maxClients` counts clients (a lobby of one human and bots is open to the next human; at `MAX_PLAYERS` the newest bot yields its seat in `onJoin`, `BotManager.yieldSeat`), the new-callsign and passcode checks sit on the profile route and the static `onAuth`, and the order budget is spent in the Room's message handler for real clients only (`BotManager` calls `enqueueClientMessage` directly). (f) Bots never start or close a lobby early: `updateLobby` (`sim/step.ts`) counts humans only, both for the cut to `fillSeconds` at `minPlayers` and for the immediate start at `MAX_PLAYERS`, so a lobby holding bots stays open until its countdown ends or eight HUMANS are in; `lobbyClosing` tops the bots up at the last moment, and neither the arrivals nor that top-up happen with no human in the room (bots alone never make a lobby). Tests: `test/botSeat.test.ts` (sim), `test/botRoom.test.ts` (a real Room with a spy on the store), `test/botLobby.test.ts` (the lobby rules), `test/botGuards.test.ts` (a real server with every guard on).
- **Bots plan through a brain module.** The lobby closes, then `planning` (when `planSeconds` > 0): `BotManager` seats the last bots in `lobbyClosing` first, so they are dealt spawns like humans (`startPlanning` counts them; `PlanningRuntime.startedHumans` keeps the win-bonus gate to humans, leavers included). The room routes a bot's private `plan` message to `bots.onPrivate`, and both drivers run a bot's frames in `planning` as well as `active` (its status is still the lobby's there; `Brain.tick` has a planning branch with no goal and no tactics, so only the modules act). `brain/modules/planning.ts` is the player at the planning screen: it reads for a tier's time after the deal arrives (Hard 2-4 s, Normal 3-6 s, Easy 4-8 s, scaled down for a window shorter than `PLAN_SECONDS` and never over 55% of it), picks one of the free dealt spawns by `planSpawnScore` (the matchup of the two camps nearest each, `mobHunt.ts`) through the tier's softmax and lapse, then presses ready a last look later and in any case before the last `min(2.5 s, 30% of the window)`; a pick the next message does not show is sent again after 400 ms plus a reaction, 3 times at most; with no deal it presses ready about a second in and the server places it, as it does a human who runs out the clock. It sends only `plan`. `boons` is empty until R7, so it picks none today; when some are dealt it takes the stats file's favourite, else any.
- **Persistence is `ProfileStore`.** Mutations are synchronous in memory (nothing awaits between a check and its change), writes are coalesced and atomic (temp file, fsync, rename, directory fsync) with retry and backoff, and `flush()` runs on shutdown. A v1 file migrates on load. A file that cannot be parsed stops the server and is moved aside, never overwritten.
- **The shop is server-only.** Purchases and equips are `POST /api/profile/:name/purchase|equip` with the profile token. The client sends only a cosmetic id (and a slot). `ProfileStore.purchase/equip` check the token, the catalog price, ownership and the balance in one synchronous step, so a double spend is impossible. Spending lowers `gv` only, never `gvLifetime` or `gvByMonth` (the leaderboard ranks GV earned). Achievements are evaluated inside `recordRun`. An id lands in `achievements` once, and its reward cosmetics are granted in the same step. Win achievements and the per-faction/weapon counters count only `winQualified` wins (the win-bonus gate: `!winBonusWithheld`), so a walkover against a throwaway callsign earns nothing. Kill and bounty achievements are not gated (their rewards are unpriced; an owner decision). The leaderboard ranking is cached per month (dropped when GV is credited in it), and `you` on `GET /api/leaderboard` is sent only with that profile's own token. Equipped cosmetics reach other players as one synced `PlayerState.look` word, fixed at join.

- **Hosting guards sit at the network edge, never in the sim.** `RaidRoom`'s message handler spends one token of the sender's bucket (`ORDER_BURST`/`ORDER_PER_SECOND`, default 30 and 15, measured against the browser's 9.6 `move`/s while the right button is held and the bots' peak of 6/s) for every message but `aim`, before `enqueueClientMessage`; an order over budget is dropped silently and is not presence (mobs never pass through it). `RaidRoom.onCreate` counts the room in `ctx.rooms` (refusing with "server is full" past `MAX_*_ROOMS`) and `onDispose` gives it back: core also runs `onDispose` for a creation that threw, so only a counted room (`counted`) gives a place back. The static `RaidRoom.onAuth` checks `PLAYTEST_PASSCODE` first (`JoinOptions.passcode`; REST: `x-playtest-passcode`, needed only to claim a free name), through the one `PasscodeGate` in `ctx` (`guards/passcode.ts`, shared with the profile route): a wrong passcode is counted against its client address and the whole server (`PASSCODE_FAILS_PER_MINUTE` 6, `PASSCODE_FAILS_PER_MINUTE_ALL` 40), an address past its limit is refused with 429 (`code: "passcode_throttled"`; `ServerError(429)` on matchmaking) BEFORE its guess is compared, so a right guess past the limit tells it nothing, and a request that carries no passcode counts for nothing. **The client address** (`guards/clientIp.ts`) is the socket's peer, or `CF-Connecting-IP` only when that peer is loopback (cloudflared connects locally; the edge overwrites the header); `X-Forwarded-For` is never read. Colyseus's own `AuthContext.ip` comes from `X-Real-IP`/`X-Forwarded-For` (anything the caller wrote) and its `req` has no socket, so `createServer` makes the http server itself and `stampClientAddress` writes the computed address into an `x-raid-client-address` header on every request (replacing what the caller sent) for the static `onAuth` to read (`addressOfContext`). Colyseus puts `Access-Control-Allow-Origin: *` and `Allow-Credentials` on EVERY response from `matchMaker.controller.DEFAULT_CORS_HEADERS`, merged under `getCorsHeaders`, so `sameOriginOnly` (`configureCors` in `index.ts`) removes them from the defaults, not only from `getCorsHeaders`. **Sign-in links.** Tokens live in `localStorage`, which is per origin, and a quick tunnel has a new origin at every start, so a returning player would find their callsign "taken": `npm run playtest:signin` prints `<address>/?profile=<name>#key=<token>` (plus `&code=` with a passcode) from the profile file (read only) and the address `playtest.mjs` records while the tunnel is up (`<profile file>.link.json`, mode 0600, removed on exit, ignored when its launcher pid is dead). The token is in the FRAGMENT so no server or tunnel sees it; `net/signin.ts` (run when `net/profile.ts` loads) takes it for a valid `?profile=` name (`PROFILE_RE`, key 1-128 visible ASCII), hands it to `profile.ts` and `replaceState`s the fragment away (it does not store a token itself). The link is the callsign's password. Its key is only a claim until the server accepts it: `net/profile.ts` keeps it in its own slot (`raid.link.<name>`), sends it on the next profile request only where the browser holds no token the server accepted (or the server has refused the stored one, which is then kept and marked), drops it on a 403 and makes it the token on a 200. So a wrong key (a truncated copy, a crafted link) never blocks the right link, and no link ever overwrites an accepted token. **The launcher's death takes the server and the tunnel with it:** `playtest.mjs` gives the server a stdin pipe it never writes and sets `RAID_EXIT_ON_STDIN_END`; the entry block of `index.ts` then calls `gracefullyShutdown` when the pipe ends (SIGKILL of the launcher included); without the flag a closed stdin does nothing. cloudflared runs under `scripts/tunnelGuard.mjs` with the same kind of pipe (and a check that the launcher's pid lives): when the launcher goes, the guard SIGTERMs it, SIGKILLs after `TUNNEL_GUARD_GRACE_MS` (5 s), and exits, so the old public address never keeps pointing at a port that a later `npm run dev` would expose with no guards. The guard writes `<profile file>.tunnel.json` (`scripts/tunnelPids.mjs`: owner, guard and cloudflared pids), and the next launch stops what that file names if the pid still looks like a cloudflared or a guard (a SIGKILL of the guard as well is the only way to leave one up). When cloudflared ends after the link was shared, the link file is marked `down` (`playtest:signin` then says the tunnel stopped; it is not removed, so the message can say why) and the check of the public link stops. A tunnel that never came up (cloudflared exits, or `PLAYTEST_TUNNEL_TIMEOUT_MS`, default 40 s and set only by the launcher's tests, runs out before an address was handed out) shared no link: the launcher says no public link was opened, with cloudflared's last output, writes no link file, reports nothing as stopped and serves locally. **The network simulator is never live on a public link:** the launcher drops `COLYSEUS_LATENCY`, `NET_JITTER_MS`, `NET_LOSS_PCT`, `NET_SEED` and `ALLOW_NETSIM_WHEN_HOSTING` from the server's environment with a note, and `createServer` itself refuses `installNetSim` while hosting (`sameOriginOnly`) unless `ALLOW_NETSIM_WHEN_HOSTING=1`, with one `console.warn`; it logs one line whenever it does install. **A named tunnel** (opt-in; `--named <tunnel> --hostname <host>` or `PLAYTEST_TUNNEL`/`PLAYTEST_HOSTNAME`, `--quick` overrides) runs `cloudflared tunnel --no-autoupdate run --url http://127.0.0.1:<port> <tunnel>` under the same guard and shares `https://<hostname>/`, which goes into the link file (`named: true`); missing credentials show as cloudflared's own error (scrubbed of anything secret-looking) under a plain note of what to run. The hostname is the launcher's alone: the server reads no hostname (same-origin mode sends no `Access-Control-Allow-Origin`). The client dir is served last in the Express app, so it cannot shadow `/api`; `/matchmake` and the websocket upgrade never reach Express.

## Conventions
- Colyseus 0.18 APIs (not 0.15/0.16 tutorials): `defineServer/defineRoom` from `@colyseus/core`, `Room<{ state: X }>`, `this.onMessage` (or `messages = { ... }`), `setTimestep` (`setSimulationInterval` is its deprecated name), `client.sessionId` (no `client.id`), a static `onAuth` for matchmake-time checks, client `@colyseus/sdk`. `RaidRoom` sets `patchRate = 0` after `setTimestep` and sends each patch itself after the step. The scene reads `room.state` every frame; there are no per-field callbacks.
- Schema uses the decorator-free `schema({ ... }, "Name")` + `t.*` builders. Give primitives `.default(...)`. No decorators anywhere.
- Only the server imports `@raid/shared/schema` at runtime; the client uses `import type` from it (it decodes by reflection).
- Tunables live in `shared/src/constants.ts`, the catalogs (`weapons.ts`, `factions.ts`, the card rows in `cards.ts`, the mob types in `mobs.ts`, `cosmetics.ts`) and the signature numbers in `signatures.ts`. Mob scale, leash, the PvP factor and the guards G1-G13, the card hooks' numbers, the weapon levers and specials, the lifesteal cap, the camp-clear heal, the chest's tier odds, the reward curve and the planning numbers are in `constants.ts`. A doc names a number that is still being tuned by its constant, not by its value. Loot weights are in `server/src/sim/lootTables.ts`. Units: world units (1 tile = `TILE` = 32), seconds for durations unless a name ends in `Ms`, server time in ms.
- Weapon numbers live in `shared/src/weapons.ts`: the v3 faction × weapon formula (a weapon is a % boost on the faction's rate; `attackInterval`, `damagePerHit`, `backlashPerSwing`; the weapon's `scaling` adds the fighter's AD, AP, bonus max hp and bonus defense through `scalingDamage`, and `fists` is in the table). It must keep matching `testing/realtime.py`: `python3 testing/weapon_reference.py` prints the reference tables, pasted into `shared/test/weapons.test.ts` and `server/src/harness/parity.ts` when the design numbers change. `DUEL_PACE` (2) multiplies every faction damage number (not hp; Sentenced's mark ramp by k²) through `pacedFaction`. Sim combat reads the paced sheet through `factionOf` (`sim/combatant.ts`), never the raw one. Signatures are typed (`SIGNATURE_SHARES`): `realtime.py` mitigates each signature part by its own type (`PowerSpec.sig_types`) and, like the sim (`applyLedger`, then the lifesteal system), applies a tick's damage BEFORE its heal, so a fighter the tick's damage takes to 0 is dead whatever it healed that tick.
- Design rules the sheet left open are recorded in `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` 9.4. Change them there and in the tests together.
- No `Math.random` in shared code. No enums or decorators (keeps tsx/Vite/TS7 happy).
- Keep comments sparse; explain *why*, not *what*.

- Lag compensation (the melee rewind and the shooter's drawn position) trusts the client's `t` anywhere inside `[now − MAX_REWIND_MS, last published tick]`. A cheating client can pick the most favourable `t` in that 300 ms window (a projectile can leave from up to 300 ms back: its origin is taken when the attack starts, and the shooter is rooted until it leaves). Only the low-level `attack`/`release` carry a `t`; the game client's locked attacks do not, so the window matters for the harness's policy clients and cheaters only. Mitigation: bound `t` per client by measured RTT, or require it to be monotonic. `MAX_REWIND_MS = 300` covers a rewind of render delay + RTT + one tick: with the adaptive delay (about 85 ms on a good link, 150 at most) that is an RTT up to ~165 ms at the usual delay and ~100 ms at the cap. The hang watchdog is a main-thread timer: a main-thread stall past `MAP_WORKER_HANG_MS` (none seen: a tick is 50 ms) would fire it before the replies already waiting on the port are read, costing one needless worker restart with the fallback covering it.

Identity and persistence
- Few rate limits. Matchmaking verifies the profile token before creating a room or reserving a seat (the static `RaidRoom.onAuth`), and refuses a profile that is already active or opened a still-unjoined room in the last 5 s. A target order out of sight costs at most one path search per `TARGET_REPATH_MS`. In-match orders have a per-player token bucket (15/s, burst 30, everywhere), so a script sending `stop` then `target` every tick is cut from about 40 fx a second to about 15, but move spam still costs an A* search per accepted order (up to 15/s). While hosting (`sameOriginOnly`) there are also: caps on live rooms (4 arena, 8 training), on new profiles (30 an hour in all, 10 per address; behind a tunnel the address is `CF-Connecting-IP`, honoured only when the socket's peer is loopback, which is where cloudflared connects from; a caller that reaches the port directly is counted by its socket address, so it can neither dodge the cap nor aim it at someone else), a shop and equip bucket per profile (token-verified requests only), and no `Access-Control-Allow-Origin` for any origin outside `CORS_ORIGINS`. In dev none of those but the order budget is on: `/api` answers every origin, profile creation is free, rooms are unlimited. All the defaults are best guesses for 8 friends. A running room broadcasts a patch at 20 Hz until its player leaves or idles out, which is what the room caps bound. The passcode is optional and a shared secret in a link, not an account; wrong guesses are throttled per address and over the whole server (see Hosting guards), so a word-sized passcode is no longer open to a dictionary run through the public link, but the throttle also means a guesser can lock every friend out for the minute (a best guess; `PASSCODE_FAILS_PER_MINUTE`, `_ALL`, 0 = off).
- Profiles are weakly authenticated: a random token in `localStorage` (per origin: a new tunnel address needs a sign-in link), stored in plain text in the profile file, no passwords, recovery or expiry. TLS only where a tunnel or proxy adds it (the quick tunnel does). Clearing site data forfeits the profile unless the host prints a sign-in link for it; whoever holds a sign-in link is that callsign. A quick tunnel's address changes every start and a browser keeps tokens per address, so every returning playtest player needs a sign-in link from the host (`npm run playtest:signin`, README "Hosting a playtest"); a link is that callsign's password, and the whole fix is a best guess. A named tunnel (README "A stable link", opt-in) has one address, so browsers keep their tokens and links are only for a new device or cleared site data. A link's key is not trusted until the server accepts it (`net/profile.ts` keeps it apart in `raid.link.<name>`, drops it on a 403, and never lets it touch a token the server has accepted, so a wrong key cannot block the right link). With the passcode off (the default) anyone with the link can claim the 30 new callsigns an hour.
- GV banks when a player's run resolves. A graceful shutdown (SIGTERM/SIGINT, uncaught exception) pays the GV earned so far (`bankVoided`). A hard kill (SIGKILL, power loss, OOM) mid-run loses it. A purchase is answered once it is in memory, before the file write lands: a hard kill in that window undoes the purchase together with its GV debit, never one without the other. Writes are batched (at most one per second; `close()` and `flush()` write at once), so the window is up to about 1 s. Under `npm run dev`, Ctrl+C reaches the server twice (terminal and tsx), and the second SIGINT exits mid-shutdown, so the last second of changes can be lost in dev.
- The profile file has a single-writer lock (`<file>.lock` holding the PID; a dead PID is taken over, and an exit hook frees it). In a container, a hard-killed server restarted with the same PID (PID 1) sees its own PID as live and refuses to start; delete the lock by hand there.
- GV can be farmed with free callsigns: the win bonus needs 3+ human starters and damage on 2+ other human players (`WIN_BONUS_MIN_*`; fill bots count for neither, rule (c) of "Bots in the sim"), but two throwaway callsigns that get killed still pay kills and the bonus. Bounties are uncapped but come from player kills only (an owner decision), so a partner can only cash a bounty that real kills built. Mob GV and chest-skip GV share the `GV_MOB_CAP` cap per run.
- GV earned after a player's result went out is dropped (`awardGv`). A projectile outlives its owner and its hit still lands; a kill by a shooter who left or whose result is out is credited by name only (`killedBy`), with no GV and no bounty.
- Mob kills do not feed achievements (owner question). "Bounty claimed" counts runs that paid bounty GV (`progress.bountyRuns`), not individual claims. There is no end-of-month trophy job: the leaderboard keeps each month and nothing is awarded when one closes.
- A player who joins without picking a faction (old clients, bots without `--faction`) gets the round-robin default by join order (`defaultFaction`). A failed join or a lobby leave still uses up a slot.

Quests
- The objective/travel seconds and parity band in `docs/quests.md` are explicit assumptions, not measured balance. `questBench` is a report-only real-sim harness (`npm run questbench -w @raid/server`, with `--quick`, `--seeds N` and `--players N`; default 3 seeds x 5 seats per policy, one per faction, about 27 s). Its live-power metric is the cards' power (`cardPower` over the live cards; a chest in the farm baseline is worth the expected tier of its pick); completion rates use attempts, not offered lobbies (a kill-a-player quest is marked † and skipped in solo seats; a completion that gained under 0.5 power is marked "no Δ" and has no ratio). Its ratio is the quest's own seconds (from the player's first choice of the quest to the turn-in) divided by the farm-only seconds needed for the same live-power gain, so BELOW 1 means the quest is faster than farming and above 1 that it costs more (target band 1.0-1.35); the "marginal" column measures the farm time from the power the player already had. The farm time is the Kaplan-Meier median of all farm-only runs; a run that dies, makes no kill for 75 s or hits the cap is right-censored at that moment, never carried flat, and the farm time is unknown (farm s shows `>`, the ratio `<`) when under 30% of the runs are still in observation. The baseline prices a camp from `bench/campDuel` costs and retreats only when losing. These are opportunity-cost measurements, not a paired counterfactual or approval to change counts, caps or carrier rules; the first R2 run has effectively 1-3 completions per quest, so use `--seeds 6` or more before any balance decision. Quest rewards were replaced by the balance pass (a base card whose tier is the time-parity rule, one lower for a fed player) and the questBench numbers in `docs/quests.md` predate them (their ratios were in buff power): re-measure before quoting one. - Sizing camp fights for camp-wide aggro (D24) is logged for later. The hunt treats a camp as one mob: `mobHunt.ts` rates one mob's matchup and hp and knows nothing of the mates a first hit wakes, so a bot that wakes one mob pulls its three mates. - Fleeing walks a straight line away, not a route. `mobHunt.ts` `retreatPoint` takes the best of 16 headings at 4 or 7 tiles whose straight segment is clear (`PathService.clear`, move mask), kept for at most 1.2 s (`tactics/kite.ts` `createRetreat`), and both the `flee` goal (`tactics.ts`) and sudden death's distance-keeping (`tactics/fight.ts`) use it. A bot can therefore flee into a wall or a dead end or into another camp, and cannot flee round a corner (72% of the deaths in the 40-game replay below were while fleeing). A path-based retreat (away from the threats along `findPath`, weighing camps and territories) is logged for later.
