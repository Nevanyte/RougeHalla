elite camps, solo fighter. Two scripted fighters, neither a player. The Brawler is an upper bound (no abilities, no kiting, swings from 80 u whatever the weapon, no heals), so its death rates are biased high. The duelist is nearer to a player: it walks to its camp, uses its weapon range (melee closes to reach, ranged weapons stand and shoot at about 80% of range) and casts its Wish when ready, but it never retreats and does not read dodges, so its rates remain biased upward. The ci95 is the Wilson interval of deaths over engaged fights: its spread is over faction x weapon x seed within the seeds run, with one camp placement per elite kind (the map is fixed; the world seed varies the fight and the banked draw, not where camps are). The headline rate (died / engaged) counts truncated fights as non-deaths, so the decided rate (died / (died + cleared)) is printed beside it. A fighting mob gives up after 20 s without hitting a player and regenerates to full in 2 s (the legacy give-up rule), so a fighter that kites resets the camp: the duelist stands and shoots at 0.8 of its range and never steps back, and each cell's resets counts the camps' give-ups (legacy behaviour, not a sim defect). Fights that never touched the camp are no_engage and fights cut off by the tick cap are truncated: both are counted, not hidden, and a truncated fight is a non-death, so kiting weapons can understate deaths. Banked builds hold no cards, so the bots' misvaluing of reworked cards (until P-AUG-BOTS-1) does not enter; the bot_sample column does carry it, and only from matches that reached minute 5 (survivorship). Until P-ELITE-TUNE-1 the table is a baseline.
